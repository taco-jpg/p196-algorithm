Semantic findings
-----------------
1. Exact enumeration: allBits starts with exactly the empty Boolean list at
   m = 0. The successor case prepends false and true to the smaller complete
   enumeration. mem_allBits_iff proves that its members are exactly the lists
   of length m. The two branches have different heads, and allBits_nodup
   proves that no list occurs twice. allBits_length proves length 2^m.

2. Injectivity: choice maps the two Boolean constructors to different
   coefficients 5 and 14. inputWord_injective first recovers equal input-half
   lengths from equal doubled word lengths, then takes the equal left halves,
   removes their common first coefficient and fixed trailing 18 reservoir,
   and applies injectivity of the mapped Boolean choices. Its premise fixes
   k, but it does not assume equal Boolean-list lengths or the desired result.

3. Actual cardinality: familyWords is the image of the complete enumeration.
   familyWords_length and familyWords_nodup jointly establish exactly 2^m
   distinct coefficient words. This is stronger than counting entries in a
   possibly duplicated list. mem_familyWords_iff also specifies the family
   extensionally, so the count is attached to the advertised construction.

4. Canonicality: every word is nonempty, symmetric, bounded by 18, and has
   first coefficient 14. Nat coefficients provide the lower bound zero.
   These are exactly the fields of Agent8.CanonicalPair. Width is explicitly
   2*(m+k+1), so every input has even width. The independently compiled theorem
   no_unpaired_center proves that no index i can satisfy 2*i+1 = input width;
   any even-center condition for an unpaired middle coefficient is therefore
   vacuous on this input family.

5. Parameter scope: these enumeration and canonicality claims correctly
   include m = 0 and k = 0. The common-image target formula is a separate
   theorem requiring k > 0; no transition claim is assumed or proved here.
