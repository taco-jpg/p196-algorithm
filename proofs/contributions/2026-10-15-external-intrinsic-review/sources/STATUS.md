# Intrinsic seam safety and arbitrary-width annihilation

Two new Lean modules replace the search over block partitions by an exact
local criterion, then prove that even arbitrarily many active defects can
disappear in a single update. They import the unchanged decimal,
incoming-carry, and raw-symmetry modules. They give no persistent safe
invariant and no proof of decimal Lychrel existence or of the 196 conjecture.

## Exact partition-free criterion

`IntrinsicSeam.lean` treats any finite coefficient word in 0 through 18.
No symmetry, canonicality, or parity assumption enters its characterization.
In least-significant-first order, the following are equivalent:

1. Some consecutive-block partition has a nonzero actual-minus-isolated
   outgoing-carry defect at some column of the word.
2. Some coefficient 9 receives the actual incoming carry 1.
3. Some adjacent pair consists of a coefficient at least 10 followed by 9.

The partition into singleton blocks simultaneously maximizes every defect
coefficient. Thus choosing more complicated blocks cannot create a defect
that the singleton partition misses. Absence of the adjacent pattern is
equivalent to every coefficient of every partition's defect being zero.

The short proof is exact decimal arithmetic. A coefficient other than 9
has the same outgoing carry for both possible incoming carries. At 9, the
outgoing carry equals the incoming carry. A singleton block starts from
zero, so its outgoing carry is `q_i/10`, the smallest isolated outgoing
carry possible at that position. An actual carry 1 at a 9 must have been
generated by an earlier coefficient at least 10; following the intervening
9-run backwards yields the adjacent pattern.

`numeric_highNine_safe` applies this intrinsic criterion to canonical
mirrored digit sums and proves that the next ordinary reverse-and-add
output is nonpalindromic. `numeric_highNine_input_nonpalindrome` also
excludes a palindrome at the input itself: a mirrored digit pair with sum
9 cannot contain two equal digits. These statements concern one ordinary
step; they contain no temporal closure hypothesis or conclusion.

## Arbitrarily large seam mass can disappear immediately

`SeamAnnihilation.lean` defines the width `k+2` word

    Q_k = (10, 9^k, 10).

For every natural k, its singleton defect is 1 at precisely the k interior
columns and 0 at the two endpoints, so the total defect mass is exactly k.
For k>0, the exact next pair word is

    G(Q_k) = (1, 1, 0^(k-1), 1, 1).

The calculation retains the terminal carry. The actual digits of the first
output are `0^(k+1),1,1` in low-to-high order, and the first numeric output
is exactly `11 * 10^(k+1)`. Every next pair coefficient is at most 2, even
in the degenerate k=0 case. Consequently every partition of the next word
has every seam defect zero.

For even k=2r, Q_k satisfies all canonical pair-word conditions. The existing
constructive realization theorem gives a positive finite decimal seed for
every r>0. More strongly, the new source proves that **every** canonical
ordinary realization of Q_k is nonpalindromic at times 0 and 1 and is
palindromic at time 2. This is an all-parameter theorem, not a finite census.

The explicit seed formula proposed in the accompanying paper argument,
`n_r = 10^(2r+2) - 10^(r+1) + 1`, is not asserted as a proved identity by
these modules. The formal nonvacuity theorem uses the existing constructive
canonical digit realization instead. Its first numeric output is proved
exactly; the second output is proved palindromic, without needing a closed
formula for its value. For r=1 the paper formula is the familiar finite
example `9901 -> 11000 -> 11011`.

This family rules out persistence from nonzero seam defect alone, and from
any fixed lower bound on its initial mass. It does not rule out additional
structural hypotheses, alternative safety certificates, or a recurrent
mechanism on a more restrictive family.

## Reproduction and verification

Run from the project root:

    python main_existence_attempt/continuation_pass/lean/check.py /workspace/scratch/fe6abe1df721/lean-runtime/lean

The checker deletes all local build products, copies the exact sources,
rebuilds every dependency using Lean 4.19.0, and prints the transitive axioms
of every new theorem and definition. Only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and
`Quot.sound` are allowed. It rejects placeholders, custom axioms, and
`native_decide` in all copied sources and compares their hashes before and
after the build. `compile.log` retains complete compiler output and
`verification.json` records each audited declaration and its dependencies.

The completed fresh build passed all six compilation stages and audited
31 new theorems plus 5 definitions. The decimal dependency rebuilt in
57.22 seconds; the two new modules rebuilt in 0.67 and 1.08 seconds. Only
the three standard axioms listed above occur transitively. All five source
hashes remained unchanged throughout the build.

## Retained development history

`development_checks/` contains every development source snapshot and its
complete compiler result. The first intrinsic draft used `by_contra`,
which is unavailable in this Std-only environment; explicit decidable cases
replaced it without changing the statement.

The first annihilation draft mistakenly stated a finite-support defect
formula for all natural column indices. The chosen coefficient function
continues with 9 outside the finite word, so its carry defect is also 1
there. Lean rejected that statement. The corrected all-index formula is
0 at the two designated endpoints and 1 elsewhere; the mass and every
finite-word claim explicitly restrict their indices to the actual word.
The next failed draft merely requested an extra tactic split after all
conditionals had already been split. Neither failure was hidden, and no
unproved assumption was introduced to discharge it.
