Agent112 author kernel checks, not independent review. `ProjectionControls.lean` defines the exact proposition `Relaxation`: a closed path of the existing three-column prefix relation, valid bounded positive prefixes, binary incoming controls, a closed6/12 phase stream with its exact update, an independently assigned exterior-charge residue stream satisfying each modulo-nine equation, positive even total overflow O, the budget2O<=P<=4O and the implication2O=P ->8 dividesP.

`no_relaxation_period_twelve` formally refutes the universal implication from this explicitly weaker proposition toP>=12. The two finite witnesses are checked by kernel reduction, not by trusting the Python discovery routine.

These are deliberately **not** `Agent109.Cert.Certificate` objects, ordinary integer trajectories, or coherent all-column carry histories. The auxiliary charge has not been identified with the charge of any complete exterior word, and the incoming carry has not been derived from one persistent interior. Consequently the controls neither refute a stronger bound for full certificates nor assert Lychrel existence. They show precisely that additional full-word compatibility is required by this approach. The negative conclusion is about `Relaxation`, not about arbitrary reverse-and-add orbits.

The actual-strip theorem supplies one possible additional compatibility restriction, but it still requires a common fixed cut and truly alternating terminal overflows. Phase alternation alone does not supply that schedule; the separate canonical1330 control records the distinction.
