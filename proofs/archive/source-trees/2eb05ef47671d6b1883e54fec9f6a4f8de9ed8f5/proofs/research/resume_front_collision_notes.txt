Symmetric collisions of the renewing 8 front

Status. Exact conditional local dynamics; no infinite decimal orbit or
palindrome-avoiding finite seed is obtained. The prescribed schedule N,O
must still be supplied by a finite exterior. No novelty claim is made.

Coordinate convention
---------------------
For an L-digit integer with least-significant-first digits d_i, put
q_i=d_i+d_(L-1-i). Thus q is a symmetric word over {0,...,18}.
N denotes a reverse-and-add step with no additional leading digit;
O denotes a step with an additional leading digit. A power such as
6^m denotes a run of m symbols, not numerical exponentiation.

A known symmetric q window is updated using an arbitrary incoming carry
c in {0,1}. For N, retain the full output window. For O, discard its outer
output pair, since the omitted exterior is required to know that pair.
Thus three O steps shorten the controlled window by 3 symbols, although
the entire integer would have grown by 3 digits. A distinct arbitrary
boundary carry is allowed at each step. This overapproximates every
possible exterior, so a universally valid local identity is sound.

Exact six-step flight
---------------------
Starting immediately before N with 8,6^m,8 and m>=5, the controlled windows
have the following forms under N,O,N,O,N,O:

  0: 8,6^m,8
  1: a,12^m,a                         a=16 or17
  2: b,6^(m-1),b                     b=a-6=10 or11
  3: p,13,12^(m-3),13,p               p in0,...,3
  4: p+4,7,6^(m-4),7,p+4
  5: r,14,12^(m-4),14,r               r in8,...,15
  6: s,8,6^(m-5),8,s                 s in S

where S={4,5,6,7,8,9,10,11,13}.

Write a=16+alpha. Then p=2*alpha+gamma, r=2*p+8+epsilon,
where gamma,epsilon are the relevant boundary-carry bits. At the last
overflow, put k=floor((r+zeta)/10). Direct column addition gives

    s=4+k+((r+1) mod10).

For r=8 this is 13; for r=9 it is 4 or 5; for r=10,...,15 it is r-4.
These formulas establish the phase table without inspecting arbitrary
word lengths. They agree with the independently formalized one-sided
front law: each front advances 4 columns in 6 steps, while the whole word
grows by 3, so the central uniform gap loses 2*4-3=5 symbols.

Discarding the newly known outer pair s,s leaves the original front
class with gap m-5. Hence for m=5k+r the flight reaches a collision
residue r in {0,1,2,3,4} after k macro-steps, provided the prescribed
overflow clock continues. The flight itself does not create an infinite
reservoir; the unresolved issue is the collision and its wake.

Minimal collision table
-----------------------
For m=2,3,4,5 the exact controlled six-step outputs are:

  m2: (x),         x in{2,4,6,8,10,12,14,16};
  m3: (x,x),       x in{6,7,8,9,10,11,12,13,15};
  m4: (s,10,s),    s in S;
  m5: (s,8,8,s),   s in S.

The m=4 formula follows from the flight table through phase 3, after which
the states are (p+4,7,7,p+4), then (r,14,14,r), then (s,10,s).
For m=3 the corresponding last states are (r,16,r), then (s+2,s+2).
For m=2 they are (u,u) with u in {10,...,17}, then the center 2(u-9).

For m=0 or 1 the minimal window disappears before or at the sixth step,
meaning exterior wake digits are needed to determine the new center.
Retaining one known wake symbol on each side, starting with
s,8,6^m,8,s for s in S, gives broad possible outcomes. At m=0 every even
central q value from 0 through 18 occurs in the local overapproximation.
At m=1 every equal central pair (x,x), x in {0,...,18}, occurs.

This variability is not solely an artifact of independent abstract
carry choices. Genuine decimal seeds with q prefix
(8,12,9,x,y,s,8), followed by its reflection, maintain the actual six-step
N/O clock for every x,y in {0,...,18} and s in S. Their center after 6 steps
attains every even q value from 0 through 18. Examples:

  seed 13004008790997 -> central q0 after 6 steps;
  seed 13000008500997 -> central q10 after 6 steps;
  seed 13000008470997 -> central q18 after 6 steps.

All ordinary integer orbits for these examples are recorded in the JSON
results. Thus the renewing terminal-8 class plus its one-symbol wake does
not prescribe a universal deterministic collision/restart outcome.
An all-time construction would need a richer correlated wake invariant
or another mechanism generating a new, unboundedly renewable reservoir.

The m=4 collision guarantees a central q value 10 after six steps, but
this is not an immediately regenerated terminal-8 packet. Under the
next N step its central digit is (10+kappa) mod 10 = kappa, where kappa
is the incoming carry, so its central q value becomes 2*kappa in {0,2}.
Both possibilities occur in actual decimal orbits with the seven-step
clock N,O,N,O,N,O,N:

  seed 130000000668400997 -> central q10 at step 6, q0 at step 7;
  seed 130000000668500997 -> central q10 at step 6, q2 at step 7.

These examples are also checked by ordinary integer arithmetic and
recorded in the JSON results. No eventual-regeneration impossibility
follows: a later restart involving a richer wake remains unproved in
either direction. In particular, the q10 collision packet alone does
not yet supply an all-time palindrome-avoidance certificate.

Palindrome avoidance is a separate requirement. The known core at
phases 0 and 4 can consist entirely of symbols below 10, so the front law
alone does not imply nonpalindromicity at every intermediate step.

Reproduce:
    python3 resume_front_collision.py

The script checks all abstract boundary choices through state-set
branching, validates the general formula for 196 distinct gap lengths,
and verifies the collision examples by ordinary decimal arithmetic.
The general phase formulas above, not the finite gap count, justify
the arbitrary-gap identity.
