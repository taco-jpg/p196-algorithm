DECIMAL LYCHREL RESEARCH: CONTINUED WORK, 13 OCTOBER 2026

Status: no proof of decimal Lychrel existence, and no proof that 196 is
Lychrel. No positive invariant or nonterminating decimal seed was found.
This record separates proved auxiliary results from restricted searches.

1. COMPLETE NUMERIC THEOREMS, CHECKED IN LEAN

Every complete first decimal carry word has a same-width positive integer
representative reaching a palindrome within four reverse-and-add operations.
This includes both overflow and no-overflow carry words. Four is optimal
over entire carry classes: the five-digit trace (0,0,0,1,1,1) permits only
five first values, all of whose first three positive-time iterates are
nonpalindromic; 99500 reaches 2102012 at time four.

Eleven consecutive complete carry words determine the initial mirrored
digit-sum word, at every finite length. Equal histories preserve the first
differing coefficient under overflow and double it without overflow. Equal
endpoint carries bound that coefficient difference by nine. A checked
outer-digit potential forces at least four doublings in ten operations.
This does not determine the initial digit split: 196 and 295 share q.

The standalone source has 186 theorem declarations, uses only Std under
Lean 4.19.0, and compiles with exit code zero. The checked numeric bridge
includes canonical digits, leading zeros in reversed strings, changed width,
both center parities, and all carries including overflow. The proof contains
no sorry, custom axiom, or native_decide. Standard Lean logical axioms are
reported individually in resume_integrated_build.log.

CarryWordsResearch.pdf gives the full mathematical arguments, sharpness,
the eleven-word corollary, and reproducibility details. A direct protected-run
proof replaces graph enumeration in its main text; Lean independently proves
the normalization bound via closed 37-state and 159-state certificates.
Neither theorem is presented as a solution to decimal Lychrel existence.
Publication priority has not been established.

The direct proof also extends to every even base at least 10 and every odd
base at least 17, with the same sharp bound for strictly positive iteration
times. Odd-base normalized representatives can already be palindromes at
time zero; this does not contradict the positive-time sharpness statement.
This general-base extension is proved mathematically in Appendix B, but is
not formalized in Lean. See resume_general_base_normalization_proof.txt.

2. EXACT BOUNDARY SEARCHES WITH SPECIFIED MODELS

The previously unfinished period-18 uniform-core construction is now closed:
both core phases generate exactly 185,531 states and edges, with no valid
exterior. Independent C++ and Python implementations agree. This excludes
that particular finite-interface construction at every interface width.

A new exact four-phase bulk was found:
  (3,10,15,8) --N--> (7,1,11,17)
               --O--> (15,8,3,10)
               --N--> (11,17,7,1) --O--> start.
Here N means no overflow and O means overflow. Compatible actual digits
and carry streams were constructed. A period-16 return grows by eight digits.

For this bulk A, exact reverse interior closure has 12,544 states and no
valid center head. This excludes every finite center returning after 16
steps in the specified fixed-annulus model. Both parities and all bulk
rotations are covered. It does not exclude all larger temporal periods or
growing, nonperiodic center regions.

A separate fixed-core return map enumerated 5,734,124 states with four
mirrored core pairs, both center parities, and all bulk rotations. There
are 144 internal return edges; every such edge exits the state space on its
next return, so there is no cycle of any return period within that bounded
core space. All 144 edges were independently checked in Python.

Uniform digit-3/6 center interfaces were excluded in both stationary and
four-column-moving period-16 models. The relevant closures each have 256
states and edges. These are different geometries; a stationary calculation
alone would not have excluded a moving interface.

The exterior search for bulk A remains incomplete after multi-million-state
caps in both directions. No intersection was found. Other two bulk classes
have complete no-exterior closures with 16,384 and 3,744 states. The incomplete
A search is not an impossibility proof; its fixed-period center obstruction
already prevents that particular complete construction.

See resume_boundary18_notes.txt, resume_boundary4_notes.txt, and their exact
graph implementations/results for the equations and restrictions.

4. CONDITIONAL WEIGHTED INVARIANTS

Searched integer scores summed along the entire folded digit-sum word.
The desired language is S>0, with safety against all palindrome predecessors
and closure S(input)>0 implies S(next)>0. Conditional closure avoids requiring
a globally increasing score. The exact all-length closure checker models a
connected Euler trail through the transducer product with integer edge flows;
no word-length cutoff is used for an UNSAT answer from that checker.

No synthesized score passed even the finite candidate stage. A 120-weight
three-category-memory template containing the sampled 196 orbit reported
UNSAT after four CEGIS rounds, within that template. Arbitrary-seed and
positive-periodic-bulk variants timed out. A 600-weight exact-previous-symbol
template rejected nine candidates for the 196 seed and thirteen for a
positive bulk cycle, then returned UNKNOWN at its per-query time limit.
Finite closure checks used 75,447 words; none is an infinite-closure proof.
The general checker was therefore never used to certify a candidate invariant.

An earlier floating-point LP experiment reported infeasibility but no exact
Farkas certificate was obtained; it is not a proved obstruction. Those
scratch files are not needed for the formal results.

5. CARRY RECONSTRUCTION AND MOVING DEFECTS

The exact polynomial difference identity is
  Delta_next = 2 Delta       without overflow,
  Delta_next = (1+x) Delta   with overflow.
Known carry forcing terms allow a current q word to be reconstructed modulo
32 after five non-overflow steps. An independent implementation passed 7,862
integer comparisons, including 989 twelve-step windows along the 196 orbit.
This supplies an exact temporal coding, not a nontermination proof.

Additional symbolic bulk and interface work is recorded in
resume_defect_renewal.txt and resume_history_search*. The protected-run
normalization proof and executable checks are in resume_normalization_algebra.*.
Each local or infinite-bulk identity must still be connected to a valid finite
decimal seed and an induction before it could establish Lychrel existence.

A universal advancing front was found: terminal pair-sum 8 against a uniform
6 tail, under alternating no-overflow/overflow operations, renews after six
steps and advances four columns. Its explicit local update is checked in the
separate resume_history_search_front.lean with no axioms. That module does
not contain a complete bridge to finite numeric trajectories. An independent
implementation checks the local semantics against actual integer steps.

For two symmetric fronts separated by 6^m, a controlled six-step window
becomes s,8,6^(m-5),8,s for m>=5. Thus a finite uniform gap is consumed.
Gap4 and gap5 collision identities are also checked in the local Lean model.
The initial front class alone does not force a regenerating collision:
additional wake digits change the outcome, and actual numeric examples
realize diverse centers. Moreover, a local front does not by itself prevent
palindromes in all phases. These local renewal identities therefore do not
provide a Lychrel witness. The search for a left-interface speed bound reached
its state cap and remains unresolved.

REPRODUCTION

The main Lean file is the canonical compiled deliverable. Modular Lean files
are retained as research history; once their declarations have been merged
into the main file, they should not be imported alongside that merged file
and recompiled as if their declarations were absent.

  lean Lychrel196Research.lean
  python3 resume_renewal_normalization.py
  python3 resume_normalization_algebra.py
  python3 resume_history_search_reconstruct.py

The archive excludes huge scratch state dumps, generated SAT instances, and
compiled binaries; code regenerates them when needed. Solver timeouts and
resource caps are preserved as unresolved outcomes.
