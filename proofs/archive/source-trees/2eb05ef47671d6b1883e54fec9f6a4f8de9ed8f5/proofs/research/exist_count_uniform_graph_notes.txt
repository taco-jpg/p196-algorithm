EXACT ALL-PERIOD SEARCH FOR A UNIFORM EVEN CORE
Status: a completed bounded-boundary exclusion, not a Lychrel-existence proof.

Search family
-------------
Coefficients are listed least-significant first. Let

    q = P ++ [B repeated M times] ++ reverse(P),

where P has fixed length k, its first coefficient is in 1..18, all other
coefficients are in 0..18, and M>=3. The six allowed bulk phases are

    16 -> 14 -> 10 -> 2 -> 4 -> 8 -> 16.

All bulk coefficients are even, so odd-width centers are admissible.
Every state is a canonical symmetric pair-sum word of a positive decimal
integer. Exact carry addition determines the next pair-sum word G(q).

Retain the transition only if

    G(q) = P' ++ [B_next repeated (M+h) times] ++ reverse(P'),

with |P'|=k and actual overflow bit h. The width of the boundary is held
fixed; the bulk may grow. States producing a palindrome at the next
numeric addition are discarded. Since B is never 0 or11, the overflow
bad pattern cannot occur. The remaining bad pattern is exactly B<=9
and every coefficient of P<=9.

Why the graph is independent of M
---------------------------------
Put C=0 when B<9 and C=1 when B>9, and D=B-9C. Every occurrence of B
resets the carry to C regardless of its incoming bit: for b=0 or1,

    floor((B+b)/10)=C.

Let b be the carry after the low prefix P. The first bulk output digit
is B+b-10C; every subsequent bulk output digit is D. The high prefix
therefore always receives carry C. Its digits, the final overflow h,
and the first/last k output pair sums are independent of M.

For h=0, the interior output pair sums equal 2D=B_next. The two end
coefficients of the core equal B+b-10C+D. Thus the whole output core
has the required value exactly when b=C, independently of M>=2.

For h=1, let p be the last coefficient of P and
H=floor((p+C)/10). The two endpoint pair sums of the new core equal

    (B+b-10C)+(p+C-10H),

while every intervening pair sum equals 2D=B_next. Hence again the
condition is independent of M>=2. The overflow inserts one extra core
position, giving length M+1. These formulas prove that testing M=3 is
already sufficient for the entire family M>=3; M=4 supplies a separate
parity control rather than an unsupported extrapolation.

Finite graph and certificate meaning
------------------------------------
A state is (phase,P), so there are exactly

    6 * 18 * 19^(k-1)

states at boundary width k. The graph is functional: each state has at
most one outgoing edge. A safe directed cycle would provide an exact
expanding family for every M>=3. Starting from an integer realizing one
of its q words, the first numeric iterate would be nonpalindromic and
its future pair-sum words would remain on the safe cycle. Thus a cycle
would give a genuine finite decimal Lychrel witness, not an infinite
digit string. No cycle was found at the exhaustively checked widths.

Completed results

 4     740,772            189,060                 0
 5  14,074,668          3,835,113                 0
 6 267,418,692         75,297,120                 0

The cycle traversal exhausts every temporal period, rather than a chosen
list of periods. Therefore no family in this exact form with boundary
width k<=6 can persist forever. Width six took about 51 seconds. Larger
boundaries, growing boundaries, and nonuniform periodic bulk words are
not excluded.

Independent control
-------------------
exist_count_uniform_graph.cpp builds the graph using ordinary column
arithmetic, separately evaluating core lengths M=3 and M=4 at every
safe state. It checks that validity, overflow, and output boundary agree.

exist_count_uniform_verify.py shares no transition code. It constructs
ordinary positive decimal integers realizing each q, computes the literal
integer sum n+reverse(n), and recovers its pair sums. For all states with
k<=3 it checks M=3,4,7,8 and reproduces all state, discarded-state, edge,
overflow-edge, and cycle counts.

Commands
--------
  g++ -O3 -std=c++17 exist_count_uniform_graph.cpp -o exist_count_uniform_graph
  ./exist_count_uniform_graph 1 5 exist_count_uniform_graph_results.json
  ./exist_count_uniform_graph 6 6 exist_count_uniform_graph_k6_results.json
  python3 exist_count_uniform_verify.py

These are computer-assisted finite graph exclusions with a mathematical
length-independence proof. The complete graph computation has not been
formalized in Lean. It is separate from exist_count_growth.lean, whose
numeric width-growth theorems are kernel checked.
