TWO SAFE INCOMING ITERATES DO NOT REDUCE THE OVERFLOW FIBER RATE
Status: exact arbitrary-length obstruction to a proposed counting bound.
This is not a proof of a decimal Lychrel seed.

Definitions
-----------
A canonical pair-sum word q is a nonempty symmetric word over 0,...,18,
with q[0]>0 and an even center when its length is odd. It is realizable
as the mirrored digit sums of a positive finite decimal integer.
G(q) is the mirrored pair-sum word of sum_i q[i] 10^i.
Call q safe if this numeric reverse-add output is not a palindrome.
The exact unsafe patterns are (i) every coefficient <=9, or (ii) outer
coefficient11 and all coefficients in{0,11}.

THEOREM
-------
For all integers m>=0 and k>=1, set L=2(m+k+7). There is one canonical
word r of width L+1 with at least 2^m different width-L words q2 such that:

  * G(q2)=r, and this transition overflows;
  * q2=G(q1), q1=G(q0), with both transitions non-overflowing;
  * q0,q1,q2 are safe;
  * r,G(r),...,G^(k-1)(r) are safe.

The words q0 are distinct canonical pair-sum words of actual finite
positive seeds. Choosing any decimal realization gives non-palindromic
starting seeds, because q0 has odd outer coefficient3. Their numeric
reverse-add orbits have no palindrome at indices0 through k+3, and all
merge at numeric index4.

Consequently the maximum overflow-fiber exponential rate stays2 per
mirrored input pair even after imposing TWO incoming safe history steps
and any fixed k-step future safety requirement. More quantitatively,
the displayed restricted fiber has size at least

    2^(L/2-k-7).

The unrestricted sharp maximum is2^(L/2-1), so the loss is only the
constant factor2^(k+6), independent of L. No uniform bound of the form
C_k rho^(L/2), with rho<2, can hold for this restricted family.

Construction
------------
Use alphabet A=5 and B=14. For every free word w in{A,B}^m, append a
six-symbol correction v(w), defined by the 16-state table below. Form

    half(q2) = (14), w, v(w), (18 repeated k times),
    q2       = half(q2), reverse(half(q2)).

All q2 have the same overflow image

    r = (5, 10^(m+6), 14, 18^(2k-1), 14, 10^(m+6), 5),

where powers denote repeated coefficients. Indeed, none of the input
coefficients equals9. In an overflow update define
rho(x)=x-9*[x>=10]. Here rho(5)=rho(14)=5 and rho(18)=9; every interior
new coefficient is the sum of adjacent rho values, and the outer
coefficient is14-9=5. This yields the displayed r exactly.

Two-layer inverse automaton
---------------------------
For one non-overflow inverse layer, state(a,b) stores the low incoming
carry and high outgoing carry. On target coefficient s, solve

    floor((x+a)/10)=a', floor((x+b')/10)=b,
    ((x+a) mod10)+((x+b') mod10)=s.

The next state is(a',b') and the emitted predecessor coefficient is x.
For target s other than0,9,18 the next state is deterministic:

    E, for even s: 00->00, 01->10, 10->01, 11->11;
    O, for odd  s: 00->01, 01->11, 10->00, 11->10.

Compose two inverse layers. Let their states be(u,v). On a target5,
the first predecessor coefficient is2 or12, so the joint transition is

    T_A(u,v)=(O(u),E(v)).

On a target14 the first predecessor coefficients for
u=00,01,10,11 are respectively7,17,6,16. Therefore

    T_B(u,v)=(E(u),O(v)) if the first bit of u is0;
             (E(u),E(v)) if the first bit of u is1.

Each is a permutation of the16 joint states. All intermediate target
symbols used above lie in{2,6,7,12,16,17}, so no exceptional inverse
transition is hidden in this description. Begin at(00,00). Reading
the initial14 emits predecessor coefficients7 and3, and leaves state
(00,01). Read the free word w and call the resulting joint state(u,v).

Append the following six-symbol correction, with A=5 and B=14:

    u    v      correction
    00   00     BBAABB
    00   01     BAABBB
    00   10     ABBABB
    00   11     AABBBB
    01   00     BAABBA
    01   01     BBABBB
    01   10     AAABAA
    01   11     ABBBBB
    10   00     ABBAAB
    10   01     AABBAB
    10   10     ABAABB
    10   11     BABBBB
    11   00     AABBAA
    11   01     AABAAB
    11   10     AAABBA
    11   11     BBBBBB

Direct application of T_A and T_B verifies that every row ends at
(11,11). At this state, target18 emits predecessor18 in each layer
and leaves(11,11) unchanged. Thus appending k copies of18 ends with
matching carries at the even center of both inverse words. The two
non-overflow inverse layers are valid for the entire reflected words,
not only for their prefixes. Their outer coefficients7 and3 are
positive. They both contain the same central18 block of length2k.

Safety and counting
-------------------
The central18 blocks make q0,q1,q2 safe by the exact unsafe-pattern
criterion. The output r has a central18 block of length2k-1. Under G,
a central constant18 block loses at most two positions per iteration:
inside that block the carry is1, each output digit is9, and interior
mirrored pairs remain18. Thus a nonempty18 block remains for every
j=0,...,k-1 in G^j(r), proving the future safety claim.

Different w give different q2, because w occupies the same initial
positions of their half-words. Their q0 must also be different since
G^2(q0)=q2. There are exactly2^m choices of w. For any realization n0
of q0, the pair sums at numeric times0,1,2,3 are q0,q1,q2,r. Therefore
the numeric values at time4 are all sum_i r[i]10^i, independent of w.

What remains open
-----------------
The theorem disproves an exponential contraction obtained merely from
two incoming image constraints, even with any fixed future safety
horizon. It neither proves nor disproves contraction when the incoming
depth grows with the current width. A finite-seed all-time proof needs
that growing-depth regime, or a different structural mechanism.

The construction varies its finite starting seeds with m and k. It
does not exchange the quantifiers to claim one seed survives forever.
No bound on total first-hit probability, no positive uniform survivor
fraction for a fixed seed ensemble, and no decimal Lychrel witness
has been established.
