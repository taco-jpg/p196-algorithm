ASTRA SUBSTITUTION / MULTIPLE PERIODIC WORD BLOCKS
13 October 2026

Status: no decimal Lychrel witness or all-time decimal orbit is established.
The constructions and search restrictions below are precisely scoped. A
finite search cap, solver timeout, or missing candidate is not an impossibility
result for the underlying existence problem.

1. A REDUCTION FOR FIXED-TIME NONERASING MORPHIC CERTIFICATES

Let F be the exact decimal mirrored-sum-word update, on valid nonempty words.
Suppose sigma is a nonerasing morphism on a finite alphabet, w is a finite word,
and a length-preserving coding gives Q_n from sigma^n(w). Assume for a fixed
p>=1 that F^p(Q_n)=Q_(n+1), for every n>=0. Then

    |sigma^n(w)| <= |w| + pn.

This follows because one reverse-and-add step increases decimal width by at
most one. In particular a direct fixed-time Fibonacci, Thue-Morse, or other
exponentially growing nonerasing substitution cannot be such a certificate.

More strongly, after passing to finitely many phases and discarding a finite
initial segment, these words have the form

    C_0 U_1^n C_1 U_2^n ... U_s^n C_s,

with fixed finite words C_i,U_i (empty U_i are allowed). Merged equal blocks
may equivalently have fixed positive integer multiples of n as exponents.
Thus fixed-time nonerasing morphic certificates reduce to finitely many
growing periodic-word blocks. This is a reduction of a certificate class,
not an assertion that arbitrary reverse-and-add orbits have this form.

Proof of the stronger statement:

Make the directed incidence multigraph of sigma: each occurrence of b in
sigma(a) gives a separate edge a->b. Restrict to vertices reachable from w.
The total number of length-n paths from the occurrences in w is exactly
|sigma^n(w)|.

Every cyclic strongly connected component must be a simple directed cycle
with one internal outgoing edge at each vertex. Otherwise there are two
closed walks beginning with distinct occurrence edges at some vertex. By
repeating them to equal lengths,
one obtains two distinct equal-length return walks; arbitrary concatenations
give exponentially many paths, contradicting the linear bound. Parallel
edges count as distinct edges here, as they must for word lengths.

No directed path may pass through three cyclic strongly connected components.
If it did, take a common multiple of their cycle lengths. Independently choose
the numbers of turns in the first two cycles, then spend the remaining time
in the third. Along a suitable arithmetic progression of path lengths this
gives quadratically many distinct paths, again contradicting the linear bound.

Call a letter bounded when the lengths of all its iterates are bounded. Every
remaining cyclic component containing unbounded letters is a simple cycle,
and every occurrence emitted off its cycle is a bounded letter: an emitted
unbounded letter would lead to another nonterminal cycle and then a third
cyclic component. Transient unbounded letters disappear after a bounded
number of substitutions, because their incidence subgraph is acyclic and
no cyclic component can emit them.

Pass to a power T=sigma^P that fixes the position of each unbounded cycle
letter a. Then T(a)=L_a a R_a, where L_a,R_a contain only bounded letters.
Bounded words have finite orbits, because their lengths and alphabet are
bounded. Increase P so that every bounded-letter orbit is eventually fixed
under T. There is a common J such that T^j(L_a)=L_a^* and
T^j(R_a)=R_a^* for every j>=J and every relevant unbounded letter a.
For n>=J,

  T^n(a) = (L_a^*)^(n-J)
           [T^(J-1)(L_a) ... T(L_a) L_a]
           a
           [R_a T(R_a) ... T^(J-1)(R_a)]
           (R_a^*)^(n-J).

After the initial transient, the seed is a fixed concatenation of such
unbounded cycle letters and bounded words. Substitute this formula into each
letter; bounded intervening words are eventually fixed. This proves the
displayed finite-block form for each of finitely many original phases.
Length-preserving coding preserves that form. QED.

The fixed-time assumption is essential: a scale-doubling construction with
return times increasing with the scale is not excluded by this argument.
Erasing codings and more general state-dependent graph substitutions are
also outside the stated theorem.

2. EXACT PERIODIC-WORD FAMILY CHECKER

astra_substitution_periodic.py represents a segment by (u,a,b): the prefix of
the infinite periodic word u^infinity having length ak+b, for every k>=0.
The word u may be nonconstant; several unrelated motifs may occur. This can
represent an unbounded number of constant-digit runs, which the previous
constant-run checker could not do with a bounded run list.

Every retained segment has a>=0 and b>=1. Its slope is divisible by its motif
period. Reverse orientation and every consumed-prefix rotation are therefore
independent of k. Alignment compares affine lengths only when the same
inequality holds for every k>=0. Crossing branches are rejected as unresolved.
The pairwise sum of two aligned periodic streams has period dividing the
least common multiple of their periods. All operations use integer arithmetic.

For digit carries, a non-nine motif resets the carry within one repetition;
an all-nine motif transmits the carry unchanged. The checker explicitly
processes the first motif, then the stabilized periodic tail. Thus no claim
of an infinite identity is based on sampling long finite strings.

Equality is checked over each full variable period, including symbols not
present at k=0. This matters: testing only the shortest segment could miss a
later mismatch. Parameter residues make all slopes divisible by every spatial
period; each residue phase is checked against the next, including wraparound.

Safety uses the exact bad-q criterion supplied in the main research archive.
A q word is safe iff it contains an entry>=10 and either its outer entry is
not11 or some entry is outside{0,11}. Any witness present in a segment at k=0
remains present for every k>=0, since that segment only gets longer. Canonical
symmetry is checked as an exact word identity. The even central coefficient
condition is checked for all parameter values by residue classes modulo twice
each possible central motif's period and exact integer interval feasibility
for every possible
central segment.

A successful candidate consists of a finite number R of residue families
Q_(r,k), each valid and safe through p exact updates, with

    F^p(Q_(r,k)) = Q_(r+1,k)       for r<R-1,
    F^p(Q_(R-1,k)) = Q_(0,k+1).

The implementation constructs these as Q_(r,k)=Q(Rk+r+shift).
Induction would give a finite decimal Lychrel witness by realizing Q_(0,0),
taking its first carried numeric output, and using safety at every phase.
No decimal instantiation has been obtained.

Tests completed:
- 9,592 comparisons between symbolic periodic-family transitions and literal
  q-word carry arithmetic, in bases2,3,10 and at parameters0,1,2,7.
- An independent audit additionally checks 7,200 symbolic transitions against
  ordinary integer reversal and addition, and 1,000 exact center-parity cases.
  The audit caught and repaired an attempted generalization of the decimal
  bad-word criterion to base 2. The decimal criterion was unaffected. Bases
  other than 10 now use an actual digit mismatch persisting for all parameters.
- The established expanding binary family is reproduced, including its exact
  four-step all-parameter identity and phase safety.
- 2,000 random symmetric decimal starts containing two to four repeated word
  motifs of lengths2..6, each examined for up to480 steps. Ten reached a
  palindrome;1,990 reached the finite step cap. No repeated compressed
  template candidate was found. This is discovery evidence only.

3. TWO-BULK SYNTHESIS

astra_substitution_twobulk.py searches the bounded family

 P B^m V C^n W reverse(C)^n reverse(V) reverse(B)^m reverse(P),

where B,C are independently selected nonconstant period-three words, W is
palindromic, and P,V,W are unknown finite boundaries. Under24 alternating
nonoverflow/overflow steps it seeks m->m+1 and n->n+1. The total width gain
is12, equal to the number of overflows. There are198 period-three motifs that
return under this24-step bulk clock (enumerated from the archived bulk graph).

Sixty bounded finite SMT queries, with exterior widths3..12, interface widths
2..8, and center widths2..8, all returned UNKNOWN at two-second solver limits.
They exclude no templates. Any SAT candidate would still need the exact
all-parameter whole-word checker; none was returned.

astra_substitution_gluing.py decomposes the same proposed construction into
three local synthesis problems, using exact cyclic carry matching:

- An exterior P coupled to the outer periodic bulk B.
- A B-to-C interface moving three positions in24 steps, with a frame shift
  every eight steps.
- A finite symmetric center behind C, moving six positions in24 steps as the
  global width grows by12. Its width alternates by one with width parity.

If all three components match, B gains one repetition and C gains one
repetition in each half of the full symmetric word. The discovery tool then
submits the assembled word to the separate exact whole-word checker. Local
components alone do not establish a finite orbit.

The first 396 local SMT queries, limited to 0.3 seconds, all timed out. A refined
two-second sweep over 208 motifs (198 nonconstant plus 10 uniform even motifs)
completed 416 queries: exterior 150 UNSAT and 58 UNKNOWN; center 28 SAT,
178 UNSAT and 2 UNKNOWN. These solver outcomes were superseded at the relevant
bounds by the complete integer enumerations below. No solver-reported UNSAT
result in these scripts is treated as a formally checked proof of exclusion.

4. COMPLETED FINITE LOCAL ENUMERATIONS AND POSITIVE CENTER COMPONENTS

astra_substitution_enumerate.py replaces short SMT queries with deterministic
integer enumeration of the initial component word and its 24-step evolution.
No future carry is guessed: each component's incoming and outgoing carries
must match the explicitly computed periodic bulk carries at every phase.

For a symmetric initial center of width 6, there are 19^3=6,859 possibilities.
Over 208 bulk motifs this exhausts 1,426,672 center states. Exactly 28 local
24-step center loops occur, one for each of 28 motifs;18 motifs are nonconstant.
Their explicit initial centers and complete24-phase carry/word traces are in
astra_substitution_center_certificates.json. The two nonperiodic center patches
among the nonconstant cases are:

  C=(9,8,10), W=(9,8,12,12,8,9);
  C=(9,10,8), W=(9,10,6,6,10,9).

The remaining 16 nonconstant center loops have W=C C with C palindromic.
All 28 positive components were independently embedded in finite symmetric
decimal q words and checked by ordinary integer reverse-and-add at every
phase:672 literal-integer component checks. They remain LOCAL components,
not decimal Lychrel witnesses; compatible exteriors and interfaces are absent.

A direct enumeration of all 6,840 nonconstant triples verifies that exactly
the stated 198 motifs return under the 24-step alternating bulk map. This does
not rely on the archived list of bulk cycles. An all-nine periodic region has
ambiguous cyclic carry states and is outside this nonneutral bulk model.

For exteriors, a width 4 exhaustive enumeration of 1,426,672 initial states
over 208 motifs found no24-step loops. A separate necessary outer-three
prefilter makes the larger width 6 bound tractable:

  states=(a,b,c), a in{8,17}, b,c in{0,...,18};
  allow either possible incoming carry from the omitted interior;
  retain exact next prefixes with the required alternating outer entry.

This complete graph has 722 vertices and 741 directed edges. Its length24
closed walks can begin at a nonoverflow phase only at

  (8,12,9), (8,13,5), (8,14,8), (8,16,16).

Every actual24-step alternating exterior loop projects to such a closed walk,
so restricting to these four prefixes is a necessary-condition filter, not
an assumption about the omitted interior. The entire graph is saved in
astra_substitution_outer_three_graph.json. The standalone generator recomputes
all states, edges, and 24-step reachability. Its edges were independently
checked using 1,444 literal integer constructions, one for each vertex and
each incoming carry.

The filtered width 6 search therefore needs only 4*19^3=27,436 initial exterior
words per motif. Across 208 motifs it exhausts 5,706,688 states and finds no
24-step exterior loop. This excludes this specific width 6 exterior model,
regardless of any inner interface/center proposed behind the selected bulk.
It does not exclude wider exteriors, other temporal periods, other clocks,
neutral-nine annuli, other spatial periods, or aperiodic backgrounds.

Reproduce the completed arithmetic work:

  python3 astra_substitution_enumerate.py --kind center --width 6 \
    --include-uniform
  python3 astra_substitution_enumerate.py --kind exterior --width 6 \
    --include-uniform --prefix-filter --out astra_substitution_exterior_width6.json
  python3 astra_substitution_audit.py

The code is an exact integer checker, not an instantiated Lean proof.

5. REMAINING GAP

The missing object is unchanged: one valid finite decimal q word in an exactly
closed safe family. Neither the morphic reduction, the binary control, local
bulk periodicity, a long finite trajectory, nor a finite solver timeout fills
that gap. No decimal existence claim follows from these files.
