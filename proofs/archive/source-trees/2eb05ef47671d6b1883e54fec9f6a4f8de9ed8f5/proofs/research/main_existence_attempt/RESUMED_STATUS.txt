1. REPAIRED FINITE-SEED ENCODING

The former search bottleneck came from integer or bit-vector rank variables.
The replacement selects a finite accepting path by Boolean flow constraints.
The source has no incoming edges; each selected noninitial vertex has at
most one incoming edge and at most one outgoing edge or terminal. Exactly
one initial departure and one terminal are selected, with flow conservation
at every other vertex. The source path cannot enter a cycle: the first
repeated vertex would have two incoming selected edges. Therefore it must
reach the selected terminal. Disconnected selected cycles do not invalidate
that witness.

Every nonempty allowed language has a simple accepting path. Selecting that
path is sufficient: its least closure lies inside any closed language that
contains it. Hence the encoding is complete for the specified grammar
template, although that template does not cover every possible invariant.

For the one-tile grammar, the 46-state transducer splits into components of
5 and 41 states, since every transition preserves the overflow flag. Any
nonempty grammar thus has an accepting seed with at most 40 edges and 81
digit positions. This bounds a seed for this grammar class, not an orbit's
palindrome hitting time. The sound witness extracted from a safe closed
grammar would be T^2 of the realized input, of width at most 83.

3. EXACT OBSTRUCTION TO GLOBAL GROUP-SCORE INVARIANTS

Assign a fixed group label to every edge and terminal tile of the exact
one-row carry-history transducer. Let S(q) be their ordered product for the
complete history q -> G(q), where G is the folded decimal update. Suppose
S(q)=e whenever G(q) is a palindrome-producing folded word, and suppose
S(G(q))=phi(S(q)) for every valid q, with phi a fixed group automorphism.
Then every complete score S(q) is the identity. Such a score cannot provide
a nonidentity finite seed that proves palindrome avoidance.

The exact proof uses useful-state prefix potentials for both the safety
language and the two-history product graph. A gauge transformation preserves
every complete product and makes the 44 spanning-forest labels identity.
For ordinary conservation, 2,429 unit-relator eliminations make all 1,123
tile labels and all auxiliary potentials identity. For the conjugated
update S(G(q))=h^-1 S(q) h, the same eliminations leave only the free
generator h. Every automorphism case embeds into such a conjugation via
a semidirect product with the infinite cyclic group.

An independent checker replays each elimination from the original equations,
checks the abelianized equations against the additive formulation, and
audits the conjugated gauge in the free group. Root independently reran
that checker successfully. The full scope, proof, exact certificates and
checker are in group_invariant/. This is a computational obstruction to
this particular score class, not a Lean proof or a result about all
invariants. It applies to arbitrary groups, finite or infinite.

A separate exact linear-algebra certificate excludes every finite-field
scalar score of the corresponding kind, for every nonzero scalar. More
generally its Laurent-polynomial pivots work over every field. Its 1,079
unit pivots leave only the 44-dimensional telescoping gauge space. Root
independently replayed this certificate as well; see finite_field/.
