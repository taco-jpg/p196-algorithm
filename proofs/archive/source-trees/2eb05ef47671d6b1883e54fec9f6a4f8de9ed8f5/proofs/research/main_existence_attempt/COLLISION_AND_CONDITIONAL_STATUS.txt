2. TWO EXACT BOUNDARY OBSTRUCTIONS

An exact front joins525 and936 domains and returns after four steps
translated one place. It also glues to a finite symmetric center. However,
a fixed24-phase finite525 exterior would insert four525 blocks per return,
adding48 to total q mass, or24 to the integer's digit sum. Decimal digit
sum after24 steps is unchanged modulo9, since2^24=1 modulo9. This is a
contradiction. Thus every finite exterior of that specific24-step family
is excluded, irrespective of width. At period24k,3 must divide k;72 is
the smallest multiple of24 not excluded by this argument.

The alternative indefinitely alternating outer coefficients9,18 are also
impossible, at any temporal period. At an N phase the second coefficient
Q lies in0..9. Exact two-step carry enumeration gives only continuing
edges0->9 and4->8 or9. Both destination vertices are sinks. The archived
rank decreases on every continuing edge, so there is no infinite path.
This is a local obstruction to that clock branch, not to decimal Lychrel
numbers. It was independently checked by another agent.
