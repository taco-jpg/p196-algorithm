3. PROBABILITY AND CORRELATION
For every even width 2m there are 9*10^(m-1) nonpalindromic inputs with every
mirrored pair sum equal to nine. Every input maps immediately to 10^(2m)-1.
Internal digits are uniform and endpoint Fourier coefficients have maximum
nontrivial modulus 1/9; the output modulus is one. Thus marginal uniformity
alone cannot justify the proposed Fourier contraction or rare-palindrome
bound. A separate all-length two-atom family violates the claimed local
carry-decoupling inequality while all selected marginal frequencies are 1/2.
These are counterexamples to deductions from single-time marginal bounds,
not a refutation of the deterministic 196 conjecture or of a separately
verified all-future hypothesis.

Exact one-step palindrome-predecessor counts are proved in writing:
N_1=4;
N_(2m)=45*55^(m-1)+8*9^(m-1);
N_(2m+1)=225*55^(m-1)+8*9^(m-1), m>=1.
Root checked these against every positive integer below one million.
A sufficient all-time prefix-count inequality is stated precisely, but its
hypotheses were not verified for any fixed surviving ensemble. In a finite
ensemble of size M, a nonzero first-hit probability is at least 1/M. An
all-time bound tending to zero therefore requires eventual deterministic
exclusion, not merely favorable finite frequencies.
Written proof and source audit: probability/PROBABILITY_RESULT.md.
