2. COMPLETE14-PHASE SPATIAL GRAPH EXCLUSION

At spatial cut i, retain:
  A_t: low carry entering coefficient q_(t,i), in every phase;
  B_t: high carry leaving that coefficient toward the outside, in N phases;
  Z_t: q_(t,i-1)-10*(high carry leaving i-1), in O phases.

The actual delayed high digit in an O phase is Z_t+b_t, where b_t is the
high carry leaving column i. Thus it is constrained to0..9. This removes
unneeded O carry coordinates without discarding their consistency bounds.

For each candidate column q_t, the exact temporal equations are:
  N: q_(t+1)=(q_t+A_t) mod10 + (q_t+c_t) mod10,

B'_t=c_t in N phases, and Z'_t=q_t-10*b_t in O phases.
