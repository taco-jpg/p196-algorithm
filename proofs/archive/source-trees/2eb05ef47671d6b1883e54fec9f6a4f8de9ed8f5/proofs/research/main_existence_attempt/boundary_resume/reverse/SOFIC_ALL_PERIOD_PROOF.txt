Why a temporal ring is the right object
--------------------------------------
Use an arbitrary positive number n of N/O pairs; the temporal period is
2n. A cut state is now a cyclic word of length n over the 88-letter alphabet

  (a,b,c,z) in {0,1}^3 times {-1,0,...,9}.

No step of the following construction chooses or bounds n. The local exact
inverse equations are those proved in REVERSE_CLOSURE_PROOF.txt, with indices
in Z/nZ instead of Z/12Z. The exterior root is the constant cyclic word in
the letter r=(0,1,1,7). Its code is41 under the encoding

  code(a,b,c,z) = (((2a+b)*2+c)*11) + z+1.

The periodic bulk cuts have minimal temporal period six N/O pairs. All
twelve specified bulk phases belong to two temporal-rotation classes of
these six-letter words. Graph G0 is the disjoint union of their two directed
six-cycles, with each edge labeled by its corresponding cut letter. Thus
G0 accepts every target cyclic word at every compatible temporal period.

Cyclic languages and exact predecessor graphs
--------------------------------------------
An edge-labeled finite directed graph G accepts a cyclic word w of length
n>=1 iff there is a closed walk of n edges whose ordered labels are w.

For every letter s, let Options(s) be its exact local inverse fiber. These
fibers have at most two members and are exhaustively checked by ordinary
forward decimal arithmetic. Write Output(x,y) for the prior cut letter
determined by adjacent options x and y; leave it undefined when their
compatibility inequalities fail.

Construct the raw predecessor graph H=PreRaw(G) as follows:

  vertices: pairs (e,x), where e is a labeled edge of G and
            x belongs to Options(label(e));

  edges: (e,x) -> (f,y) whenever target(e)=source(f) and
         Output(x,y) is defined;

  edge label: Output(x,y).

This is an exact predecessor-language construction. If a new cut word is
accepted by G and a prior cut word maps to it by one spatial column, choose
the accepting closed walk e_j and the inverse options x_j supplied by that
column. The pairs (e_j,x_j) give a closed walk in H whose labels are the prior
word. Conversely a closed walk in H gives a closed walk in G and a compatible
cyclic sequence of inverse options, so the local inverse-completeness proof
gives the corresponding spatial edge. The argument holds for all n>=1.

Trimming and the finite closure cycle
------------------------------------
Delete every edge of H that belongs to no directed cycle. Equivalently,
retain only edges inside a nontrivial strongly connected component or a
self-loop. A finite closed walk lies wholly inside one strongly connected
component, so this trimming preserves exactly the cyclic language.

This is not an inference from equal vertex or edge counts. Therefore the

Proof-carrying finite certificate independent of an SCC implementation
--------------------------------------------------------------------
sofic_rank_certificate.json stores the eight graphs, totaling1,940 vertices
and7,442 edges. For each raw predecessor graph it supplies two arrays:

  rank[u]     : a natural-number rank on raw vertices;
  mapping[u]  : a retained vertex of the next graph, or -1 if removed.

The checker exhaustively verifies for EVERY raw labeled edge u -(l)-> v:

  rank[u] <= rank[v];
  if rank[u]=rank[v], both mappings are defined and
     mapping[u] -(l)-> mapping[v] is an edge of the destination graph.

Around any finite closed walk, a nondecreasing sequence of natural ranks
returns to its starting rank, so all ranks are equal. Hence all walk edges
project to a labeled closed walk in the destination graph. These finite
local checks alone prove the required predecessor-language inclusion.
One does not have to trust the SCC procedure that originally found them.

Root exclusion
--------------
The same certificate supplies a root-only rank for every graph. For EVERY
edge labeled r=(0,1,1,7), its source rank is strictly less than its target
rank. A positive-length closed walk all of whose labels are r would give
a strict rank increase back to its starting vertex, a contradiction.
Thus the constant root word r^n is rejected by every G0,...,G7 for every
n>=1. A separate structural audit observes the stronger short-factor fact
that no accepted word contains three consecutive r letters; the general
rank exclusion already suffices here.

All-width conclusion
--------------------
Suppose a finite exterior of width k exists at some temporal period2n.
Its cut after the first mandatory8/17 column is r^n. Every subsequent
column gives one exact spatial graph edge, and the final cut is one of
the accepted periodic bulk target words. Starting at that target and
following the finite sequence of spatial predecessors puts every earlier
cut in the union of the eight accepted languages. In particular r^n must
belong to that union, contrary to root exclusion. This contradiction is
independent of k and n.
