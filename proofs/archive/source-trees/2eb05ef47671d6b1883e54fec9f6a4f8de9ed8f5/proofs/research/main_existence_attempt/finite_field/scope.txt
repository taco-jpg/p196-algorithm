Exact obstruction to scalar finite-field carry-history scores
===========================================================

No decimal Lychrel existence or nonexistence claim is made.

where c is a nonzero element of a field, and require F(q->Gq)=0 whenever
Gq is a palindrome-predecessor word. These conditions cannot produce a
nonzero score on ANY accepted history, for ANY field and ANY nonzero c.
