1. COMPLETED: EXACT UNIFORM DIGIT-SUM ESCAPE IN LEAN

Let T(n)=n+reverse10(n), and let S(n) be the ordinary decimal digit sum.
Set E(0)=0, E(1)=1, and for M>=2 define

    E(M)=5*(2*(M-1)*E(M-1)+2*M-1).

For every positive integer n and every natural B,s there is an integer t
such that

    s <= t <= s+E(2B),     S(T^t(n)) > B.

Thus every block of E(2B)+1 consecutive iterates exceeds the ceiling B at
least once. The bound is independent of n, its decimal width, and the
starting time s. No initial digit-sum bound is assumed. The theorem includes
196. It does not claim pointwise divergence of the digit sums.

This is the exact decimal recurrence already proved in the written
manuscript. The new contribution in this pass is its completed concrete
formalization, including the previously missing nonsymmetric isolation and
the exact embedding of ordinary decimal integer orbits.

The proof extends digit-pair sums to finite nonsymmetric profiles and
inducts on a mass ceiling. A sufficiently long zero gap isolates a smaller
positive component for the required time, allowing the induction hypothesis.
If there is no long gap, bounded mass and controlled gaps bound the support
width, contradicting its proved growth. Positive endpoints give the sharper
width estimate, and the mass-one case is proved directly. The profile mass
along an embedded numeric orbit is exactly twice its ordinary digit sum.

proved by DecimalProfile.sharpEscapeBound_eq. The corresponding special

3. MOVING-FRONT CRITERION: STRUCTURAL OBSTRUCTION FOUND BY AUDIT

A different attempt allowed an arbitrary growing wake behind a finite
known suffix. Its intended certificate used an upper potential for the
front's displacement relative to the moving center. Independent audit
found the specified local operation makes that criterion impossible.

For an edge v->u, let Delta be the cut displacement, h the overflow bit,
and s_v,s_u the retained suffix lengths. The operation discards h leading
columns and possibly D>=0 further known columns. Hence exactly

    Delta-s_u+s_v = h+D.

On a cycle, suffix lengths telescope and

    sum(2*Delta-h) = sum h + 2*sum D.

The proposed upper potential requires this sum to be nonpositive. Thus
no cycle can contain an overflow. Closure under both overflow choices in
a finite nonempty graph forces an all-overflow cycle, a contradiction.
The same argument applies to complete graphs with the prescribed N,O
clock. This rules out the exact criterion for every suffix budget K and
spatial period p; increasing the finite search cap cannot repair it.

The preceding raw searches are preserved: 561 completed local graphs with
positive cycles, 516 state-cap outcomes, and 69 transient motifs. Their
individual execution statuses remain unchanged; the structural theorem
supersedes uncertainty about satisfiability of this particular potential
criterion. Root replayed all 561 saved cycles by independent ordinary-integer
arithmetic. Three direct finite-family searches with other spatial periods
and free overflow flags timed out and remain UNKNOWN; the local criterion
obstruction does not apply to those separate queries.

See constructive/STATUS.md and INDEPENDENT_CRITERION_AUDIT.txt. The original
conditional construction is retained with its inconsistency made explicit.
This is not an obstruction to all growing-wake constructions or Lychrel
numbers; a different operation that recovers earlier information is outside
the argument. The new obstruction is a written proof, not a Lean theorem.
