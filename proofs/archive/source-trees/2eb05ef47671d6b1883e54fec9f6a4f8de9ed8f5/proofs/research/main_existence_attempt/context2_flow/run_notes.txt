CONTEXT-TWO RANK-FREE POSITIVE INVARIANT SEARCH

Scope
-----
The target is a nonempty forward-closed feature language that avoids the
exact bad language. Only a positive fixed point, followed by independent
all-length verification, can yield a decimal Lychrel witness. Rejected
seed closures do not prove that those individual integer orbits terminate.
Timeouts and graph caps are UNKNOWN, never nonexistence results.

Encoding
--------
See encoding_proof.txt. The 21,409 feature Booleans select a unique simple
path from one of two starts to a terminal, possibly with disconnected
cycles that are discarded. There are 874 graph nodes. Degree/flow and
cardinality constraints replace ranks without losing completeness for
nonempty safe closed context-two languages. Every learned clause has a
finite numeric unsafe derivation, checked before use in the solver.

change its heuristic because proposed paths grew to roughly 700 pairs.
run600.log records 300 additional checked outcomes by 157.438467393

NONE of these additional outcomes is retained proof evidence or a

at most 400 seconds for the one authorized continuation. No attempt was
made to regenerate the lost sequence.

Always-false continuation
------------------------
Command:
python main_existence_attempt/context2_flow/search.py --seconds 400
  --prefix always_false400
  --resume main_existence_attempt/context2_flow/extraction_check3_unsafe_derivations.json

The current source sets phase='always_false' on SolverFor('QF_FD'). This
changes a heuristic only. The graph cap is 100,000 states per exact graph.
No length cutoff or restriction beyond the complete simple-path encoding
is imposed.

Each fully checked proof is appended and fsynced to
always_false400_unsafe_derivations.jsonl before a solver clause is added.
The journal starts with the 84 replayed baseline proofs. Complete lines
are authoritative; an incomplete trailing line after interruption is
ignored. SIGALRM, SIGINT, and SIGTERM request clean checkpointing. Creating
always_false400.stop requests termination at the next CEGIS iteration.
Final replay and artifact writing occur after the search timer and have
their own elapsed-time field. The final result JSON, when present, is the
authoritative record of this continuation's status and retained counts.

Replay
------
python main_existence_attempt/context2_flow/search.py --verify
  main_existence_attempt/context2_flow/always_false400_unsafe_derivations.jsonl

This replay checks concrete finite carry histories, feature admission,
ordinary integer reverse-and-add steps, and the final palindrome witness.
It does not rely on the SAT solver's search or reachability claims.
