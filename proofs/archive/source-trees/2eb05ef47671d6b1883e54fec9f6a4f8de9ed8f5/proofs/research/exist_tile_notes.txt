FULL-CARRY-TILE INVARIANT SEARCH: EXACT OUTCOMES

This branch did not prove decimal Lychrel existence. The any-seed grammar
search remains UNKNOWN. The results below exclude explicitly defined proof
abstractions for 196 and 18887, not the integers themselves.

Representation and grammar class
--------------------------------
A folded word w=(p,c) represents the symmetric pair-sum word
p ++ [c] ++ reverse(p), omitting c for even width. Every p entry lies in
0..18, its outer entry is positive, and an odd center c is even in 0..18.
G(w) is the pair-sum word after one ordinary decimal reverse-and-add step.
The fixed 46-state transducer records overflow, the low incoming carry,
the high outgoing carry, and the previous high output digit where needed.
A full tile is a labeled edge with BOTH endpoint states, or a center tile.
A tile language accepts every valid transducer path all of whose tiles lie
in a specified allowed set. This permits spatial recombination; it is much
coarser than an exact orbit or an arbitrary regular language.

Positive synthesis attempt and Horn simplification
--------------------------------------------------
For the composed G/G transducer, forward reachability and backward
co-reachability with allowed FIRST-row tiles determine exactly which
SECOND-row tiles occur on a complete accepted path. All such output tiles
must be allowed. Thus safety and closure admit a Horn implication encoding:
83,363 clauses, 3,731 variables, including 1,123 tile variables.
Only nonemptiness needs a non-Horn ranked accepting witness.

A least-Horn-closure computation contradicts safety for seed 196 in about
0.1s, and likewise for 18887. The any-seed full solver reached its45 s timeout.
A lazy learner instead proposed accepting paths, extracted Horn-conflict
cores, and checked 2,964 such candidates in 70 s. No positive invariant was
found. Adding those learned clauses to the full solver still timed out.
These outcomes are UNKNOWN for the unrestricted any-seed grammar class;
no UNSAT statement is made for that class.

Small fixed-grammar obstruction for 196
--------------------------------------
Four closure applications suffice, with spatial tile recombination between
them. Write (p;c), and '-' for no center:
 (7;18) -> (15;16) -> (6,13;-).
The tiles now available also accept (6;18). Therefore closure forces
 (6;18) -> (13;16) -> (4,11;-).
The last word maps to (9,3;-), all of whose full pair sums are <=9, hence
its following reverse-and-add output is a palindrome. A safe closed tile
language containing196 cannot exist. This does NOT say that the actual
196 orbit contains the recombined word (6;18).
Certificates: exist_tile_derivation_196.json (four steps, 11 tiles),
exist_tile_derivation_18887.json (27 steps, 49 tiles).

Arbitrary time dependence
-------------------------
Allowed tiles may vary freely with time; no periodic clock is assumed.
Projection of all accepted images forces a spurious two-step-palindrome
word by stage 16 from196 and stage 7 from18887. A finite certificate lists,
for every needed forced tile, an explicit accepted input word and its exact
image containing that tile. Each input uses only previously forced tiles.
The proof is induction over this finite dependency certificate.
The independent checker recomputes complete carry-tile signatures directly
from elementary decimal arithmetic; it imports no discovery or SAT code.

Strengthening by actual arithmetic invariants
--------------------------------------------
The same construction was repeated with separate width-parity tile sets
and exact digit-sum residue: half the full q sum is r_t mod 9, where
r_(t+1)=2*r_t mod 9. The required seed 196 residue is r_0=7.
Then alternating q value modulo 11 was imposed from time 6 onward. This is
valid for 196 since its third iterate 7436=11*676, and reversing a multiple
of11 remains divisible by 11. For an odd-width word the required residue is
  2*sum_i((-1)^i*p_i) + (-1)^len(p)*c == 0 mod11.
For even width the symmetric q word is automatically zero modulo 11.
Finally, separate length classes modulo 4, 8, 16, 32 were tested together with
both digit-sum and modulo 11 restrictions. Every tested abstraction still
fails at stage 16 for 196. The recorded certificates are independent of
any numeral-width cutoff. Individual certificates establish only the
listed modulus; no assertion for every possible modulus is made.

Examples of the forced spurious word at stage 16:
* Residue mod 9 + parity: (16,0,15;0), full qsum 62 ==8 mod18.
* Additionally mod11: (16,0,2,0,2,0,2;0), qsum 44 ==8 mod18 and
  alternating qsum 44 ==0 mod11.
* Additionally length mod 8: (16,0,0,16,1,2,2,2;2), length 17 ==1 mod 8,
  correct residue mod 9 and mod11. Its image is (7,7,0,7,8,3,4,4,4;-),
  a palindrome predecessor.

Precise finite-certificate theorem
---------------------------------
For each listed filter configuration, there is no sequence of tile sets
A_t (or A_(t,length class)), t>=0, such that:
  (1) the seed's folded pair-sum word is accepted at time 0;
  (2) every accepted word satisfies the configuration's filters;
  (3) G takes every accepted word at time t to an accepted word at time t+1;
  (4) no accepted word has a palindrome-predecessor image under G.
The certificates force a word violating (4) after 16 or 7 time transitions.
There is no Lean formalization of this concrete certificate theorem yet.

Positive direction left open
----------------------------
The obstruction identifies a lost dependency: allowed tiles can recombine
in ways absent from an orbit even when digit-sum, divisibility by 11,
length parity, and several length residues are preserved. A useful stronger
positive invariant would retain spatial correlations between tiles, such
as multi-tile blocks or a nontrivial stack/grammar. Merely adding a temporal
phase or the tested global residues cannot repair this particular proof
class for 196. Arbitrary regular languages and richer memory remain open.
