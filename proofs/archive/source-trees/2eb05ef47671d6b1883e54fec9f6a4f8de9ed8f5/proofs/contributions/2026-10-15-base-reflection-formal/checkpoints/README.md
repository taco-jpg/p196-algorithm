# Audit checkpoints

The three-module checkpoint is the exact successful 61-declaration report
and compiler log retained immediately before adding SeamSafety. It audits
9 prerequisite declarations plus 52 new declarations.

Earlier incremental 24- and 40-declaration reports were superseded before
checkpoint retention was requested; those original timing-bearing reports
are unavailable. The stable source hashes and independent phase reviews
identify those scopes. No replacement is presented as an original report.
The final complete check freshly rebuilds and audits all their declarations.
