## Actual local renewal and the limits of a high-region return

The [high-carry renewal proof](../contributions/2026-10-15-high-carry-renewal/HIGH_CARRY_RENEWAL.md)
gives an exterior-only third-output safety condition and two exact local
conditions that regenerate mixed positive-low/high coefficients at time two.
In decimal, outside coefficient at least 15 guarantees nonpalindromic first
and third positive outputs, even when the second is palindromic. Within the
stated high region, `q_0=18,q_1>=11`, or `14<=q_0<=17` with
`29<=q_0+q_1<=32`, guarantees three nonpalindromic positive outputs.
The initial seed need not be nonpalindromic.

For the restricted length-at-least-three nonoverflowing return mechanism
with all adjacent sums at least 28 and outside at most 17, return after two
steps to the high region is equivalent to all adjacent sums being at least
32. The boundary recurrence permits at most two consecutive such returns,
and the bound is attained. This does not exclude broader or later returns.
The all-parameter written proof, 177 ordinary controls, ten boundary controls,
independent 708-transition replay and root byte-exact reproduction are retained.

The [neutral-exterior lemma](../contributions/2026-10-15-neutral-exterior-overflow/NEUTRAL_EXTERIOR.md)
removes the overflow branch from every two-step high-region return, in every
base at least two. A symmetric word with exterior `b-1` that overflows has a
coefficient at most `b-2` in its updated actual-width pair word. Consequently,
a return to the high region must use a nonoverflowing second addition,
including the maximum initial outside coefficient case. This does not by
itself exclude broader returns. Its written proof and independent audit retain
22,773 exact controls, including 11,355 overflows; no Lean theorem is claimed.

## Congruence repair still fails the quantitative carry budget

The [finite-family construction](../contributions/2026-10-15-congruence-family-obstruction/CONGRUENCE_PATHS.md)
builds arbitrarily delayed terminating paths from the previously published
finite-prefix family. Their first and last edges are actual reverse-and-add;
the intermediate artificial edges preserve the exact immediate-palindrome
predicate, strict growth, width bounds and modulo-9/modulo-11 laws. The
quantifiers are pathwise, not one all-input countermap or an actual orbit.

For every family parameter at least three, the sparse artificial jump violates
the true digit-sum/carry-count lower bound. Thus the quantitative carry budget
excludes this specific congruence repair even though its residue laws hold.
All 134 paths, 8,259 states and 8,125 edges are retained in a lossless gzip,
with independent audit and root byte-exact reproduction. This is a written
obstruction and finite verification, not a new Lean theorem.
