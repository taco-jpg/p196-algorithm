Carry-history reconstruction and a renewable decimal bulk front

Status: these are exact auxiliary identities and a local propagation theorem.
They do not construct a finite decimal Lychrel number or prove the 196 conjecture.
The front theorem assumes the alternating N,O overflow schedule and a sufficiently
long uniform tail. These hypotheses still need a finite-word construction.

1. Constructive carry-only reconstruction

Write Q_t(x)=sum_i q_(t,i) x^i for the symmetric pair-sum polynomial at time t.
Let C_t(x) be the outgoing-carry polynomial; its last coefficient is the overflow
h_t in {0,1}. If the old width is L_t, the actual digit polynomial of the next
integer is E_t=Q_t+(x-10)C_t, including the leading overflow digit. Therefore

  Q_(t+1)=A_(h_t) Q_t + F_t,
  A_0=2, A_1=1+x,
  F_t=(x-10)C_t + rev_(L_t+h_t)((x-10)C_t).

Here rev_M reverses coefficients in the length-M coordinate frame. After a
window containing N non-overflows and H overflows,

  Q_t=2^N(1+x)^H Q_0 + F(carry history).

If N>=5, the first summand vanishes coefficientwise modulo32. Every coefficient
of Q_t belongs to {0,...,18}, so it is precisely the ordinary residue modulo32
of the corresponding coefficient of F. Thus the current Q is explicitly
recoverable from the carries alone: start with the zero polynomial, repeatedly
apply r->A_h r+F_t modulo32, and interpret the resulting coefficients in0,...,18.
A residue in19,...,31 proves that a proposed carry history is invalid.

Unlike an injectivity-only argument, this gives an executable decoder. Each
forcing term uses a carry and its adjacent carry on either reflected side.
Later overflows introduce at most H further spatial shifts. Thus the decoder
has a bounded spatial dependence range H+1, at most8 in a twelve-step window
satisfying the established growth bound. This does not make the forward carry
map a radius-eight cellular automaton: a newly computed carry can still propagate
through a run of q=9. It gives exact finite spacetime constraints instead.

resume_history_search_reconstruct.py checks the decoder against independent
ordinary integer arithmetic in7862 comparisons, including989 consecutive
12-step windows along196. For example, at step12 the integer is60744805 and
its reconstructed q word is(11,0,15,8,8,15,0,11). These finite checks validate
implementation; the polynomial calculation proves the general identity.

2. Universal advancing front

Use the mirrored-sum bulk update

  G_h(q)_i = (q_i+a_i) mod10 + (q_(i-h)+b_(i-h)) mod10,

where a_i,b_i are incoming carries from the left and right. Values below9 reset
carry to0, values above9 reset it to1, and9 propagates the incoming carry.
The supplied overflow schedule is N,O,N,O,N,O (h=0,1,0,1,0,1).

THEOREM. Suppose an arbitrary left region ends in a symbol8 and is followed by
an infinite uniform6 tail, before an N step. After six steps the rightmost
nonuniform symbol is again8, the tail is again uniform6, and the front has
advanced exactly four positions. The conclusion holds for every possible
sequence of carries arriving from the arbitrary left region.

PROOF. Only the following suffixes are needed; symbols further left can be
forgotten. The front displacement counts its rightmost nonuniform position.

 step   known suffix       uniform tail   displacement
 0      8                  6              0
 1      16+a               12             0
 2      10+a               6              1
 3      p,13               12             2
 4      p+4,7              6              3
 5      r,14               12             3
 6      s,8                6              4

Here a is0 or1, p=2a+c lies in0,...,3, and r=2p+8+e lies in8,...,15;
c and e are further incoming carry bits. At the final O step, the new leading
front symbol is3+5=8 independently of r and of the last unknown carry. The
preceding value s lies in {4,5,6,7,8,9,10,11,13}; it is irrelevant to renewal.
Each row follows directly from decimal quotient and remainder at the displayed
symbols and the uniform-tail carry. In particular, p+4<=7 forces the carry
entering the following7 to be0, so the fifth row ends in14 rather than15.
The new terminal8 satisfies exactly the original hypothesis. Induction renews
the front for arbitrarily many six-step blocks, advancing4 per block. QED.

This is an actual all-time front mechanism in the stated infinite-bulk model,
not an empirical speed estimate. Its speed is2/3 column per decimal step.
For symmetric fronts around a finite uniform central gap, six alternating steps
add only3 total physical digits while the two fronts consume8 positions. Hence
an isolated central gap contracts by5 per six-step block until the fronts
interact. Collision, rather than propagation, becomes the missing renewal step.
The front theorem alone does not show that collision regenerates suitable fronts.

3. Formal and computational verification

resume_history_search_front.lean defines the exact local suffix update, allowing
an arbitrary incoming carry from the omitted left region. On an overflow it
forgets the first output position, whose companion digit lies outside the known
suffix. It preserves every determined later position and trims only the uniform
tail. The theorem CarryFront.terminal_eight_front_renews verifies all64 possible
six-bit carry histories: the resulting suffix has length2, ends in8, has uniform
value6, and has displacement4.

Lean4.19.0 checks the theorem using kernel decide. `#print axioms` reports no
axioms. No sorry, custom axiom, or native_decide is used. This formalizes the
local update certificate; it has not been linked in Lean to the main arbitrary
integer/digit formalization. The local interpretation above is proved by the
ordinary column-addition rule, and the Python implementation was independently
compared with2000 actual decimal additions with varied surrounding digits.

Commands:
  lean-runtime/lean resume_history_search_front.lean
  python3 resume_history_search_front.py
  python3 resume_history_search_reconstruct.py

The existence question still requires both a finite exterior sustaining the
assumed clock and a compatible central collision/renewal mechanism. Neither is
asserted by these files.

4. Finite collision certificates

The same Lean file now includes two additional kernel-checked local center
certificates, each universal over64 incoming-carry histories:

  gap_four_collision:
    The window8,6,6,6,6,8 becomes a length-three symmetric window(s,10,s)
    after six N/O steps in the exact cropped-center frame.

  gap_five_contact:
    The window8,6,6,6,6,6,8 becomes a length-four window(s,8,8,s).

Both theorems, like the front theorem, report no axioms. They do not assert
that the collision recreates a uniform gap or reflected renewable fronts.
The general gap-contraction proof and fuller collision classification are
being recorded separately by the collision investigation.
