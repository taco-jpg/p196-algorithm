PERIOD-FOUR DECIMAL LYCHREL CONSTRUCTIONS: EXACT REFINEMENT
13 October 2026 -- continuation of the existence search

Status: no decimal Lychrel witness or existence proof was obtained.
This note describes a completed computer-assisted exclusion of one bounded
construction class. The concrete graph calculation is not formalized in
Lean. It is not claimed to be a publication-ready solution or a new result
in the mathematical literature.

1. THE CONSTRUCTION SOUGHT

Let T(n)=n+reverse(n). Write a positive decimal numeral's digits from low
to high as d_i and put q_i=d_i+d_(L-1-i). The symmetric word q determines
T(n) exactly. A valid word has coefficients0..18, positive outer coefficient,
and an even central coefficient when its length is odd.

The target shape is

  q = P ++ take(M,repeat(Q)) ++ reverse(P),

where P has a fixed length k, Q has spatial period dividing4 but is not
constant, and M>=16. The exterior P, core Q, and core-length residue may
change at every addition. No temporal period is prescribed. If an exact
closed safe cycle of these finite data existed, growing M would give an
all-length inductive family and an actual finite decimal Lychrel witness.

This pass sought such a cycle and found none for1<=k<=4. It does not
exclude k>=5, exteriors whose length changes with time, larger spatial
periods, or nonperiodic centers.

2. EXACT BULK AND OUTER EQUATIONS

Write s=M mod8. Reflection requires Q_j=Q_((s-1-j) mod4). Because Q is
nonconstant, at least one coefficient differs from9. Carry propagation
around a period therefore has a unique stable stream in each direction:

  A_(j+1)=floor((Q_j+A_j)/10),
  B_j=floor((Q_j+B_(j+1))/10).

Indices on Q,A,B are modulo4. At an even center, A_(s/2)=B_(s/2).
At an odd center, Q_((s-1)/2) is even and
A_((s-1)/2)=B_((s+1)/2). These are the conditions at an actual finite
center, rather than arbitrary periodic boundary conditions.

Set l_j=(Q_j+A_j) mod10 and u_j=(Q_j+B_(j+1)) mod10.
If h is the numerical overflow bit, the deep interior evolves by

  Q'_j=l_j+u_(j-h),            s'=(s+h) mod8.

The complete nonconstant bulk graph has15,768 states. Its recurrent
components containing an overflow edge contain1,168 states.

For an exterior prefix P=(p_0,...,p_(k-1)), propagate the ordinary low
carry from0 toward the center. Independently choose either possible carry
at its high-side inner end, propagate toward the leading column, and
obtain h. Pair the produced digits in the same alignment when h=0, or
in the one-position-shifted alignment when h=1. Both inner carries are
allowed, so this is a necessary-condition graph until the core is joined.

3. REFINEMENT RETAINS EVERY RECURRENT POSSIBILITY

Intersect the bulk and exterior graphs by requiring their overflow bits
to agree. Every expanding return family must project into a strongly
connected component containing an overflow edge. When another outer pair
is added, project by dropping that pair. Any directed cycle projects into
one component of the preceding graph, with the same positive total growth.
Thus states outside the preceding positive recurrent components can be
removed before constructing the next level. This is an exact reduction
for the existence of expanding cycles; it is not a sampling argument.

4. JOINING THE ENTIRE EXTERIOR: COMPLETED ALL-PERIOD EXCLUSION

For each surviving state at width k, now treat P as the ENTIRE exterior.
Form the actual finite q word, perform ordinary decimal column addition,
and test both of the following exact conditions:

  * The resulting q word has an exterior of length k and the predicted
    nonconstant period-four core of length M+h.
  * The actual numerical output is not a palindrome.

This is more general than imposing a stable carry immediately on entry
to the core: boundary carry defects are allowed if their final pair sums
still have the required shape. The full numerical addition decides this.

Why this covers every M>=16 in its residue class:
The first non9 coefficient resets an incoming carry, so any discrepancy
from the periodic carry stream disappears within four core positions.
Afterwards the carried digits repeat with period4. Increasing M by8
preserves the phase reaching the high exterior. Reflection can move an
endpoint discrepancy by at most one position when h=1. Thus all possible
shape failures occur within five positions of an end, or within one
complete interior period. A core of length16+s,0<=s<8, includes every
one of those cases, with disjoint boundary regions. Adding eight positions
only inserts copies of the already checked interior pattern.

Palindrome safety is length-independent too: the existing exact one-step
criterion depends on the set of q coefficients and the outer coefficient,
not their multiplicities. Each core of length at least16 contains all four
core phases. The program additionally compares the complete outcomes at
M=16+s and M=24+s at every tested state; the independent checker also uses
M=32+s. These comparisons check the implementation, while the carry-reset
argument supplies the all-length justification.

The resulting full-exterior graph is functional. Edges between distinct
necessary recurrent components can be omitted because no cycle uses them.
The complete results are:

It also excludes an infinite avoiding orbit that remains in this model
with fixed k<=4 and nonconstant cores at every sufficiently late step.
Its state space (P,Q,s) is finite, so an infinite trajectory repeats a

edge, contradicting the complete graph result. This does not imply that
such an orbit must encounter a palindrome: it may leave the specified
periodic-core shape instead.
