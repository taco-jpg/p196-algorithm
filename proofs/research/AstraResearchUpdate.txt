MAIN COMPLETED RESULT

Write M = 2^alpha 5^beta q with gcd(q,10)=1. Put

  k = max(1,alpha,beta), r = ord_q(10), B(M) = 2k+4qr,

using r=1 if q=1. For EVERY L >= B(M), a residue a modulo M contains
an ordinary positive L-digit palindrome exactly when

  not(10 divides M and 10 divides a),
  and, if L is even, gcd(M,11) divides a.

A complete elementary construction and an independent adversarial proof
audit establish the universal quantifiers. The proof is in
ExactLengthPalindromes.pdf and astra_modular_fixed_modulus_theorem.md.

Consequently, no nonempty forward-invariant palindrome-free set can have
membership determined solely by exact decimal length and one fixed residue
modulo M. Allowed residues may depend arbitrarily on length. This excludes
a certificate class; it does not say that an orbit hits the palindrome
constructed in its residue class.

The note also proves that the reversal residue is determined by value
residue and length exactly for M dividing 99, and that ordinary reverse-add
has no continuous extension to the 10-adic integers. Residue determinacy
does not determine the next digit length. Relevant earlier palindrome
distribution theorems are cited; publication novelty has not been established.

TEN TASKS AND WHAT EACH CONTRIBUTED

1. Substitution and multiple periodic blocks
   Proved that fixed-time, finite-alphabet, nonerasing morphic certificates
   of linear width growth reduce, after phases and a transient, to finitely
   many growing periodic-word blocks. Built an exact family checker with
   binary positive controls. Found 28 exact 24-step center components;
   a complete filtered enumeration of 5,706,688 width-six exterior states
   found no compatible loop for the specified clock and bulk model.
   Larger or different models remain open. Files: astra_substitution_*.

2. Correlated digit languages
   Derived all-length carry-complement identities and correlated-family
   branches. Two explicit counterexamples show that marked non-neutral
   defects can disappear. Eight Lean theorems and 35,818 literal-integer
   family/transition checks preserve this result. No closed safe language
   was found. Files: astra_languages_*.

3. Counting and inverse image depth
   For every m>=0,k>=1, constructed an overflow fiber of width
   L=2(m+k+7) with at least 2^m preimages, each with two earlier safe
   iterates and k later safe steps. A complete 16-state correction table
   proves the arbitrary-length construction; 2,555 numeric seeds were
   checked. Fixed incoming depth does not reduce the exponential fiber
   rate below two per mirrored pair. Growing incoming depth is unresolved.
   Files: astra_counting_*.

4. Spectral and algebraic invariants
   Proved that the ideal generated by all actual carry defects is
   (x^2-20x+1,9(x+1)) in Z[x,x^-1]. Its quotient is
   (Z/9)[epsilon]/(epsilon^2) times Z/22. Explicit palindrome-producing
   words fill the retained physical classes. An independent proof audit
   and 85,447 polynomial checks support this precisely scoped obstruction.
   Carry-sensitive invariants are not excluded. Files: astra_spectral_*.

5. Modular, valuation, and completion methods
   Proved the main exact-length theorem, invariant-set obstruction,
   reversal-residue classification, and 10-adic discontinuity. Constructed
   2,512 finite palindromes in selected classes, including lengths up to
   1,288,411 digits. The valuation/carry persistence needed for a positive
   construction remains open. Files: astra_modular_*.

6. Primary literature and transfer ideas
   Audited purported decimal proofs and established the prior-art boundary
   for exact-length residue saturation. Derived a finite piecewise-affine
   transition theorem for multivariate run lengths and checked a two-
   parameter decimal identity at 10,000 pairs. No closed Presburger domain
   was found. Files: astra_literature_*.

7. Independent proof audit
   Audited the full exact-length theorem, no-invariant corollary, modulo-99
   classification, spectral ideal/quotient, and sharp time-six theorem.
   Found no substantive gap; tightened the finite-prime/cap qualification
   and the distinction between residue and full state updates.
   Files: astra_audit_*.

8. Lean formalization
   Added the 25 unconditional modular/orbit theorems described above,
   compiled them, and audited all axiom dependencies. The root integrated
   them and the eight language lemmas and compiled the complete file.
   Files: astra_lean_* and astra_root_axiom_audit.*.

9. Base transfer by literal blocks
   Exhausted all 918,274 modular-eligible stationary binary-to-decimal
   codes of block widths one through four. Exact periodic-word arithmetic
   excludes every eventual tail of the specified binary family, even
   with variable positive return times and intermediate palindromes.
   Uniform parameter threshold 13 suffices. Literal decimal replays and
   binary/quaternary positive controls agree. This computation is not
   Lean-certified; other encoders are not excluded.
   Files: astra_base_transfer_*.

10. Moving boundaries and renewable defects
    Derived an exact growing-wall transfer and a difference-wall model
    that can continue through overlap. The positive closure obligation is
    preservation of a specified carried-digit ordering. Its failure is
    measured by missing carries and a digit-sum increase. A possible
    growing-parameter renewal requires a separate rebalancing theorem;
    no such all-time theorem or finite witness was obtained.
    Files: astra_moving_boundary_*.

THE UNRESOLVED STEP

The required object remains one positive FINITE decimal seed whose orbit
avoids palindromes at EVERY time. A family of seeds surviving longer and
longer finite intervals does not establish this. Neither a nonempty local
component nor a solver timeout establishes global closure. The present
results narrow specified approaches and provide checked tools; they do
not constitute a proof or disproof of decimal Lychrel existence.
