INDEPENDENT AUDIT: THE FINITE MOVING-FRONT CRITERION
Date: 2026-10-14

Reviewed: CONDITIONAL_FRONT_THEOREM.md, STATUS.md, and the suffix_step
operation in front_language.py. This was a mathematical and source-reading
audit. No search, experiment, or source modification was performed.

VERDICT

The stated hypotheses would justify the claimed actual finite-seed orbit
induction, including the boundary, carry, center, and safety obligations.
However, those hypotheses are inconsistent for the stated local operation:
no nonempty finite certificate closed under both overflow values can satisfy
the upper potential. This is an all-K structural obstruction, independent
of the searched motifs, periods, safety conditions, or finite search limits.
Thus the implication is sound but vacuous for this certificate format.

1. THE EXACT PER-EDGE OBSTRUCTION

Write s_v=|S_v|, b=j-s_v, j'=j+Delta, and b'=j'-s_u.
The local operation starts its known output at b+h. After removing the
matching periodic tail, it keeps a suffix of the remaining known prefix.
Consequently it never retains a coefficient before b+h. More precisely,
if D is the number of known prefix coefficients discarded by the K cutoff,
then D>=0 and

    b' = b+h+D,
    Delta-s_u+s_v = h+D >= h.

This identity also covers an empty retained suffix. It is directly visible
in suffix_step: ns=start+h; newcut=ns+len(out); then out=out[-K:].

For a directed cycle C, suffix lengths telescope. Therefore

    sum_C Delta >= sum_C h,
    sum_C (2*Delta-h) = sum_C h + 2*sum_C D >= sum_C h.

The upper potential requires the same cycle sum to be <=0. Hence every
cycle in any graph satisfying that potential must have h=0 and D=0 on
every edge. In particular, no cycle may contain an overflow.

But every vertex of the proposed finite nonempty graph has an outgoing
h=1 edge (indeed one for each incoming carry). Fix either incoming carry
and repeatedly follow such edges. Finiteness forces a directed cycle made
entirely of h=1 edges. Its weight is at least its positive edge count,
contradicting the upper potential. This proves nonexistence for every K
and p. The lower potential, center constraints, and safety witnesses cannot
repair this contradiction.

The same argument applies to a complete finite graph with a prescribed
alternating N,O clock: every clock-compatible cycle contains overflow edges.
Thus the positive-cycle outcome is structurally unavoidable for that exact
finite-window abstraction, not merely a feature of the sampled motifs.
State-cap outcomes remain state-cap outcomes; this argument does not claim
to reconstruct an unfinished reachable graph.

Equivalently, along any path,

    j_t-j_0 >= H_t+s_t-s_0,
    2*j_t-H_t >= 2*j_0+H_t-2*K.

A bounded upper potential would bound H_t. The retained window loses at
least one column on every overflow, while the center moves only half a
column. A bounded suffix cannot absorb that difference indefinitely.

2. CHECK OF THE CONDITIONAL ORBIT INDUCTION

Apart from the preceding inconsistency, no missing boundary hypothesis was
found in the written implication.

- Both actual branch variables are covered: h is the actual finite-word
  overflow and a is the actual low carry at b. The arithmetic is not forced
  to follow an independently prescribed clock or incoming-carry sequence.

- The upper potential and initial R margin telescope with the stated signs
  to L_t-2*j_t>=2p+4. The lower potential and initial U margin give b_t>=0.
  In fact the local operation already gives the stronger b_t>=b_0+H_t,
  so a constant lower potential would suffice for physical containment.

- Nonneutrality supplies a reset in every p consecutive periodic positions.
  For the entire left half of the finite word, the old central margin
  2p+4 leaves at least p reset positions before the opposite finite wake
  can influence the required high directional carry. The low scan is exact
  from its actual entering carry. On overflow, retaining only i>=b+1
  ensures the delayed high digit at i-1 lies in the known input window and
  excludes the special physical endpoint. Reflection supplies the other
  half. Computing the local edge using a longer artificial periodic window
  does not require that artificial window itself fit within the left half.

- The residues propagate the actual cut modulo p and actual length modulo
  2p. The stated reflection condition on B and even odd-center coefficient
  supply the finite symmetric center. The seed has a positive outer pair
  sum and hence the stated positive canonical decimal realization.

- A safety coefficient in S is physically retained, and a coefficient in B
  occurs because the central band contains a full period. A coefficient
  greater than 9 and unequal to 11 excludes both exact palindrome-predecessor
  classes. This proves nonpalindromicity of every successor, so n_1 rather
  than an unrestricted n_0 is the proposed Lychrel witness.

3. LIMITS

This is not a nonexistence proof for decimal Lychrel numbers, arbitrary
moving-front constructions, correlated wake languages, or a redesigned
local operation that can recover earlier known columns. It concerns the
specified operation that starts known output at b+h and thereafter only
discards information, together with a finite state set and the stated
upper potential. Merely increasing K, changing motifs or spatial periods,
or adding the lower potential cannot remove this obstruction.
