COMPLETED UNIFORM DECIMAL DIGIT-SUM ESCAPE
14 October 2026

The actual theorem
------------------
Let T(n)=n+reverse10(n), and let S(n) be the ordinary decimal digit sum.
Define

  E(0)=0, E(1)=1,
  E(M)=5*(2*(M-1)*E(M-1)+2*M-1) for M >= 2.

For every positive integer n and every B,s in the natural numbers,

  there exists t with s <= t <= s+E(2B) and S(T^t(n)) > B.

The bound depends only on B. It does not depend on n, the initial width,
or the chosen starting time s. No initial digit-sum bound is needed.
Iteration is continued after a palindrome, exactly as defined in the
existing reverse-and-add development. The special case n=196 is included.

Principal declarations:
  DecimalProfile.sharpEscapeBound_eq
  DecimalProfile.sharp_uniform_escape
  DecimalUniformEscape.digitSum_uniform_escape_sharp
  DecimalUniformEscape.digitSum_uniform_window_sharp
  DecimalUniformEscape.digitSum_uniform_window_196_sharp

This is exactly the manuscript's E_10 recurrence. For example E(2)=25,
E(3)=525, E(4)=15785. The earlier, coarser U recurrence and its proved
declarations are retained unchanged as separate results.

What was completed
------------------
The earlier manuscript had a written uniform theorem, but its checked
quantitative deadline depended on the initial decimal width. The new
modules close that particular formal gap. The proof is concrete throughout:

1. SharedProfile defines finite, positive anchored coefficient profiles,
   with ordinary two-direction decimal normalization. The terminal forward
   carry is retained. No symmetry or dynamics axiom is imposed.
2. ProfileDynamics proves preservation of the profile conditions, support
   growth, and propagation of the zero-run bound without symmetry.
3. ProfileIsolation proves exact carry splitting across a zero gap,
   finite-time autonomy of the left component, and nonvanishing of the
   right component. Hence the left mass is strictly smaller throughout
   the isolated time interval.
4. UniformEscape inducts on the mass ceiling. A long initial gap invokes
   the smaller-mass induction hypothesis; otherwise controlled gaps and
   bounded mass imply bounded width, contradicting the support clock.
5. DecimalEmbedding proves that padding the actual symmetric pair-sum
   word intertwines the old numeric representation with the new profile
   map, at every iterate. Its mass is exactly twice the ordinary digit sum.
6. UniformDecimal discharges all profile hypotheses and proves the numeric
   theorem and its every-time-window form.
7. SharpProfileBounds uses positive endpoints to prove the stronger width
   bound L <= M+(M-1)*K and improves the zero-gap propagation hypotheses.
8. SharpUniformEscape proves the mass-one base directly and the exact E
   recurrence, with an unconditional concrete profile escape theorem.
9. SharpUniformDecimal transports that result to ordinary decimal integers,
   every starting time, and the special case 196.

Verification
------------
The initial six new modules and their unchanged standalone dependency were
rebuilt in a fresh directory with Lean 4.19.0. The three sharp refinements
were then compiled against that same fresh build after all seven existing
source hashes were checked unchanged. Every exit code was zero. Separate
post-build axiom audits also passed. The final theorems depend only on

  propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

There are no admitted proofs, custom axioms, or native computation oracle
dependencies. In particular Lean.ofReduceBool is absent. The finite data
checks used in the earlier boundary-obstruction project are not imported
into this proof. This verification concerns the actual stated decimal
theorem, not only a conditional abstract induction.

The complete source hashes, compiler output, and exact audits are recorded
in verification.json, root_clean_compile.log, sharp_verification.json,
and sharp_root_compile.log. The first pair records the initial coarse
result; the second records its completed sharp refinement. Reproduce all
ten source modules from this directory using Lean 4.19.0:

  python3 compile_all.py /absolute/path/to/lean

The runtime, generated .olean files, and temporary build directory need
not be distributed. All source dependencies are included in the archive.

Limits
------
The manuscript's exact decimal recurrence is formalized. Its general-base
extension and the sharper decimal asymptotic constant 3/4 are still written,
separately audited results; they are not proved by these Lean declarations.

The theorem does not prove decimal Lychrel existence, the 196 conjecture,
or pointwise digit-sum divergence. It applies equally to orbits which hit
palindromes. No new finite palindrome-free seed was supplied by this proof.
No claim of publication priority or external peer review is made.
