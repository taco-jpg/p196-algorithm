BOUNDARY RESEARCH: 14 October 2026

Target and current limit
------------------------
The target remains existence of a finite positive decimal integer whose
ordinary reverse-and-add orbit contains no palindrome, preferably196.
No such integer or all-time avoidance proof has been obtained. The new
results below are supporting exclusion theorems, not a solution of that
target. Publication novelty and external peer review are not established.

A completed all-width, all-period obstruction
--------------------------------------------
The previous checkpoint left open whether a sufficiently wide exterior
could complete the proposed936-type finite families. That gap is now
resolved negatively for the specified alternating8/17 branch and all
twelve phase/orientation/complement tails generated by(9,3,6) and
(9,12,15). The exclusion covers every finite exterior width and every
positive temporal period. Increasing the width or temporal period of
this same exterior construction cannot repair it.

The proof constructs eight finite edge-labeled graphs, containing1,940
vertices and7,442 edges in total. Their cyclic languages contain the bulk
targets and are closed under taking one spatial predecessor. A supplied
natural-number rank and vertex projection certify this closure on every
raw predecessor edge. The forced exterior letter is r=(0,1,1,7), code41.
Every r-labeled edge strictly increases a separate rank, so no graph can
accept a nonempty cyclic word consisting entirely of r. This contradicts
the cut immediately after the mandatory first8/17 column.

The discovery graphs satisfy G8=G5 exactly, including their numbered edge
lists. The proof certificate only needs the local rank/projection checks;
it does not require trusting the SCC algorithms used in discovery.
A further small certificate shows that even the temporal factor rrr is
absent. The rank values for this observation are only0,1,2.

There is also a fixed-width nonperiodic consequence. If prefix width k
were valid forever against this periodic bulk, the finite state consisting
of temporal phase and the k coefficients would eventually repeat. This
would produce a periodic exterior, already excluded. Thus an indefinitely
valid exterior of this same fixed width and bulk is impossible even
without an initially assumed temporal period. A boundary whose necessary
width grows, or a different bulk or clock, is outside the conclusion.

Independent checks
------------------
The discovery and independent replay use different SCC procedures.
The coordinating audit, root_sofic_audit.py, imports neither search nor
checker code. It independently reconstructs the inverse options from
ordinary decimal divmod arithmetic, reconstructs all52,520 raw edges,
checks every rank/projection condition, checks all root ranks, and
verifies all twelve primitive target words. It passed. No SCC procedure
is used by this audit. All checks here are internal computer-assisted
checks, not external human peer review.

Before the smaller all-period certificate was found, a separate exact
reverse certificate solved the24-phase problem. It contains39,194
temporal-rotation orbits and was also independently replayed. This older
finite certificate is retained for cross-checking and provenance.

Constructive branches: completed bounded searches
------------------------------------------------
The 525 front-and-center construction has not supplied a finite exterior.
Its bulk has primitive period 6 in ordinary steps. The earlier statement
that 72 is the first period not excluded by the modulo-nine test referred
only to multiples of 24. Allowing all compatible cap return times, 36 is
the first such period not excluded. This is an unexcluded possibility,
not an existence result.

Exact inverse-language searches reached depth 12 for 525 and its
complements, and depth 9 for all 334 catalogued recurrent length-three
motifs under the alternating clock. No root cycle was found. The next
layers exceeded the unchanged two-million raw-edge limit; arbitrary
width remains UNKNOWN. All 92 saved forward/backward bisimulation
quotients were independently audited, including power lifting and actual
inverse strips replayed with ordinary column arithmetic. See
reverse525/STATUS_AND_POWER_LIFT_PROOF.txt.

Joining forward boundary languages to these backward bulk languages
strengthens the finite-width exclusions. The completed joins cover
exterior widths through 17 for 525/complements, and through 14 for all
334 motifs. Temporal periods are unrestricted. There were 172 completed
empty cyclic intersections and 35 capped intersections, each recorded
as UNKNOWN. Forward depth 9 exceeded the two-million raw-vertex limit.
Eight complete forward images and 30 quotient operations were audited;
eight reconstructed strips passed independent ordinary-carry replay.
The strongest two empty product graphs were independently reconstructed
and checked by sink deletion. See forward_join/ for the exact proof,
results, reconstruction code, and limits.

Bisimulation does not preserve the language of closed words at a fixed
length. These searches use its soundness for cyclic words up to positive
powers; witness reconstruction explicitly lifts the temporal period.
Neither a capped graph nor the absence of a witness within the completed
widths supplies an arbitrary-width theorem. No new finite decimal seed
has been obtained from these constructive branches.
