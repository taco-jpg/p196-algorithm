Uniform-core existence attempt: exact period obstruction and clock screening
===========================================================================

Status
------
No decimal Lychrel number has been proved to exist. No witness or all-time
nonpalindromic orbit is supplied by this work. The new unconditional results
below exclude specific proposed constructions and prescribed overflow clocks.
They are computer-assisted integer-arithmetic results, not complete Lean
formalizations. The main Lean file already proves the outer-digit potential
inequality used below; the new graph certificates have separate reproducible
Python and C++ checkers.

1. The old period-72 restriction was conditional
----------------------------------------------
The pair-sum bulk 16 has phase cycle
  16 -> 14 -> 10 -> 2 -> 4 -> 8 -> 16.
The corresponding actual bulk-digit cycle is
  8 -> 7 -> 5 -> 1 -> 2 -> 4 -> 8.
The old Brown72 search imposed alternating maximal growth. Its outer three
pair sums then follow an eight-cycle, which combines with the six-phase bulk
and modulo-9 restriction to require a return period divisible by 72.

That argument did NOT rule out slower clocks. In particular period 24 with
nine new digits, rather than twelve, is arithmetically compatible. We pursued
this broader positive construction in the present pass.

2. Equality classification for the existing growth bound
--------------------------------------------------------
Let q_i be mirrored decimal digit sums, in outside-to-inside order, with
1 <= q_0 <= 18 and 0 <= q_i <= 18. Let h be the step's overflow bit. The
existing Lean declaration CarryHistory.outer_potential_step proves
  P(q'_0) - P(q_0) <= 1 - 2h,
where the integer-valued P is CarryHistory.outerPotential. In a periodic
exterior of period p and total growth g, telescoping yields 2g <= p.

For equality, every step has zero potential slack. The exact transition of
three outer pair sums depends only on those three values and one unknown
incoming carry from farther inside. Both possible inner carries, zero and
one, are allowed. This is an overapproximation of every numerical orbit.
The 6,498 possible triples and 12,996 carry inputs were exhaustively checked.
Every edge has nonnegative slack. Repeatedly deleting sources or sinks from
the zero-slack graph leaves precisely the directed eight-cycle

  (8,16,16) -> (17,13,14) -> (8,12,9) -> (17,5,0)
     -> (8,13,5) -> (17,6,11) -> (8,14,8) -> (17,8,17)
     -> (8,16,16).

Any directed closed walk of zero total slack consists entirely of zero-slack
edges, and every vertex on that walk survives source/sink deletion. Therefore
any periodic exterior attaining g=p/2 has period divisible by eight. This
classification is new here; the general growth bound itself was already
proved in the main Lean development.

Output: exist_uniform_critical_results.json.

3. Complete period-24, gain-9 exterior exclusion
-----------------------------------------------
The scalar outer sum a=q_0 ranges from 1 through 18. If b in {0,1} is the
incoming carry from the interior, set
  h = floor((a+b)/10),
  low = a mod 10,
  high = (a+b) mod 10,
  a' = low+1 if h=1, and low+high if h=0.
Every numerical reverse-and-add step obeys this transition. Enumerating ALL
length-24 closed walks with exactly nine h=1 transitions, then identifying
cyclic rotations of their binary clocks, gives exactly 1,077 clocks.
The Python and independent C++ implementations produce identical catalogs.

For each clock, group its steps into nine segments. A segment of length r
has r-1 nonoverflows followed by one overflow. A spatial exterior state
records, for each segment:
  * the left digit at the segment's start;
  * the carry bits leaving that digit towards the outside;
  * the carry bits entering the right digit from the outside.
At the exterior head, the left start digits are all one, left carry masks
have only the segment's final bit set, and right input carry masks are zero.

One inward spatial transition is calculated solely by ordinary decimal
addition. At each step with current paired digits a,b, the left and right
sums are a+b+c_left and a+b+c_right. Their remainders modulo ten are the
next digits; their quotients are the next carries. Each unknown inward carry
is tried as zero or one, and the prescribed outward carry is checked.
Across a segment ending in overflow, the new leading digit shifts the left
spatial index by one, so the segment-output left digits are cyclically
rotated by one position in the next state. The right digits must match around
the entire temporal cycle. These equations exhaust every possible finite
exterior with that clock; they do not make a probabilistic assumption.

Breadth-first reachability was run until every queue was empty. Initial tight
caps were explicitly marked incomplete; all such cases were subsequently
finished. An independent C++ implementation of the decimal equations matched
the Python graph counts in every completed case and resolved the final two
large cases. The entire 1,077-case replay was then repeated after adding an
explicit directed-acyclicity check.

Final certificate totals:
  clocks:                 1,077
  sum of reachable states: 987,037
  sum of reachable edges:  985,960
  maximum spatial depth:       10
  incomplete graphs:            0
  graphs with a directed cycle: 0
  reachable uniform targets:    0

Each individual graph has edges = states-1, and the separate topological
check removes every vertex. The conclusion is stronger than failure to find
a uniform target: none of these exterior graphs has any reachable spatial
cycle. Thus no arbitrarily wide temporally periodic exterior exists for a
period-24, gain-9 clock. In particular no finite exterior can connect to an
indefinitely repeated spatial bulk under such a return. This does not rule
out changing/growing exteriors or aperiodic temporal behavior.

For the uniform six-phase bulk, each target state has an exact spatial
self-loop, independently checked by the decimal equations. A uniform-run
return family with arbitrarily long middle runs would give an arbitrarily
long exterior path, reaching such a target. The acyclic finite graphs rule
this out at EVERY exterior width, not merely up to a search cutoff.

Reproduction (Python standard library and a C++17 compiler):
  g++ -O3 -std=c++17 exist_uniform_spatial_check.cpp -o exist_uniform_spatial_check
  python3 exist_uniform_period24_verify.py
Output: exist_uniform_period24_verified.json.

The verification script compares the independent C++ clock enumeration and
all spatial graph counts against the preserved Python results. The final
checker itself imports no old decimal-transition implementation. The C++
source includes --clock-catalog mode and a parameterized spatial checker.

4. Consequence: uniform six-phase pumps need period at least 30
---------------------------------------------------------------
Consider a family with a fixed finite prefix and suffix and an arbitrarily
long constant middle digit d, where d is in {1,2,4,5,7,8}. Suppose that after
p reverse-and-add steps the same family returns with g>0 additional middle
digits. The prefix/suffix may vary with the temporal phase, but are fixed
at each phase. Any finite unequal boundary lengths can be normalized by
absorbing a bounded number of middle digits into the boundaries.

Far from the boundaries, ordinary addition forces the six-phase digit cycle,
so 6 divides p. Modulo nine, reverse-and-add multiplies the number by two.
As 2^p=1 modulo nine, adding g middle digits changes the digit sum by d*g,
which must be zero modulo nine. Since d is coprime to nine, 9 divides g.
The exact outer-potential telescoping above gives 2g <= p.

For p=6 or 12 these conditions are incompatible. For p=18 they force g=9
and equality in the growth bound; the eight-cycle classification excludes
that period. For p=24 they force g=9, excluded by the complete exterior
certificate. Hence every such positive-growth return family has p>=30.

This applies to the q=(8,16^k,8) route after its first numerical addition,
whose middle is in the same six-phase cycle. It does not exclude an arbitrary
Lychrel number, a nonuniform spatial bulk, or a nonperiodic construction.

5. The new nonuniform period-four centers fail their exterior clocks
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The geometry pass produced eleven locally recurring period-four pair-sum
bulk cycles, with eight distinct overflow clocks. Each clock was screened
without imposing its bulk: the exact exterior graphs closed at depth one,
with 129 or 257 states. Python and independent C++ graph counts agree for
all eight. Files: exist_uniform_period4_screen.py/results.json.

A stronger, separate check keeps just two outer pair sums and the temporal
phase of the proposed clock. Its transition is computed directly from the
four affected decimal digits, allowing either inner carry at each step.
All eight products are acyclic. Their longest matching runs have respectively
15,17,31,35,27,20,42,31 steps. Thus NONE of those clocks can persist forever,
even if the center grows or is temporally aperiodic. The stated numerical result assumes at least four decimal digits, so the
two outer digit pairs are distinct. The model deliberately allows both inner
carries and therefore overapproximates all numerical dynamics in that domain.

For example the primitive clock (O,N,O,N,N), up to rotation, cannot match
16 consecutive steps. Its phase product has 1,710 states and is a DAG with
maximum path length 15. This pinpoints why the valid local center recurrence
does not extend to an actual infinite decimal orbit.

Reproduction:
  python3 exist_uniform_period4_screen.py
  python3 exist_uniform_clock_obstruction.py
The latter checker contains standalone integer transition equations. Its
684 possible two-pair/carry inputs were cross-checked against the separate
low-to-high/high-to-low digit-loop implementation in exist_uniform_outer.py.

6. Final positive pass with exact two-pair exterior control
---------------------------------------------------------
The geometry pass then intersected the recurrent nonuniform period-four
bulk with the exact two-pair exterior model. It produced representative
cycles from 25 positive components, using four distinct overflow clocks.
All four exact spatial exterior graphs were completed:

  temporal period / growth       states    edges     max depth   cycles
       24 / 8                       514       513        2          0
       24 / 8                     2,369     2,368        2          0
       24 / 8                       577       576        2          0
       35 / 16                  262,145   262,144        2          0

The first three counts were independently replayed in Python; all four used
the independently audited C++ arithmetic. The largest completed in about
2.16 seconds. No case remains capped. Files:
  exist_uniform_two_outer_screen.py
  exist_uniform_two_outer_screen_results.json

Thus those exact representative clocks cannot connect to any repeated
spatial bulk with the proposed temporal return. This statement does NOT
exclude every cycle in the 25 bulk components, longer temporal returns,
changing exteriors, or aperiodic evolution. Unlike the eight earlier clocks,
no separate indefinite-clock obstruction is claimed for these four.

7. Remaining scope
------------------
Uniform pumps with periods >=30, wider boundaries, other cycles of the
nonuniform bulk graph, larger spatial periods, and nonperiodic constructions
remain open within this work. The present exclusions are not a proof of
decimal Lychrel nonexistence and are not a substitute for the requested
existence proof.
