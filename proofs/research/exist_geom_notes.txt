FREE-CLOCK UNIFORM AND PERIODIC-BULK CONSTRUCTIONS
13 October 2026

Status: no finite decimal Lychrel witness was found. These are exact
construction models, positive LOCAL bulk components, and bounded solver

No claim of novelty or publication priority is made.

1. Exact uniform-run construction criterion

Let T(n)=n+rev(n). Fix exterior width k, M=10^k, and decimal digits d_t.
Write a family as n_{t,m}=A_t || d_t^m || B_t, where A_t is a positive
k-digit prefix and B_t is a zero-padded k-digit suffix. Put
  epsilon_t=[d_t>=5], d_{t+1}=2*d_t-9*epsilon_t.
For d_0=8 the bulk cycle is 8,7,5,1,2,4; for d_0=6 it is 6,3.

The following are sufficient exact conditions for a one-step family map:
  low=B_t+rev_k(A_t), floor(low/M)=epsilon_t,
  B_{t+1}=low mod M,
  high=A_t+rev_k(B_t)+epsilon_t, h_t=floor(high/M).
If h_t=0, set A_{t+1}=high. If h_t=1, require high mod10=d_{t+1}
and set A_{t+1}=floor(high/10). Then for every m>=1,
  T(n_{t,m})=n_{t+1,m+h_t}.

Proof: the low exterior addition supplies exactly the stable carry required
by the constant digit block. Every middle digit therefore becomes d_{t+1}.
The high exterior receives epsilon_t. Without overflow the stated prefix
is direct; with overflow its low digit must match the middle digit, so that
digit extends the middle run and the remaining k digits form the prefix.
This is ordinary positional addition, with no approximation in m.

If the exterior digits and bulk phase return after p steps, their total
growth is g>0, and every phase has A_t!=rev_k(B_t), these identities prove
that every member is a decimal Lychrel number. This final conjunction has
NOT been found. The programs search it rather than assume it.

The digit-sum congruence forces 9 | d_0*g when p returns the bulk phase.
Thus d_0=8 requires g divisible by9; d_0=6 requires g divisible by3.
A further necessary modulo11 constraint is used optionally in the q model.
Every positive-growth closed family eventually has an even-length input,
whose reverse-add output is divisible by11; this divisibility persists.
After two full family returns the number has the same residue modulo11.
Consequently every phase is already divisible by11. For a uniform-q family
with folded exterior E and middle coefficient2*d, evaluating an odd-width
q word at10=-1 gives
  sum_i (-1)^i E_i + (-1)^k d = 0 (mod11).

Files:
  exist_geom_uniform_freeclock.py -- actual prefix/suffix digit SMT model.
  exist_geom_uniform_qclock.py -- smaller exact pair-sum SMT model.
Both use bounded unsigned 5-bit column sums; the largest sum is19, so there
is no bit-vector wraparound in a decimal column. SAT output, if obtained,
must pass literal integer checks at multiple middle lengths. UNSAT reports
are solver results for the indicated widths/periods, not Lean proofs.

Search outcomes:

The exact outcomes, including every timeout, are in the matching JSON files.
Separate work by the uniform-clock agent subsequently excludes all p24/g9

2. Unknown periodic spatial bulk with a free growth clock

The generalized family is a symmetric pair-sum word
  C_{t,m}=E_t ++ take(2*r*m+G_t, cycle(Q_t)) ++ reverse(E_t),
where Q_t has spatial period r, G_0=0 and G_{t+1}=G_t+h_t. A return
requires E_p=E_0, Q_p=Q_0, and g=G_p a positive multiple of2*r.

Two periodic carry streams A_j and B_j are retained:
  A_{j+1}=floor((Q_j+A_j)/10),
  B_j=floor((Q_j+B_{j+1})/10).
Indices are modulo r. The low and high digits are respectively
  l_j=Q_j+A_j-10*A_{j+1},
  u_j=Q_j+B_{j+1}-10*B_j.
Then the new periodic pair sums are
  N: Q'_j=l_j+u_j;
  O: Q'_j=l_j+u_{j-1}.

Crucially, the periodic word is constrained at its actual finite center:
  Q_j=Q_{G_t-1-j}.
If G_t is even, at j=G_t/2 require A_j=B_j. If G_t is odd, at
j=(G_t-1)/2 require Q_j even and A_j=B_{j+1}. These are the exact
empty-gap or central-column carry matching conditions. They eliminate
periodic patterns that cannot be the center of any finite numeral.

The exterior is an ordinary finite column-addition chain, with low incoming
carry0, low outgoing carry A_0, and high incoming carry B_0. Its high
outgoing carry is h_t. At O, the last high exterior output must equal
u_{r-1}; this lets one digit be absorbed into the periodic middle. The
next exterior q coefficients are l_i+u_i at N, or l_i+u_{i-1} at O,
with the new leading digit1 replacing u_{-1} at the very outside.

The exact next-palindrome criterion is imposed at every phase: at N some
q coefficient must be >=10; at O it is forbidden that the outer coefficient
is11 and all coefficients belong to{0,11}.

If all these finite constraints had a solution, positional addition would
give the family recurrence for every m>=1. Every symmetric q word with
coefficients0..18, even central coefficient if necessary, and positive
outer coefficient has a canonical decimal realization. Starting with such
a realization and applying T once would then supply a finite Lychrel
witness, by induction on the family recurrence. No satisfying complete
solution was found.

Implementation: exist_geom_periodic_qpump.py. A direct test compares the
derived local recurrence with ordinary integer reverse-and-add, not with
another SMT encoding. It accepted and verified5,314 eligible periodic-bulk
steps among20,000 random tests. Uniform digit equations were independently
verified on25,303 eligible prefix/suffix choices among100,000 samples, each
at middle lengths1,2,11. Reproduce with:
  python3 exist_geom_model_controls.py
These are implementation controls, not substitutes for an all-time proof.

The complete spatial-period4 central graph has15,768 nonuniform states,
30,000 retained edges, and7 strongly connected components with growth.
Intersecting these with the necessary scalar outer-pair graph still leaves
center-compatible examples of temporal periods20,24,28 and total growth8.
The data include the entire q-phase traces and exact growth clocks:

One exact example starts with periodic q=(18,6,6,18), runs clock
(O,N,O,N,N)^4, and returns after20 steps while adding8 positions. Its
necessary scalar outer sums cycle15,6,12,3,7. This is a genuine finite-
center-compatible bulk component, but it has no finite exterior connector:
the independently computed exterior E graph for segments(2,3)^4 closes
at257 states,256 edges, depth1, and has no directed cycle.

The corresponding actual digit/carry lift is saved in
exist_geom_period4_target.json. All four of its spatial edges were checked

larger-width tests contain both failures and explicit UNKNOWN outcomes.
Subsequent exact two-outer-pair/clock graphs independently exclude all eight
distinct clocks underlying these11 extracted cycles at arbitrary width.
These results do not exclude every possible local cycle.

4. Final all-period two-outer-pair product and focused completion attempt

To remove the scalar outer model's spurious clocks, the full period4 bulk
graph was intersected with the exact two-outer-pair necessary dynamics.
For q0 in1..18, q1 in0..18, the unknown inward high carry is either0 or1.
Ordinary decimal addition determines the next two pairs and the overflow.
All684 input/carry cases match a separately authored arithmetic routine.

Only the1,168 recurrent bulk states can participate in a pump cycle.
Their product with342 outer states contains399,456 states and565,592 edges.
It was completely decomposed into268,057 strongly connected components;
25 components retain an internal growth edge. A representative positive-
growth cycle was extracted from each of those25 components.

These representatives use four distinct return clocks:
  period24, growth8:  (100)^8;
  period24, growth8:  (100010001001)^2;
  period24, growth8:  101000100010101000100100;
  period35, growth16: 10110000101010100101010101010101010.
Here1 means overflow and0 means no overflow.

All four were given one focused exterior completion attempt. Their exact
exterior E graphs closed completely with, respectively,
  states: 514, 2369, 577, 262145;
  edges:  513, 2368, 576, 262144;
  maximum depth: 2 in every case;
  directed cycles: 0 in every case.
Thus none of these FOUR EXTRACTED CLOCKS can support an indefinitely
repeated spatial bulk behind a finite exterior. The first three counts
match independent Python and C++ implementations; the final262145-state
case was checked by the C++ implementation. No state/time cap remains in
these four recorded runs. See exist_uniform_two_outer_screen_results.json.

This is NOT an exclusion of all cycles in the25 product components, all
period4 constructions, all other carry clocks, or decimal Lychrel numbers.
The product components generally contain other cycles. They were not
exhausted by selecting one representative from each component.
