## Positive local weights cannot uniformly bound safe incoming fibers

The [folded-weight proof](../research/main_existence_attempt/continuation_pass/finite_seed/FOLDED_WEIGHT_OBSTRUCTION.md)
constructs actual same-width no-overflow fibers with two independent choices
per block. Summing this binary family gives an exact exponentially growing
incoming-weight ratio for every strictly positive coefficient-product weight,
including arbitrary width-only normalization. Buffering the blocks extends the
obstruction to every strictly positive translation-invariant product of fixed
finite-window potentials. The base of the exponential is strictly greater than
one for every such positive potential.

This excludes a uniform finite pointwise bound for the **safe incoming** operator
on the complete stated domain. It does not exclude position-dependent,
length-dependent, unbounded-range or vanishing weights, or estimates restricted
to deeper image supports. The all-parameter carry/factorization argument is a
written proof. Root independently reran the exact rational controls: 6,141
unbuffered and 1,270 buffered predecessors. No new Lean result is claimed here.

## Three exterior zero columns can occur only by time four

[ZeroExterior.lean](../research/main_existence_attempt/continuation_pass/lean/zero_exterior/ZeroExterior.lean)
proves an all-time statement about actual positive decimal orbits: if the
canonical pair word at time `t` begins `(p,0,0,0)` with positive `p`, then
`t<=4`. Every earlier step has no overflow, preserves the width and three zeros,
and doubles the outside coefficient. The time-four example from `10000000` is
sharp; iteration here continues after any palindrome. A checked overflow
counterexample shows that two zeros cannot replace three.

The source also proves no positive-time return to the same positive outside
symbol with this exterior. It does not cover `(p,p,0)` exteriors or arbitrary
orbits. The new module has 12 supporting theorem declarations and 3 definitions.
