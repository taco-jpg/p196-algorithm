# Base-10 cyclic forbidden factors and density-admissible necklaces, p<=32
**PAPER PROOF:** a time-periodic first-column root transition for a base-10 overflow word cannot contain the cyclic factor `1101` or `11001`. Re-root its period at the factor; the rotated initial x is positive because the first factor bit is 1 (forcing x>=b-1). The canonical-root equations are time-translation invariant, and the previously proved `11 0^r 1` obstruction gives b<=2^(r+1), contrary to b=10 for r=1 or 2.
**COMPUTED (exhaustive, no state cap):** for each period p=1..32, count density-admissible (2*G<=p) binary necklaces removed by the two cyclic factors, separately for all and primitive necklaces; complete counts in `calculation/decimal_frontier/lattice_formats/agent12_forbidden_necklaces_32.csv`.
At p=16, total 2,463; excluded 1,839; surviving 624. At p=24, total 405,986; excluded 362,755; surviving 43,231.
At p=32, total 76,501,983; excluded 73,118,415; surviving 3,383,568. Primitive p=32: total 76,499,520; excluded 73,116,576; surviving 3,382,944.
**Method:** 16-state de Bruijn trace with 1-weight tracks rooted cyclic words avoiding both factors, with density; Burnside and Mobius inversion yield all and primitive necklace counts. Independent direct enumeration matches every rooted/necklace count through p=16. Additionally, direct base-10 first-column enumeration checks every forbidden cyclic word of p<=12 has zero closed root trajectories.
**Reproduce:** `python3 calculation/decimal_frontier/lattice_formats/code/agent12_forbidden_necklaces.py --pmax 32 --brute-upto 16 --csv /tmp/check.csv`; compare the CSV with the committed compact data. Standard library only.
**Scope limit:** this is a necessary-condition prefilter, *not* a complete position-automaton/SCC census, a claim that any survivor is reachable, or a decimal Lychrel certificate.
