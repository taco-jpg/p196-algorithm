# E2P21FourColumnRigidity

**COMPUTED (complete finite graphs).** For the exact canonical-root base-10 position automaton with fixed time-rooted overflow stream `g=001001010101001010101` (p=21), every periodic exterior of **any temporal period `21k`, `k>=1`**, and exterior width at least 4 has its phase-zero lowest four pair coefficients in an explicit set of just **18** quadruples (out of 123,462 possible).
In fact, the first two coefficients must be `(3,5)`, and the third must be `6` or `8`; the 18 possibilities for the fourth are exactly `(3,5,6,x)` with `x∈{3,4,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14}` or `(3,5,8,x)` with `x∈{6,8,9,11,13,14,15,16}`. See `p21_head_phase0.txt` for complete K2/K3/K4 tables.
**Soundness argument:** At spatial position j, for each temporal step t choose the unknown incoming high carry `c_j∈{0,1}`, require `floor((x_j+c_j)/10)=H_j`, and compute `x'_j=(x_j+L_j)%10+[g_t?Y_j:(x_j+c_j)%10]`, `L_(j+1)=floor((x_j+L_j)/10)`, `H_(j+1)=c_j`, `Y_(j+1)=(x_j+c_j)%10`. Root initializes `L_0=0,H_0=g_t,Y_0=1` and x0 nonzero. Every actual 21k-periodic rooted exterior induces a directed closed walk in this time-labelled finite graph, hence its phase-zero head must lie on a cyclic SCC.
Exhaustive cyclic SCC counts at K=2: **7,182 vertices, 5,586 edges, one 71-state cyclic SCC, four phase-zero heads**; K=3: **136,458 vertices, 106,134 edges, one 115-state cyclic SCC, four phase-zero heads**; K=4: **2,592,702 vertices, 2,016,546 edges, one 427-state cyclic SCC, eighteen phase-zero heads**. Exact enumeration has no state/time-period cap.
Primary `p21_head_primary.cpp` enumerates bitmask carry edges and uses Kosaraju SCC; independent `p21_head_checker.cpp` uses recursive carries and Tarjan SCC. Their counts and every phase-zero row matched in local control runs. `run_p21_head.sh` also reproduces mandatory base-9/base-13 bauto5 controls and compares all 26 listed rows.
**Reproduce:** `bash calculation/decimal_frontier/lattice_formats/code/run_p21_head.sh`; all source, expected tables, controls and exact commands are under `calculation/decimal_frontier/lattice_formats/code/`.
**Not claimed:** an absorbing lattice reachable at any width, exclusion for widths below 4, arbitrary overflow words, a periodic centre, a decimal Lychrel number or the existence of any actual infinite integer orbit.
