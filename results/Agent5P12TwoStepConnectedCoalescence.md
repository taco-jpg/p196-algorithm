15 classes of palindromic pairwords admit a strictly connected three-site
symmetric bulk perturbation: every one of the three coefficients changes.

translation by 9 retaining a 30-cell centre margin, their first successor
pairwords differ, their second successor pairwords are identical, and their
actual reverse-and-add integers first become equal at step THREE.

not fixed by the proof. The exact reset-carry/local-cone proof and fifteen
motifs are in `reports/Agent5P12TwoStepConnectedCoalescence.md`.
