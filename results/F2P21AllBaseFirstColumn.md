Each coefficient trajectory gives exactly 2^9=512 distinct outgoing root cut-state edges (independent carry choices at nine overflows).
At a zero time `q_(t+1)=2q_t+c_t` with `q_t+c_t<b`; at a one time the following zero forces `q_t>=b`, hence `q_(t+1)=q_t-(b-1)`, with the overflow carry free. This exhausts the `bauto5` canonical position-zero equations, including cyclic closure and nonzero leading coefficients.
There are 12 zeros, so composing the 21 transitions gives **`4095 q_0 =1519(b-1)-R`**, where `0<=R<=4095` is the unique 12-bit weighted zero-carry word. The congruence leaves one R or, if `1519(b-1)≡0 (mod 4095)`, the two choices `R=0,4095`. Thus there are at most two coefficient trajectories for any base, with explicit reconstruction of the entire 21-step orbit.
Since gcd(1519,4095)=7, increasing b by 585 keeps R unchanged and increments q_t by the fixed vector **`D=(217,434,868,283,566,1132,547,1094,509,1018,433,866,281,562,1124,539,1078,493,986,401,802,217)`**. At every zero time `0<=D_t<585`; at every one time `585<=D_t<=1170`; the lifted path respects all coefficient and carry inequalities. Therefore each residue base in 4..588 controls all larger bases with the same residue mod 585.
The exact 585 residue representatives b=4..588 have one valid path each except b=586 with two; b=2,3 have none. This finite check, plus the proved lift, establishes the all-base statement; the special residue b≡1 (mod 585) has its first two-path representative at b=586.
