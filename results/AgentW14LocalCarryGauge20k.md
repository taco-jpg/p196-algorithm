# AgentW14LocalCarryGauge20k
- **PAPER PROOF:** An arbitrary-base local carry-gauge eraser for an overflowing even-length symmetric pairword: at any internal pair sites with residue in `1..b-2` modulo `b-1` and both neighbours not `b-1`, independently replace `x` by `x+(b-1)` or conversely. The *entire* successor pairword is unchanged. It is a local theorem, not a globally uniform-mask ansatz.
- **PAPER PROOF (conditional on finite replay):** The rooted p8/m20 decimal `B_0 ++ W_0^q ++ reverse(W_0)^q ++ reverse(B_0)` has `15q+18` independently switchable pair sites for every `q>=1`, giving that many bits of distinct canonical decimal seeds whose second ordinary-integer iterate is identical.
- **COMPUTED:** For `q=300,301` respectively, `2^4518` and `2^4533` distinct seeds merge by step 2 and are palindrome-free for steps `0..20000`. One deterministic nontrivial member of each family was independently replayed by GMP and a schoolbook decimal digit-vector; the original family member's 20,000-step baseline is also in `AgentN16DecimalProtectedExterior20k`.
- Verified minimum p8/m20 matching prefixes after time 0: `1494` and `1514`, respectively. Each witness starts with 12,078 or 12,118 digits. FNV64 hashes `f0e6842c418614a1`, `af6452dc8028a4a1`.
- Reproduce: `sh calculation/new_structures/agent-W14/run.sh` (GMP, C++17, Python 3). Source: `gauge_replay.cpp`, `independent_check.py`, data: `rows.txt` in that directory. Full proof: `reports/AgentW14LocalCarryGauge20k.md`.
- Not claimed: an infinite decimal Lychrel orbit, a renewing centre, a general-q 20,000-step survival theorem, a Lean result, or an exclusion theorem.
