# AA51 target

**Target T***: prove or refute a physically rooted decimal p12/m9 family with a complete 120-step return `T(q,k) -> T(q,k+5)` for all sufficiently large independent q,k, restoring the W9 exponent, seam, centre/phase, and non-palindrome guard; if the seam moves, specify and prove a closed seam family.

Route: BASE-TRANSFER variant 1, comparing bases 2, 4, 8, and 9. The missing all-time mechanism is not another finite replay: it is a self-reproducing boundary/carry state (or a finite cycle of such states) whose bulk-length increment is positive and whose defect budget does not decay.
