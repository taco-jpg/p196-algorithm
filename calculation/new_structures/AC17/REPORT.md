## Why the centre is genuinely eliminated

For symmetric decimal pair-coefficient word `P` with known rooted prefix length L, digits after normalizing `P` have `d_i=(P_i+c_i) mod 10`, `c_(i+1)=floor((P_i+c_i)/10)`, `c_0=0`; the new pairword pairs `d` against its high reverse, including the actual appended top digit if overflow gamma=1. An *unknown* carry entering the mirrored high suffix is either 0 or 1. At `P_i=9`, the two possibilities propagate; at **every** other `P_i in 0..18`, they coalesce after one digit. Thus if the prefix ends with exactly r consecutive coefficient-9 entries and has a non9 before them, the high terminal overflow `gamma` is forced and the next prefix is known, of length `L'=L-r-1+gamma` (the gamma=1 index shift is crucial). Its coefficients are obtained by applying schoolbook decimal normalization separately to the known low prefix (carry 0) and its reversed high suffix (carry 0 or 1, agreeing after the reset). This is an equality for **every possible** missing interior, not a probabilistic pruning rule.

Take L=280 at t=0. These coefficients include B8 W8^14, all J and a U prefix; neither the unspecified centre nor the U/centre boundary is present. The actual half length is at least `34+9*14+3+3*100=463`, so the two known 280-cell end zones are disjoint for every admissible C. As 120 steps progress, L only shrinks and physical length does not shrink; the induction remains applicable. The C++ program runs this carry recurrence for every J/U and proves the root B/W mismatch **without reading a single C coefficient**. A returning template necessarily restores B/W, so it is impossible.

For q/k growth, apply the paired-insertion proof in `calculation/new_structures/T12/NOTES.md` to the **phase-8 rotated** W rows and the unchanged `(1000)^30` overflow schedule. The phase-8 B/W protected-root loss is **92** (versus phase-0's 91); at q=14 the W insertion cut after two W9 copies at cW=52 still has `160-92-52=16` guard cells, more than one W9 reset period. The U left/right carry-uncertainty losses are at most 152/122, with 26 separating cells at k=100. Thus paired insertion commutes throughout all 120 steps, and hypothetical all-width fixed-seam return reduces to the exact reference-width condition just disproved. This is the phase-8 version of T12's conditional pumping argument, not an inference from five example widths.
