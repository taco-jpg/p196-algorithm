## Why this excludes *all* centres and k >= 48 (paper lemma)
For a symmetric LSD-first pairword `P` of actual length `N >= 2L`, knowing its first `L` coefficients also knows its last `L` in reverse order. Normalizing the decimal coefficient sum computes the first `L` digits from carry 0. In the top `L` coefficients, the unknown incoming carry is only 0 or 1. Let `r` be the trailing count of 9s in the known low-end prefix `p[0..L-1]` (these are the leading `r` entries of the mirrored high-end suffix). After the first non-9, the outgoing carry no longer depends on the incoming one. If the actual overflow is `gamma in {0,1}`, the first `L-1-r+gamma` coefficients of the next pairword are uniquely determined by the known prefix. If `p[0]!=9`, `gamma=[p[0]>=10]` is forced irrespective of the unknown interior carry; when `gamma=1` the extra top normalized digit is 1. Iterate 120 times, dropping exactly the uncertain suffix each step. This is an endpoint-only sound overapproximation: the interior is unrestricted at all times, so temporary W9 damage and repair are allowed.

Initial `L=34+9*14+|J|+48*3 <= 308`, hence every `k>=48` has the same initial known prefix and symmetric high end; the full palindrome has length at least `2L`. At all successive steps the actual length does not shrink and the known prefix does not exceed its initial length. Every enumerated 120-step prefix exhibits a W9 mismatch before its valid cut. The gamma sequence is forced (no 9 at index 0), exactly the phase-5 clock with 30 overflows.
