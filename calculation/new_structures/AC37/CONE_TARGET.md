# AC37 stronger target

For every q>=14, k>=100, any finite J, any of the thirty charge-zero U3 rotations, and either palindromic centre C=[4,4] or C=[4,14,4], show that 120 genuine reverse-add steps of the rooted phase-4 p12/m9 template cannot produce ANY symmetric template with the same U orientation and C adjacent to U copies on both sides. Prove the clock and centre mismatch using independent exact two-carry outer/inner cones, without T12 and without assuming W9 preservation during the epoch. The all-time decimal Lychrel existence problem remains open.
