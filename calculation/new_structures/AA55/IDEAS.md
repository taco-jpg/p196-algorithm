# AA55 ideas and falsification notes

## Alternating two-W9-band widths
Premise: use two W9 bands with widths `(q1,q2)` and allow an epoch to exchange their roles/widths, rather than demanding one stationary W9 cut. This is motivated by the supplied observation that stationary seams lose about 22 W9 columns per 120-step epoch.

Status: UNTESTED, not rejected. A width exchange is useful only if the exact 240-step seam map closes on a finite family and the net width drift vanishes or is absorbed by a rigorously specified permutation. A visual/approximate exchange or a finite replay is insufficient. Need the rooted decimal construction and independent verifier before drawing conclusions.
