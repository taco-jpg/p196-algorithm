# T12: proof of rooted 120-step finite-cut pumping

**Mathematical scope:** ordinary base-10 integers, not merely symbolic CA trajectories. This is a PAPER PROOF of a conditional two-counter pumping implication, supported by an exact finite carry kernel and independent integer arithmetic. Generic paired-insertion carry-loop soundness was previously described in reports/AgentN28PairedInsertion.md; T12's new contribution is the concrete rooted W9/U3 phase-and-cut bound and its single-width consequence.

**1. Genuine two-carry law.** For a positive integer with LSD-first digits d_0,...,d_(L-1), put P_i=d_i+d_(L-1-i). Normalize P with c_0=0, c_(i+1)=floor((P_i+c_i)/10), and e_i=(P_i+c_i) mod 10. The next integer is exactly n+reverse(n) with digits e_i plus the final overflow gamma=c_L. Thus the next symmetric pairword has Q_i=e_i+e_(L-1-i) if gamma=0; if gamma=1, Q_0=e_0+1 and Q_i=e_i+e_(L-i) for i>=1. Writing b_i=c_(L-i), the reverse-side equations are b_i=floor((P_i+b_(i+1))/10) and e_(L-1-i)=(P_i+b_(i+1)) mod 10. The gamma-one shifted upper index is essential; no phantom leading zero is used.

**2. Reset-cone lemma (arbitrary carry inlets).** For c in {0,1}, T_x(c)=floor((x+c)/10) forgets its incoming c at every x!=9; only x=9 propagates it. For two symmetric equal-length pairwords equal on an interval [a,b), with the same gamma and terminal runs of 9 of lengths rL from a and rR backward from b-1, their next pairwords agree on [a+1+rL, b-1-rR+gamma). This follows because both normalized low and mirrored high digits agree after their first non9 reset. For a rooted prefix of length m with P_0!=9 (so gamma is fixed by the common leading pair), the matching next prefix length is m-1-rR+gamma. The same rooted-prefix statement holds for different total source lengths: the normalized upper suffix and overflow are aligned as indicated by the exact formula in section 1.

**3. Periodic finite carry calculation.** A cyclic word w containing a non9 has unique periodic low and high carries alpha_(i+1)=T_(w_i)(alpha_i) and beta_i=T_(w_i)(beta_(i+1)), because any one full traversal resets the carry. It has an exact cyclic physical bulk successor F_gamma(w)_i=((w_i+alpha_i) mod10)+((w_i+beta_(i+1)) mod10) for gamma=0, or ((w_i+alpha_i) mod10)+((w_(i-1)+beta_i) mod10) for gamma=1. cut.py exhausts all twelve published B/W phase rows with both possible remote upper carries and all nine terminal W residues, and all 30 allowed U initial rows through 120 exact F transitions. The W rows obey W_(t+1)=F_(gamma_t)(W_t) modulo 12; the U rows return exactly to U after 120 steps, with one isolated 9 per 3-letter row at every intermediate phase.

**4. Actual rooted clock and causal edges.** Each published B_t begins with 10 when t=0 mod4 and otherwise with 1,2,5 (in that order). Its first coefficient is never 9, so the actual overflow bit is independently fixed regardless of the central carry: gamma_t=1 exactly when t=0 mod4. The two-port cap checks and the prefix-cone lemma inductively preserve the B_t prefix for all 120 phases when q>=11. The protected W prefix length shrinks by 1+rR-gamma at each step; the exact 120-step loss is 91. For a cyclic U phase row v_t and an initially pure interval [a,b), its protected left edge advances by 1+[v_t[a_t mod3]=9], and the right edge retreats by 1+[v_t[(b_t-1) mod3]=9]-gamma_t. The exact summed left/right losses are (152,121),(151,122),(150,120) for the three rotations, uniformly over all ten a values. These are safe upper bounds on physical propagation, not assertions of the smallest possible true defect. The B_t row has an odd coefficient for every t, giving a nonpalindrome guard for every actual t=0..120.

**5. Local full-period insertion.** In a genuine periodic block containing a full reset period to either side of an insertion cut, low and high carries at the cut have the unique periodic values. Inserting one whole row w at the low cut and its reverse at the reflected high cut leaves both external carry states unchanged, because its transfer loops once. The inserted normalized digit blocks, when combined using the gamma-correct pair formula, yield exactly F_gamma(w) and its reflection. Hence taking one actual decimal step commutes with paired insertion at such a cut. This is an equality of the *entire* pairwords, including the high overflow shift, and applies to either centre parity.

**6. Uniform bounds and pumping equivalence.** For q>=14 take W cut cW=34+18=52, after two copies. The rooted prefix has lost at most 91 at time 120, so even at q=14 the cut retains at least one complete W period plus reset guard on each side for each step t<120. For k>=100 take U cut cU=34+9q+|J|+165, after 55 copies. Since U-side losses are at most 152 and 122, the chosen cut has at least 13 protected columns on both sides even at time 120, more than one U reset period and the gamma shift. Therefore by induction through t=0..120, P_t(q+1,k)=Insert_(cW,W_(t mod12))(P_t(q,k)) and P_t(q,k+1)=Insert_(cU,U_t)(P_t(q,k)). At t=120 both inserted rows are back to W0 and U0, exactly matching the insertions in target P(q,k+5). Since insertion at a named cut is injective, Return(q+1,k) iff Return(q,k), and Return(q,k+1) iff Return(q,k). Induction in both independent counters proves Return(q,k) iff Return(14,100), for all q>=14,k>=100.

**7. Reproduction, independence, limits.** Run python3 calculation/new_structures/T12/cut.py from the repository root. It checks the 12-root two-port identities, W phase map, every 30-word U phase cycle and its cone, and 120 full paired insertion orbits (30 U words, two sizes q/k=(14,100)/(20,160), both W and U insertions, t=0..120). Every trajectory step is computed by both Python arbitrary-precision n+reverse(n) and a separately coded schoolbook digit-array method; tested centres include odd [10] and even [8,8] and nontrivial arbitrary junctions. These checks confirm the small arithmetic identities; the all-width quantifier follows from the reset and insertion induction, not from sampled widths. No specific J,C,U is known to satisfy Return(14,100); no actual decimal Lychrel number is proved.
