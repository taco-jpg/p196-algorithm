# AC46 target: physically rooted phase-0 reference-width seam return

PHYSICAL_TSTAR_RETURN_AT_REFERENCE_WIDTH is the existential problem of a finite junction J of arbitrary length and palindromic coefficient centre C, such that the canonical base-10 pairword

P(q,k;J,U,C) = H ++ C ++ reverse(H), H=B0 ++ W0^q ++ J ++ U^k,

undergoes 120 ordinary integer reverse-and-add iterations and returns to P(q,k+5;J,U,C), with exactly the same J,C,U and phase. U is one of thirty rotations of (9,a,18-a), a even 0..18. The full free-length marked-fold emptiness problem remains UNDECIDED.

**This attempt's strict sublemma:** for every finite J and all rooted q>=14,k>=100, and for each of the thirty U, no exact 120-step fixed-J,C return exists when C is a realizable palindromic coefficient word of length 1..6 (odd midpoint must be even). Prove by a sound both-carry-inlet central cone and a complete 331,470-case finite centre enumeration, NOT by a strict-per-phase W9 filter.

A fixed-seam regenerative certificate would require at least one centre outside this subfamily; this neither excludes changed centres nor proves that a decimal Lychrel number exists.
