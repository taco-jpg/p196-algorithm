# AC35 all-phase rooted T12 insertion theorem

**Label: PAPER PROOF**, finite arithmetic checked by `phase_cut.py`; not Lean-verified. **No decimal Lychrel certificate.**

Let p be an arbitrary temporal starting phase in {0,...,11}. Take the published LSD-first B_p (length34), W_p (length9); U any of the 30 charge-zero three-letter rotations of (9,a,18-a) for even a=0,...,18; arbitrary fixed finite coefficient junction J; any palindromic, canonically realizable coefficient centre C. Write H_p(q,k)=B_p ++ W_p^q ++ J ++ U^k and P_p=H_p ++ C ++ reverse(H_p). Return_p(q,k) means **120 true ordinary-decimal reverse-and-add steps** send the canonical source realizing P_p(q,k) to a number whose exact symmetric pairword is P_p(q,k+5).

**Theorem.** For all p, J,U,C, and independently variable q>=14,k>=100, Return_p(q,k) holds if and only if Return_p(14,100) holds. Every intervening phase retains an odd rooted coefficient, so a true returning seam would furnish an all-time nonpalindromic decimal orbit after the +5 centre-band growth. The theorem does NOT supply such a seam.

**Proof.** For LSD-first symmetric pair coefficients x_i, carry c_0=0 and c_(i+1)=floor((x_i+c_i)/10) give the exact normalized decimal digits e_i=(x_i+c_i) mod10 of n+reverse10(n). In the next pairword the upper partner is e_(L-1-i) with gamma=0 or e_(L-i) with gamma=1, treating the new leading overflow as digit1. The two-ended carry is therefore ordinary integer arithmetic, not an idealized spatial rule.

For coefficient x in 0..18 the carry map T_x(c)=floor((x+c)/10) resets its 0/1 inlet whenever x!=9; x=9 is the identity. A spatially periodic band with a non9 thus has unique cyclic low/high carries, and its exact one-step phase word is F_gamma(w). The 12 published B/W phase rows satisfy the two-ended rooted port identities for *both* high carry inlets, and W_(t+1)=F_(gamma_t)(W_t). B_t[0] follows 10,1,2,5 and fixes the actual overflow gamma_t=1 iff t mod4=0 regardless of the remote centre. Every U candidate evolves by F over the shifted 120-state clock, returns to itself, and has one isolated 9 per phase.

At the left protected seam-facing W edge, let m_0=34+9q. The worst-case causally protected prefix satisfies m_(t+1)=m_t-1-r_t+gamma_(p+t), where r_t is the contiguous 9 tail. Direct exact finite evaluation for p=0..11 gives 120-step losses **(91,91,92,90,90,90,90,91,92,92,93,90)**. For q=14 this leaves at least 67 protected coefficients beyond the fixed W insertion cut cW=52, exceeding one full 9-cell reset guard. The U flank loss recurrences, for bulk temporal phase v_t, are l_(t+1)=l_t+1+[v_t[l_t mod3]=9] and r_(t+1)=r_t+1+[v_t[(-r_t-1) mod3]=9]-gamma_(p+t). Exhaustively evaluating 12 starts x30 words yields l<=152, r<=122. Insert U after 55 copies, cU=34+9q+|J|+165. At k=100 the 165-152 and 135-122 margins are both >=13, larger than a complete U reset period. Greater independent q,k only improve guards.

Insert a complete W or U cycle at its protected cut and the reflected copy at the mirrored cut. The unique cyclic carry states make the inserted block's **both** physical port states unchanged; normalization with the gamma-shifted mirror rule maps it to precisely F_gamma(w). By induction across each of the 120 actual steps, the whole resulting pairword is the corresponding paired-block insertion at every temporal phase. At phase p+120 both band words and the gamma clock return. Paired insertion is injective, giving Return_p(q+1,k) iff Return_p(q,k), and independently Return_p(q,k+1) iff Return_p(q,k). Iterating the two equivalences proves the theorem. The preserved B_t has an odd coefficient at each of the 12 phases, excluding a palindromic integer in every intermediate state.
