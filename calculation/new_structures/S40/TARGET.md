Let R(n)=n+reverse10(n). For a canonical L-digit decimal number n, let P(n)[i]=d_i+d_(L-1-i), least-significant coefficient first. Let A=B0 ++ W0^7 ++ W0[:2], where B0 (34 cells) and W0 (9 cells) are the physically rooted p12/m9 arrays in guard.py. Thus A consists of 99 coefficients.

**THEOREM.** For every canonical decimal n of length L>=198 with source pairword prefix P(n)[:99]=A, and every integer t from 0 through 120 inclusive, the true decimal integer R^t(n) is not a palindrome. The genuine length-overflow clock on those 120 steps is (100010001000)^10 with 30 ones. The arbitrary interior digits and centre parity are unrestricted.

**Proof, universal one-step lemma.** The symmetric pair-coefficient word P(n) evaluates to R(n), before carrying. Each coefficient is in [0,18], and every ordinary decimal normalization carry is 0 or 1. Suppose P(n) has a known first m coefficients a, with L>=2m. Its last m coefficients are the reverse of a. Normalize the first m from low-end carry zero. To normalize the last m, explicitly consider **both** possible carries 0 and 1 entering from the unknown middle, then propagate over the reversed a. These two calculations return high digits and an outgoing overflow gamma. If both gamma values agree, the length of R(n) is exactly L+gamma. For gamma=0 the next physical *source* pairword prefix at index i is low[i]+high[m-1-i]. For gamma=1 it is low[0]+1 at index zero, and low[i]+high[m-i] at all i>=1. Keep the longest common prefix of the two calculated successors, length m'<=m. This is valid for **every** possible middle, and L+gamma>=2m', so the argument is inductive. Every case of leading overflow and lagged high digit is covered.

**Finite closed computation.** The exact two-port routine guard_next in guard.py repeatedly applies this sound step to A, taking both unknown high-end carries and checking their outgoing gamma agrees. There are 120 such universal steps, all with physical clock (100010001000)^10. At each of the 121 stages at least five source pair coefficients are known; the first five equal PHASES[t mod 12], where an odd coefficient occurs at positions [4,0,1,0,2,0,1,0,2,0,1,0]. The retained prefix has length exactly five at t=120. If a canonical number were a palindrome, every source pair coefficient would equal 2d_i and hence be even. Consequently none of the 121 actual integers can be palindromic. This proves the theorem for all widths, not merely for tested values. QED.
