# AC30 target: phase-9 fixed odd-centre T* subcase

Prove or refute for every independently varying q>=14 and k>=100 a physically rooted p12/m9 phase-9 literal 120-step return

    P9(q,k;J,U,C) -> P9(q,k+5;J,U,C)

where J is any five coefficients in 0..18, U is any of thirty charge-zero rotations of (9,a,18-a) for a even 0..18, and the palindromic fixed odd centre is C=(4,14,4). Here P9=H+C+reverse(H), H=B9+W9^q+J+U^k. Require genuine ordinary-decimal reverse-and-add, exact overflow clock, and nonpalindrome guard.

This target is only the fixed odd-centre half of the phase-9 J5 cell. The even C=(4,4) and every other palindromic centre are not decided.
