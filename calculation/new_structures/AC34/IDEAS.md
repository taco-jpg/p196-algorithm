# AC34 ideas and honest limitations
- The first W9 mismatch cannot be asserted at one universal location: changing J alters the first mismatch even at fixed U and C; tested locations include indices 135..142.
- No strict-phase W9 MDD is used: intermediate W9 damage may heal before the endpoint, so strict-phase pruning would not decide endpoint-only returns.
- Mod-11 gives a rigorous all-width odd-centre necessary condition, but says nothing about even palindromic centres. At one fixed even/even (q,k), U ranks cannot be eliminated by the all-width two-parity argument.
- Further work: exact two-ended carry-state endpoint product/DP with intermediate healing permitted, or a sound all-q,k reset-cone lift and full scan. Neither a 120-step mismatch nor the necessary modular gate establishes a decimal Lychrel certificate.
