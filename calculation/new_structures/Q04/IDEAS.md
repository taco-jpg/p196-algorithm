# Q04 rejected routes and precise constraints

- A single non-nine coefficient resets binary carry, so a repeated nonneutral W block forgets the incoming bit after one copy. This is known from FORMATS and is not new. Persistent repair must use physical centre information, two-sided phase coupling, or genuinely neutral rails.
- Abstract 9-class two-port product maps are not the coupled physical temporal centre maps. Their closed-subset count cannot prove or refute a decimal Lychrel orbit.
- Partial-length prefixes rather than whole blocks can have spatial residue-dependent carry. A mixed-polarity W need not give one entering carry for all cut lengths. A phase residue belongs in the state.
- The all-width rec2 upper seam gate U=(01,0,01,1,01,1,01,1) holds at one step, but does not force the centre to reproduce the gate after eight or more steps. The needed proof is a physical nonconsuming defect repair.
- A fixed centre carry perturbation changes normalized digits, but the centre can rewrite coefficients, so this does not exclude adaptive regenerative centres.
