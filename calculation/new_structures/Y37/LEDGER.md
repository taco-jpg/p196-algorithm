## Exact length/resource ledger

Let q,k count 20-coefficient W,V blocks and c be the finite-collar length in the half-template, excluding the fixed centre. Every 80-step p8 epoch has 40 true overflows, so

20 Δq + 20 Δk + Δc = 20.

The independent T14 shift-7 replay gives:

| t | q_out | k_out | c_out | prefix/tail collar | centre |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | q | k | 24 | 24/0 | (5,5) |
| 80 | q+1 | k-4 | 104 | 64/40 | (2,2) |
| 160 | q+1 | k-8 | 204 | 124/80 | (10,10) |
| 240 | q | k-12 | 324 | 204/120 | (6,6) |
| 320 | q-1 | k-15 | 424 | 264/160 | (16,16) |

Thus the first 80-step edge has (Δq,Δk,Δc)=(+1,-4,+80). To close the collar in the next 80 steps with Δq=0, the length identity forces (Δk,Δc)=(+5,-80). Combined, the hoped-for 160-step cycle would have net (+1,+1,0). The actual second edge is (0,-4,+100), so the required recharger is absent from T14's current path.

For Y37's five rotations, the exact 160-step endpoint has q_out=q-1, k_out=k-8, collar length 244 (prefix 164, tail 80). Its length ledger is -20-160+220=+40, matching the 80-overflow half-width growth, but it is not a W emitter. Literal daughter collars are generated by the checker.
