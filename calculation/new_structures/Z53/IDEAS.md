# Z53 ideas and redirects

- **Redirect:** Do not treat a phase-0 MDD census as complete if its shard rank uses the phase-9 12-state cardinality. V48 reports phase 9 = 12 pairs / 20 triples / 31 quadruples, while phase-0 controls are 16 / 36 / 43. AA10's initial phase-0 s=0..11 contract omitted four phase-0 pair states, but AA10 retracted and corrected it before any completion claim (#6492969761, #6492970680). Require a source-grounded, phase-tagged phase-0 list/hash; do not count runs using the old predicate as complete. AA02's later claim #6492611613 ranks all 361 J[0:2] pairs and is not affected by this specific count mismatch.
- The strict-W9 MDD only characterizes paths that preserve W9 at every intermediate phase. It cannot certify completeness for endpoint-only restoration while transiently damaged/repaired W9 paths are omitted.
- Q51 found the general Q03 overflow row `18 -> 9, cL:any` is false; its concrete counterexample has `(cL,cU,dU)=(1,0,0)`, so require `cL=0`. No audited census code uses the broad row as a filter; do not generalize it beyond the corrected physical gate.
- The common owner `1+((s+u) mod 12)` covers 16×30=480 pairs disjointly, but its twelve shard sizes are uneven. Do not assume 40 pairs per shard; verify the actual owner predicate and per-slice counts.
- Keep the even-centre Q39 counterexample and p16/p12 scope warning separate from any claim about a full seam return.
