# Z53 ideas and redirects

- **Redirect:** Do not treat a phase-0 MDD census as complete if its shard rank uses the phase-9 12-state cardinality. V48 reports phase 9 = 12 pairs / 20 triples / 31 quadruples, while phase-0 controls are 16 / 36 / 43. AA10's phase-0 s=0..11 contract and AA02's later s=0..11 contract omit four phase-0 pair states; rebuild from a source-grounded, phase-tagged phase-0 list/hash.
- The strict-W9 MDD only characterizes paths that preserve W9 at every intermediate phase. It cannot certify completeness for endpoint-only restoration while transiently damaged/repaired W9 paths are omitted.
- Keep the even-centre Q39 counterexample and p16/p12 scope warning separate from any claim about a full seam return.
