Then, after exactly 120 genuine N <- N+reverse(N) steps, (i) the 120 overflow bits are exactly (0001)^30, and (ii) the first 70 coefficients of the resulting pairword equal the first 70 of B_5 ++ W_5^14. This applies to **every** J,U,C of T* at reference q=14, including both centre parities. It does *not* require W9 preservation at intermediate phases.

**Proof of soundness.** At any step a symmetric pairword has equal reversed outer coefficient blocks p[0:L]. Normalize the low block from carry 0, which gives its digits exactly. The unknown carry entering the high block from the unobserved middle lies in {0,1}, since every coefficient is at most 18. Normalize the reversed high block separately for both incoming carries. If both give identical outgoing overflow g and first L' of the *paired* next coefficients, every possible actual middle yields the same g and that prefix. For g=0 partner indices are n-1-i; for g=1 they are n-i, with new high digit 1 at i=0. Thus `step_cone` safely intersects both cases. Induct over 120 exact normalization steps; the length n never decreases while the retained prefix length never increases, preserving the disjoint outer-block premise. `prove_reference(14)` checks the finite deterministic cone and yields L'=70, g=(0001)^30 and exact prefix equality. The proof has no strict-phase-W9 assumption and does not depend on how the original coefficient pairs are split into source digits.
