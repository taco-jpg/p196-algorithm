# Target (UNDECIDED)
For the published rooted p12/m9 PHASE 3, determine whether there exists a fixed length-6 junction J, U among the 30 charge-zero U3 rotations, and palindromic centre C, such that all canonical integers realizing P(q,k)=H+C+rev(H), H=B3+W3^q+J+U^k, return after exactly 120 ordinary decimal reverse-and-add steps to P(q,k+5) with the same B3,W3,J,U,C, the complete phase and non-palindrome guard, uniformly for all sufficiently large q,k.

Reference-width finite predicate: q=14,k=100, even C=(4,4), odd C=(4,14,4). A negative at that reference width is only relevant to uniform pumping when the T12 cut/insertion theorem's hypotheses have been proved; we do not claim this for arbitrary centres or moving junctions.
