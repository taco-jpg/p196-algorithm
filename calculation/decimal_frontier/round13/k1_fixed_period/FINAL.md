Depth (r12g section 1): an orbit has defect confined to depth D if for every t >= t0 the pair word agrees with L on every folded column below c_t := h_t - D, and gamma_t = g_t. ## 2. New objects and reduction (PAPER PROOF)

**Definition.** An *accepting* state of R_K is a window state (phi, w) with w_0 != L(t, c) (bottom cell off-lattice). E(K,T) := states of R_K on a closed walk of length exactly T (T a multiple of the phase orbit length Lo). E_acc(K,T) := states of R_K lying on a closed walk of length exactly T that passes through an accepting state. E_acc(K,T) subset E(K,T) subset Cyc(K).

**Lemma R1 (centre <-> accepting closed walk).** Let O be a centre of period dividing T and depth exactly D >= K >= 1. In the frame c_t = h_t - D the bottom K cells of the window states of O form a closed walk of R_K of length T, and at some time of the period the bottom cell (column h_t - D) is off-lattice. Hence all its states lie in E_acc(K,T). *Proof.* r12g Lemmas 2, 3: the window states of O are a periodic orbit of F_D; projecting to the bottom K cells gives a closed walk of R_K (period dividing T, so a closed walk of length exactly T). Since D is the depth, for some t the column c_t = h_t - D differs from the lattice, i.e. the bottom cell at that time is off-lattice. QED

**Lemma R2 (incremental construction of E_acc).** E_acc(K,T) subset E_acc(K-1,T) x {0..18} for K >= 2, and E_acc(K,T) is exactly the set of states of the subgraph of R_K induced on E_acc(K-1,T) x {0..18} that lie on a closed walk of length T through an accepting state. *Proof.* Projection to the bottom K-1 cells (r12g Lemma 3) maps a closed T-walk of R_K to a closed T-walk of R_{K-1} with the same bottom cell (K >= 2), so an accepting closed T-walk of R_K lies inside E_acc(K-1,T) x {0..18}. Conversely a closed T-walk of the induced subgraph is a closed T-walk of R_K. QED (Same argument for E(K,T) with "accepting" removed.)

**Lemma R3 (exact fold check).** If F_K has no orbit of period dividing T inside E_acc(K,T), there is no centre of period dividing T and depth exactly K. *Proof.* A centre of depth exactly K is a periodic orbit of F_K of period dividing T whose states lie in E_acc(K,T) by Lemma R1. QED. (The code checks F_K from the anchor layer = smallest layer of E_acc(K,T); every orbit visits every phase.)

**Theorem R4 (closure).** Let T = 80L (or 192L for rec2). If E_acc(K0,T) = {} for some K0 and F_K has no orbit of period dividing T inside E_acc(K,T) for all 1 <= K < K0, then there is no centre of period dividing T at any depth >= 1 in the computed phase class; depth 0 (the all-lattice orbit) is excluded separately (r12g: the exterior lattices are not self-mirror; here eset2's K <= 26 exact checks found no all-lattice exact orbit either).
*Proof.* Depth D >= K0: by Lemma R1 with K = K0 the set E_acc(K0,T) would be non-empty. Depth 1 <= D < K0: Lemma R3 with K = D. QED

**Phase classes, rec1.** By Phi (r12g section 3) E_acc(K,T) in orbit Phi(O) = Phi(E_acc(K,T) in O) (Phi preserves "bottom cell off-lattice": 18-w_0 != 18-L iff w_0 != L). So the statement for orbit 0 covers all 4 classes. It was nevertheless computed directly for all four orbits of rec0 and of rec1 (identical EMPTY levels, see the table).

## 6. Structural observations (data only, not proved)

* The surviving closed 80-walks of R_K are nearly fully off-lattice windows with their front near the bottom: in E_acc(K,80) for K >= 30 every state has its front (lowest off-lattice cell) at index <= 8 (front histogram f = 0..8 only; 0 states with f >= 9), and accepting states (f = 0) occur only at the six phases (t,j,par) = (1,15,1), (2,15,1), (3,15,0), (4,15,0), (5,0,1), (6,0,1) (layers 57-60 and 77-78 of orbit 0; K = 44: only (5,0,1) and (6,0,1)). The replayed K = 44 walks have the front at cells 0..8 throughout the period and the cells above the front off-lattice at almost all times (src/walkcheck2.py: the X/. pattern, logs/acclayers output). So the walks are rigid "half-kinks": a defect that fills the window above a front that stays within 8 cells of the frame, sustained by the free top inputs up to width 44, and impossible at width 45.
* Accepting states: 160,776 (K = 15), 148,136 (K = 17), 105,082 (K = 20), 84,402 (K = 23), 58,012 (K = 25), 22,210 (K = 29), 13,618 (K = 32), 4,265 (K = 35), 1,269 (K = 39), 523 (K = 42), 116 (K = 43), 6 (K = 44), 0 (K = 45).
* **Split identity (consistency check between eset2 and eset3).** For K >= 2 the closed T-walks of R_K split into those that are accepting and those whose bottom cell stays on lattice at all times; the latter correspond bijectively (drop the bottom cell, frame c+1) to the closed T-walks of R_{K-1} in the phase orbit shifted by j -> j+1 (proof: Lemma 3 of r12g applied with a lattice bottom cell; the lattice-exact check at c+1 includes cell c). So |E(K,T)| = |E(K-1,T)| + |E_acc(K,T)| - (overlap), where overlap counts states on both kinds of walks. Observed for T = 80 (rec0 orbit 0): overlap = 0 exactly for K = 21..26 (|E(K)| - |E(K-1)| - |E_acc(K)| = 0), small and decreasing before (K = 20: -18, K = 19: -2921); equal orbit sizes make the shift harmless. Hence |E(K,80)| is eventually constant: |E(K,80)| = 32,428,479 for all K >= 44 (= |E(26)| + sum_{27..44} |E_acc|; not recomputed directly beyond K = 26), consistent with the observed linear growth. E grows because accepting walks of depth D exist for every D <= 44 and each contributes its states.
* **The deepest walks are sporadic, not a second lattice.** At K = 44 there are 709 states (5-12 per layer, differing mostly in the top 1-3 cells); comparing the windows with all rows of the lattice (direct and reversed, all (t, j)) gives at most 9 of 44 matching cells, so the defect region is not a shifted or mirrored copy of the lattice (random-looking digits; src/patcmp.py). There is no "second phase" behind the collapse; the sets simply thin out and die at K = 45.
* **Exact fold chains inside E_acc are short.** For every K <= 44 the exact fold map F_K stays inside E_acc(K,80) for at most 27 consecutive steps (K = 11, 15, 16, 20: 27; K = 30: 20; K = 40: 14; K = 43: 11; K = 44: 7; logs/foldlen_rec0_o0.log), nowhere near the 80 steps of a period. So even the exact-orbit check is not close: the sets of states of E_acc(K,80) have short forward F_K-lifetimes.
* Depth bound D_max(T) = (largest K with E_acc(K,T) non-empty) - no general law is known: rec0/rec1 T = 80: D_max = 44 (collapse at K = 45); rec2 T = 192: D_max = 39 (orbit 0; collapse at K = 40, see section 7). A naive light-cone guess (D_max ~ T/2 + c) fails for rec2. Not proved in general; for every T the emptiness at some K is the theorem that matters (Theorem R4).

## 8. Routes considered but not needed / failed (Task B)

Task B ideas and what happened (all UNDECIDED beyond the T = 80 statement unless a label is given):

1. **Meet in the middle at a cut column.** Measured the obstacle first: the number of closed 80-walks of R_K (trace of the 80-step
   transfer, all sources of the anchor layer) is W(K,80) = 3.5e7, 5.3e10, 1.0e13, 1.4e14, 5.8e14, 1.3e16, 1.5e17, 2.8e17, 4.0e17, 5.1e17, 5.9e17, 6.3e17, 6.5e17, 6.9e17 (K = 1..14).
   The per-level growth saturates near a factor 1.05 at K = 12..14 (sideways transfer is near-critical), and the number of distinct interface streams
   is at least W / |anchor| ~ 4e13 at K = 14. So explicit stream sets are hopeless. Superseded by E_acc, which keeps only states, not streams (the sideways structure is what makes E_acc small).
2. **SAT with learned structure.** Used only as the cross-check (relsat, K = 44, 45, T = 80; section 5). Direct fixed-period SAT at depth 30+ was not attempted beyond that because E_acc settled T = 80.
3. **Invariants.** Derived (PAPER PROOF, simple): N_{t+1} = 2 N_t mod 9 (digit sum) and N_{t+1} = (1 + (-1)^{n-1}) N_t mod 11; block forms with boundary flux (mod 9 and, with alternating signs, mod 11, using 10 = 1 and 10 = -1). Consequences:
   (i) for period 80K' with 3 not dividing K': digit-sum charge Q = 0 mod 3 (from 2^80 = 4 mod 9); (ii) N = 0 mod 11 at every odd length after the first even length (a factor 11 filter at odd-n phases of the all-period forward iteration). Neither gives an obstruction on its own (the lattice block sums are 0 mod 9 and 0 mod 11, checked by hand for rec0 t = 0, 1), and neither was needed for T = 80. Not used for any result here; a possible 2-3 level gain for the r12g forward route (not attempted).
4. **Wall speed (K2) in the period-80 regime.** The T = 80 statement says: every closed 80-walk of R_K with K >= 45 has its bottom cell on-lattice at all 80 times, i.e. the deepest possible front of a period-80 solution is 44 cells below the top of the window (D_max(80) = 44; K = 44 explicit walks exist, K = 45 none). That is a sharp, computed "wall" for T = 80. No analogue for general T (see section 7). A pure front-speed lemma remains impossible (r12d; the relaxation sustains half-kinks up to depth 44 at T = 80).
5. **Consequences of the sideways near-criticality:** the sharp collapse of E_acc after the peak is empirical; I have no proof that E_acc(K,T) is eventually empty for every T, and no simple law for the collapse depth: T = 80 (rec0, rec1): K* = 45; T = 192 (rec2): K* = 40 and 41 (the larger period collapses earlier).
