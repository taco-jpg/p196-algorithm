# AgentQ21SlowManifoldChargeGate: the first admissible unit-charge slow cycle

**PAPER PROOF; necessary arithmetic only.** Base 10, physically rooted p8/m20 paired lattice B,W (|B|=39, |W|=20, sum(W)=180), a literal two-counter slow-centre ansatz, fixed finite caps. No decimal Lychrel number or regenerative carry cycle is obtained. This is a restriction on an **all-symbolic-length** return, not a finite-horizon claim.

Write every pair-coefficient word least-significant first. Let A,V,J be fixed words with V nonempty of length d, J palindromic, and coefficients in 0..18. Set

P(q,s) = B ++ W^q ++ A ++ V^s ++ J ++ reverse(V)^s ++ reverse(A) ++ reverse(W)^q ++ reverse(B).

Assume, for **all** q>=q0 and s>=s0, these are pairwords of canonical decimal integers, whose genuine reverse-and-add evolutions follow overflow 10101010 for 88r steps and return **literally** to P(q+e,s+delta) with identical B,W,A,V,J. Here r>=1, e>=0, delta>=1 are fixed. An all-time certificate would additionally require carry-closed individual edges and a nonpalindrome guard at every phase; these are **not** supplied here. Let h=sum(V).

**Theorem (necessary conditions).** 20e+d*delta=22r and (7^r-1)*h=0 (mod 9). If 3|r, also delta*h=0 (mod 9). Consequently if gcd(h,9)=1, then 3|r and 9|delta. In this unit-charge case **no return with r<6** can satisfy the gates: the first arithmetically admissible macroperiod is r=6 (528 true additions). For example e=3,d=8,delta=9,h=64 is compatible **only at the arithmetic level**, not a physical candidate.

**Proof.** P(q,s) has length 2|B|+40q+2|A|+2ds+|J|. The p8 overflow word has four ones per eight additions, so after 88r additions the source digit length grows by 44r. Comparing the literal source and return lengths gives 40e+2d*delta=44r. At each true decimal reverse-and-add step the pairword coefficient sum (twice digit sum) doubles modulo 9. Hence sum(P(q+e,s+delta)) = 2^(88r)*sum(P(q,s)) (mod 9). Since 2^88=7 (mod 9) and 2sum(W)=360=0 (mod 9), subtracting this identity at s and at s+1 yields (7^r-1)*2h=0 (mod 9), equivalent to the slope gate. If h is invertible modulo 9, 7 having order three forces 3|r. For these r, 2^(88r)=1 (mod 9), and cancellation of fixed caps and W contributions yields 2*delta*h=0 (mod 9), so 9|delta. At r=3 the length equation says 0<=e<=3 and 20e+d*delta=66; since 9|delta, 2e=3 (mod 9), or e=6 (mod 9), impossible for 0<=e<=3. For r=1,2,4,5 the slope gate already fails. At r=6, (e,d,delta,h)=(3,8,9,64) satisfies the displayed gates and length equation, but not any claimed carry/interface/guard equation. QED.

**Scope and prior art.** Q01's docs/CERT_SPEC_Q01.md proves a related fixed-centre all-q charge gate. The present two-counter theorem allows an independently **growing centre band** while the original W exterior remains constant or grows. REPORT_205 gives other interface-charge restrictions for six-cycle uniform bands. The r<6 obstruction applies **only** to literal returns with unit-charge V, both fixed finite caps and nonshrinking exterior; nonunit-charge V, longer fast periods, changing caps, shrinking q or nonlinear centres remain open. It neither constructs nor excludes an actual base-10 Lychrel number.
