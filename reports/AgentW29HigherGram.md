# Universal higher-gram parity quotient (W29)
**PAPER PROOF** for the all-base finite reduction and strict one-step decimal family; **COMPUTED** for finite controls. No Lean proof, no infinite decimal Lychrel integer.

Fix base b>=2 and width h>=2. For the ordinary pair-coefficient carry normalizer on input symbols a=0,...,2b-2, track the binary carry, the suffix of at most h-1 previously emitted digits, and a mod-2 bit for each reversal orbit {u,reverse(u)} of asymmetric length-h digit words. Whenever the next emitted digit completes a length-h window, toggle the orbit's bit. Any final overflow 1 is appended by the same rule. A palindrome has ZERO such parity mask because every occurrence of an asymmetric h-gram is paired with the occurrence of its reversal at the mirrored position.

**General proof.** For a nonempty coefficient block w, the outgoing carry map on {0,1} is monotone, so it is identity or constant and is idempotent. Once the first w copy has run, subsequent copies emit a fixed nonempty digit block. After h copies in total, the last h-1 emitted digits are entirely from steady copies and form a stable suffix at the repeated-block boundary. Every subsequent w copy produces the same suffix, carry, and XOR mask increment. Two additional copies cancel under XOR. Therefore T_w^(h+2)=T_w^h as transformations on every carry/suffix/parity state, for all b>=2,h>=2,nonempty w. This is exact, without a digit-length bound.

For all exponents k>=1, reduce k to k if k<h and otherwise to h or h+1 according to k's parity. Composition inside arbitrary literal contexts is preserved. A template with r independently positive unbounded powered bands has only (h+1)^r representative exponent vectors for its h-gram successor safety check. If each gives nonzero mask, the normalized successor is nonpalindromic for every permitted band length. For a genuinely infinite certificate, a separate exact successor-closed template cycle, exponent positivity and a nonpalindromic initial integer are still indispensable; no such base10 cycle is asserted here.

**Strict decimal gain.** Let L=[2,8,12,4,12,6,16], W=[8,12], C=[8], and P(k)=L++W^k++C++reverse(W)^k++reverse(L), least-significant coefficients first and k>=1. Every coefficient is even, so the earlier odd-pair test gives no witness. Split the outermost p0=2 into low/high digits (0,2); each other lower-half coefficient p into (min(9,p),p-min(9,p)); set the middle coefficient 8 to digit 4. This defines a canonical decimal N(k) with pairword P(k), nonpalindromic because its outer digits differ. The normalized successor has exactly one odd-frequency digit, so the histogram obstruction also fails. Its unordered 2-gram parity mask is ZERO. But its 3-gram parity has exactly TWO nonzero reversal orbits, represented by triples (5,2,8) and (5,2,9). For k=1,2,3,4 these are the same two bits, and T_w^5=T_w^3 proves all k>=1 reduce to these four cases. Thus N(k)+reverse(N(k)) is nonpalindromic for every k>=1. This is a universal ONE-STEP family, not a closed reverse-add orbit. Example k=1 normalized successor is 2925276929296725282.
