# All-modulus classification for fully spatial and temporal pair-weight/carry corrections on rec2

**PAPER PROOF**, supported by two independent finite exact arithmetic implementations. This classifies a 768-variable cyclic-bulk ansatz, not the existence of a decimal periodic centre or a base-10 Lychrel certificate.

## Cyclic bulk and local test domain

Let P_j in 0..18 for j modulo 48, with unique cyclic ascending a_j and descending d_j satisfying a_(j+1)=[P_j+a_j>=10] and d_(j-1)=[P_j+d_j>=10]. The row maps are

`F0(P)_j=2P_j+a_j+d_j-10(a_(j+1)+d_(j-1))`,
`F1(P)_j=P_j+P_(j-1)+a_j+d_(j-1)-10(a_(j+1)+d_(j-2))`.

Take eight rec2 coefficient rows W_t with overflow g=10101010, copied as `lat2.txt`. The test domain consists of all eight W_t, every valid W_t±e_j, and every valid adjacent pair W_t±e_j±e_(j+1), with cyclic wrap. There are exactly **2252** row/time tests. Carries are actual periodic cyclic solutions, not formally independent variables.

Consider 768 independent phase/spatial weights v_(t,j), w_(t,j) modulo any M>=2, normalized by v_(0,0)=1:

`C_t(P)=sum_(j=0..47)(v_(t,j)P_j+w_(t,j)d_j(P)) (mod M)`.

Demand `C_(t+1)(F_(g_t)(P))=A_t C_t(P)` on those tests, with A_t=11 if g_t=1 and A_t=2 otherwise. **Theorem:** this test-domain covariance problem is feasible if and only if **M divides 198**.

## Necessity: checkable finite arithmetic certificates

Subtract reference equations from perturbation equations. The integer matrix has 767 free coefficients (v_(0,0) fixed) and an integer right-hand side. Four explicit **integer linear combinations** of selected equations cancel every one of those 767 coefficients. Their exact right-hand sides are:

1. `57722492937563942400` (66 terms; last tag t7,j7,+1);
2. `-20438186631342374822400` (75 terms; t7,j9,-1);
3. `-66109667096044368096840629247895333360281104449322505169610391005188121785752` (389 terms; t7,j44,+1);
4. `1835319004200821590347687954670075948937815901849781099479800727332204713303180` (393 terms; t7,j45,+1).

Their gcd equals exactly **396**, so every feasible modulus M must divide 396. A further 82-term linear combination **modulo 4** cancels all 767 coefficients while leaving right-hand side **2 modulo 4**. Consequently, if 4 divides M the mod-4 reduction is inconsistent. Thus M|396 and 4∤M force **M|198**.

The complete coefficient multipliers for the five certificates are deterministically regenerated by the committed, standard-library `generate.py` from `lat2.txt`. Its exact fraction-free input row construction and rational row elimination, plus p-adic mod-4 elimination, are independently checked coefficient-by-coefficient by `independent_check.py`, which constructs the successor row by a different cyclic digit-normalization algorithm. These are **finite proof witnesses**; their reconstruction needs no external SAT/SMT package or private data and takes about 13 seconds on one local core. The divisibility consequence holds for every M, not a capped list of test moduli.

## Sufficiency

Modulo 2, take v_(0,0)=v_(0,1)=v_(1,1)=1, all other v and all w zero. The independent implementations verify these weights on **all 2252 specified rows**. Modulo 99, take v_(t,j)=10^j and w=0. This weighted cyclic bulk charge obeys the F0 multiplier 2 and F1 multiplier 11 for every cyclic coefficient word by the usual telescope: descending carry residuals contain 1-10^2=-99 and wrap closure uses 10^48=1 mod 99. Both solutions normalize v_(0,0)=1. Combining each weight separately by the Chinese remainder theorem produces a solution modulo 198, and reduction gives a solution for every divisor M of 198.
