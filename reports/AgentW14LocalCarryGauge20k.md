# W14: local modular carry-gauge erasure and exponential decimal finite-horizon family

## Exact local theorem (PAPER PROOF)
Fix base `b>=3`, an even-length symmetric coefficient word `P=(P_0,...,P_{n-1})` with `0<=P_i<=2b-2`, `P_0>=b`, and canonical decimal or base-b integer realizing it. Define `S` as one or more lower-half indices `1<=i<n/2-1` such that `P_i` is in `{1,...,b-2,b,...,2b-3}` and both `P_{i-1}` and `P_{i+1}` are different from `b-1`. At any subset of S, independently toggle `P_i` between `r` and `r+(b-1)` (where `1<=r<=b-2`), making the same toggle at its mirror. Write `P'` for the changed symmetric coefficient word. Then `pairs(norm_b(P))=pairs(norm_b(P'))`, including the same leading overflow digit.

**Proof.** Set `z_j` to zero at untouched sites, and to the signed change `+1/-1` in the coefficient's `(b-1)` multiple at toggled sites; extend symmetrically. Thus `P'_j-P_j=(b-1)z_j`. A coefficient different from `b-1` absorbs any incoming carry in `{0,1}`; for toggled sites the outgoing carry difference equals exactly `z_j`, since their residue lies in `1..b-2`. At an untouched neutral (`b-1`) position, the incoming carry difference is zero by induction, because its immediately preceding position is not toggled. Therefore the difference of outgoing carries after column `j` is `z_j` for every j (with `z_{-1}=z_{n-1}=0`). In digit normalization, `d'_j-d_j=(b-1)z_j+z_{j-1}-bz_j=z_{j-1}-z_j`. The overflow digit is unchanged and equals 1 by `P_0>=b` and symmetry. The successor has length `n+1`; its symmetric pair at j uses positions `j` and `n-j`, whose digit differences are negatives by `z_{n-1-j}=z_j`. Hence all successor coefficients agree. QED.

For any pairword P realized as mirror-digit pairs of an integer N, `N+reverse_b(N)=value_b(P)` and hence `pairs(N+reverse_b(N))=pairs(norm_b(P))`. Consequently the *second* true integer reverse-add iterates of any two masked seeds coincide, although their initial integers differ. If a seed contains a fixed odd coefficient and its first successor pairword does also, and their common later orbit is palindrome-free through H, then *all* masked seeds are palindrome-free through H.
