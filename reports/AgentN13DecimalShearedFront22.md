## Why the observed structure is relevant

This is a **moving interface**, not a stationary or periodic centre. The first mismatching pair moves outward by 20 positions per 88 steps, exactly one spatial lattice period while the exterior returns after a multiple of its temporal period 8. This is a concrete candidate glider *front* with a rational speed 5/22 in space per time. Its defect is invading, not healing: after 20,000 steps only 556 paired positions remain exterior-identical between the two orbits. The data therefore redirect the centre search from short stationary cores toward **two-interface collision/regeneration**: look for a complementary inward-moving or replenishing domain boundary whose net effect keeps the exterior safe. Mere finite replay cannot assert this happens indefinitely.

## An explicit new candidate certificate FORMAT: semilinear spacetime atlas

A finite description of a reverse-add orbit need not have a time-periodic finite core. Let time t>=0, digit index 0<=j<n(t); permit n(t) to be any Presburger-definable function and **permit separate moving interfaces j=alpha_i*t+beta_i on congruence classes**, with bounded digits D(t,j) in {0,...,9}, symmetric pair coefficients P(t,j) in {0,...,18}, and carry bits C(t,j) in {0,1}. Each function is specified by a finite disjoint partition of (t,j) into semilinear regions with a constant output (potentially periodic residues). A checker validates the following universally quantified local conditions, with the upper-edge carry condition and a fixed initial decimal seed:

1. `D(0,j)` is the given canonical seed, `D(t,n(t)-1)>=1`, and `P(t,j)=D(t,j)+D(t,n(t)-1-j)` for all t,j. (The checker may instead use the latter identity only for t=0 and the next-step identity below thereafter.)
2. `C(t,0)=0` and `P(t,j)+C(t,j)=D(t+1,j)+10*C(t,j+1)` for every `0<=j<n(t)`.
3. `n(t+1)=n(t)+C(t,n(t))`; if the terminal carry is 1, `D(t+1,n(t))=1`, otherwise that index is outside the word.
4. A verified nonpalindrome witness: for each t, an index `w(t)<n(t)` with `D(t,w(t)) != D(t,n(t)-1-w(t))`, or equivalently a counterexample to all-digit reflection. The witness can itself be Presburger-definable.

**PAPER PROOF of the conditional soundness statement** (the format, NOT a decimal instance): by the base-10 carry equation and `C(t,0)=0`, positional evaluation of the digits in (2) and (3) gives `val(D(t+1))=val(P(t))=val(D(t))+val(reverse(D(t)))`; canonical length follows from the leading-digit/terminal-carry rules. Induction on t establishes the infinite orbit starting at D(0). Condition (4) excludes palindromes at every step. All verification obligations are first-order Presburger arithmetic with constants and fixed congruences; each counterexample search is decidable by standard Presburger arithmetic decision procedures. The description is finite even with **growing centre width and unequal wall slopes**, because every field and region is semilinear in time and position. This is strictly a format soundness argument. No Presburger atlas satisfying these conditions for base 10 has been found or formally verified here.

The finite p8 replay exhibits an exterior region where the atlas phase field is `j mod 20, t mod 8` and a measured inner interface satisfying a 22-step translation. It does **not** prove that the entire interior is semilinear, and no such claim is made. A credible next search should take the observed 88-step interface translation, solve for a full local tile/carry-state return word across the interface, and pair two interfaces with opposite flux. That would be a genuine infinite certificate candidate, unlike enlarging the initial seed indefinitely.
