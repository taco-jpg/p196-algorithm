# Agent N12: constructively pumped decimal zero-corridor orbits

Status: **COMPUTED** for the 20,000-step decimal instances and their exact row traces; **PAPER PROOF** for the central-insertion lemma below. Neither is a decimal Lychrel proof. All trace conclusions are finite-horizon.

## Seed-first construction and shape
For L,R equal-length nonzero decimal endpoint packets, use N(L,g,R) = integer with decimal digits L ++ 0^g ++ R (g=12000). For a state of n digits d_0,...,d_(n-1) least-significant first, define its symmetric pair coefficients P_i=d_i+d_(n-1-i). The canonical central-zero corridor shape is P=B_t ++ 0^(z_t) ++ reverse(B_t), where B_t is the shortest prefix ending in a nonzero coefficient, and width w_t=|B_t|; then z_t=n_t-2w_t. This is a real constraint z_t>=16, not a generic consequence of pair symmetry. A state is a palindrome iff every opposing pair of digits agrees; the checked first mismatch is also recorded.

For both seeds 39 ++ 0^12000 ++ 43 and 71 ++ 0^12000 ++ 59, all 20001 states t=0..20000 have no palindrome and have a central zero corridor of at least 33 and 46 positions respectively. Primary digit-vector reverse-add with explicit carries and independent GMP arbitrary-precision N + reverse(N) produce **identical complete 20001-row traces**, not merely matching endpoints. The row hashes are 64-bit FNV-1a of every decimal digit, least-significant first, and the entire traces have SHA256:
- 39/43: f25735497a6f490165cf06b4f133310f44030b9fcbf9abde7e3200741203d0cb, n_20000=20283, w_20000=10125, z_20000=33, 8279 overflow steps.
- 71/59: 4e3785cb54a57cd893d241caca33c13cca09157c9ff51e08f51b4d21359f3f62, n_20000=20236, w_20000=10095, z_20000=46, 8232 overflow steps.

## Exact central-padding identity (paper argument, not Lean verified)
For a decimal digit string d with a *symmetric central zero corridor* of length z>=4 and positive leading digit, let Pad_k(d) insert k additional zero digits at the central split, for any k>=0, without disturbing endpoints. Write T(d)=d+reverse(d), using ordinary integer arithmetic and canonical digits. Then **T(Pad_k(d)) = Pad_k(T(d))**, with Pad applied to the centre of the new canonical digits. In the pair-coefficient normalization P_i=d_i+d_(n-1-i), inserting central zeros inserts the same central zeros in P. Normalizing P left-to-right carries c_i in {0,1}; a zero coefficient turns an incoming c into one output digit c and resets the outgoing carry to 0. Thus the first zero before the centre extinguishes the carry, every inserted zero outputs zero, and the high-side digits and final overflow are identical. If n grows by 1, the new central split shifts by half a digit but remains in that zero corridor. The output retains a symmetric central-zero corridor with z' >= z-2. Inserting central zeros also preserves the truth value of palindromicity, since all inserted digits are central zeros and every original opposing pair remains unchanged.

Consequently, if the observed unpadded trajectory has z_t>=4 for every t<=H, then for **every** k>=0 the trajectory from Pad_k(seed) is exactly Pad_k of that observed trajectory at every t<=H, with unchanged palindrome status and z_t increased by k. Since the independent traces establish z_t>=33 / >=46 through H=20000, they establish infinite *families of distinct seeds with 20,000-step finite-horizon avoidance*, not infinite-time Lychrel orbits. This proves a central-vacuum exact simulation primitive potentially usable for a moving-boundary certificate format, but provides no invariant after the two interfaces eventually collide.
