# Exact six-cell physical-carry phase covariance classification

**PAPER PROOF.** Base 10, rec2 p=8/m=48, overflow 10101010. For each phase t consider all 2^18 square-free GF(2) monomials in the six coefficient parities, six ascending carries and six descending carries of a cyclic physical pairword. The eight-phase covariance equations have rank **170,496** and kernel **1,926,656** out of 2,097,152 coefficients. Every solution is phasewise pointwise-zero, endpoint-gradient, or length-parity; this does NOT exclude any finite centre or establish a decimal Lychrel number.

## Exact graph and upper bound

A physical column state is (P,a,d), 0<=P<=18, a,d in{0,1}. Its successor (Q,A,D) is legal iff A=floor((P+a)/10) and d=floor((Q+D)/10). Exhaustive six-column walks in this 76-state/1444-edge graph number **188,183,524**, yielding exactly **27,296** admissible 18-bit six-cell masks. Their five-cell prefix/suffix endpoints form a connected graph with **5,984** vertices and six self-loops. An independent checker derives the same graph by gluing physical four- and five-cell masks rather than enumerating six-column walks.

For any physical cyclic 48-word, the parity histogram of six-cell masks has zero boundary incidence and even total edge parity. The graph cycle space has dimension E-V+1=21,313. Its constant-one edge functional is independent of gradients (a self-loop has odd length). Thus the histogram rank is **at most E-V=21,312**. The dimension of universal cyclic-zero Boolean functions is at least 2^18-(E-V)=**240,832**: 234,848 functions supported on inadmissible masks, 5,983 vertex gradients, and one length-parity function.

## Matching independent lower bounds

The primary engine constructs exact physical cyclic 48-words from every admissible six-mask. Each target has an actual 6-column carry-state path, extended by forced 0/18 boundary cells and freely varied remaining coefficients. It uses SplitMix64 seed `0x6a09e667f3bcc909` with 16 deterministic reservoir witnesses per mask and 16 fixed backgrounds. Ordinary cyclic bulk updates are

    g=1: Y_j=P_j+P_(j-1)+a_j+d_(j-1)-10(a_(j+1)+d_(j-2)).

The g0 image reaches rank **21,312** in the fixed-background pass; g1 reaches **21,247**. Six further independently seeded targeted passes yield g1 ranks **21,300, 21,308, 21,310, 21,310, 21,310, 21,311**. A final explicit 48-coefficient physical source

has a g1 image with the rare admissible six-cell mask **135103** (six odd parities, ascending carries 0,1,1,1,1,1 and descending carries 0,0,0,0,0,1) occurring **once**. This image adds the missing pivot and raises g1 rank to **21,312**, matching the upper bound. It specifically refutes a tempting but false one-bit g1 parity obstruction suggested by random-only rank 21,311. All witnesses are ordinary physical cycles, not formal independent-carry assignments.

The primary writes only independent pivot witnesses to two binary files: one 48-byte coefficient word for a single-cycle row, or two 48-byte words for the XOR of two valid cycle rows. Both files are generated by the committed primary source, each <5 MB. The standalone independent checker does not use the primary's cyclic update equations: it builds canonical 386-digit ordinary decimal integers with root coefficient 1 (g0) or 18 (g1), normalizes digit by digit, extracts the 48 interior output coefficients, independently normalizes four repeats to recover physical output carries, and verifies every witness row with a **lowest-bit** GF(2) elimination (the primary uses highest-bit pivots). It also independently reconstructs the graph by gluing shorter masks and checks eight genuine 406-digit rec2 phase transitions, exact overflows, periodic interior returns and deliberately wrong overflow shifts. The binary witness files are generated artifacts, not needed in the repository; all code and compact input data are committed.

The Boolean zeta transform from six-cell mask indicator functions to all square-free monomials is invertible over GF(2), so the exact histogram rank is the exact monomial-feature rank. At g0 phases, covariance multiplier 2 vanishes modulo 2 and forces the successor phase coefficient into the local-zero subspace by full g0 image rank. At g1 phases, multiplier 11=1, and the already local-zero source coefficient forces the successor phase coefficient into that same subspace by full g1 image rank. Thus all eight phase coefficients are independent local-zero functions: kernel 8×240,832=**1,926,656**, rank 8×21,312=**170,496**.
