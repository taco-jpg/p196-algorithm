# W07: deterministic backward pairword decoding and exponential one-step fibres

**PAPER PROOF (all bases b>=2): fixed-width pairword uniqueness.** Let P and Q be length-n palindrome words of coefficients in [0,2b-2] with equal base-b numerical value. Suppose D=P-Q !=0 and let j be its first nonzero position. The value equation shows b divides D_j; since |D_j|<=2b-2, |D_j|=b. Palindromicity makes j's reflected position n-1-j the last nonzero position with the same signed coefficient. If j=n-1-j the value equation immediately contradicts nonzero D_j. Otherwise, after factoring b^j, let m=n-1-2j>=1. The highest term has magnitude b^(m+1), while all lower terms together have magnitude at most 2(b-1)(b^m-1)/(b-1)=2(b^m-1). Since b^(m+1)>2(b^m-1) for b>=2, the value cannot be zero. Thus P=Q.

**PAPER PROOF: exact inverse decoder.** For a target digit word y (least significant first), choose a possible source width n: n=|y| or n=|y|-1. In the latter case target's leading digit must be the final overflow 1. For symmetric source sites i,j=n-1-i with shared pair coefficient s, low incoming carry l and high outgoing carry h, enumerate low outgoing l' and high incoming h' in {0,1} satisfying
 s=y_i+b l'-l=y_j+b h-h', 0<=s<=2b-2.
The incoming low carry starts 0 and the outgoing high carry starts at the specified overflow. At the centre accept iff low carry equals high carry for even n, or iff s_mid=y_mid+b h-l is even and in [0,2b-2] for odd n. Require s_0>=1 so a leading source digit exists. At most four boundary carry states occur per layer, giving an O(n) dynamic program and exact all-length rejection or unique pairword reconstruction. It does NOT choose an arbitrary predecessor integer: those require digit splits.

**PAPER PROOF: full ordinary predecessor fibre.** Each noncentral coefficient s has independent digit splits (a_i,a_j) with a_i+a_j=s, 0<=a_i,a_j<b. For the outermost pair require the leading a_j>=1, giving exactly the factor computed by fibre_count; if n odd, its centre coefficient must be even and gives a unique digit. Every independent combination yields exactly the same successor, since integer reverse-add is weighted normalization of its pairword. Conversely every ordinary predecessor yields such a split, so the count is exact. The target's width can allow two possible n, but the pairword for each n remains unique.
