# X27 second positive construction: seven coalescent eight-digit parity beacons

**Label: COMPUTED (100,000 ordinary decimal iterations); a finite one-step coalescence identity is a PAPER PROOF ingredient. No infinite Lychrel certificate.**

The seven integers `a 999999 b`, for `a=1,...,7` and `b=7-a`, are canonical eight-digit decimals. Their full least-significant-first symmetric pair coefficient word is `(7,18,18,18,18,18,18,7)`. For every base-10 integer `N`, `N+rev(N) = sum_i (d_i+d_(n-1-i)) 10^i`, with leading zeros in the formal reverse harmless. Therefore all seven seeds have exactly one common successor `89999987`. Each initial seed has at least one odd pair coefficient (in fact both outside pairs equal seven), ruling out a palindrome at `t=0`. From `t=1` onward their orbits are literally identical, so one long verification suffices for all seven.

With `N_0=19999996` (the `Q=3` instance of X27's `1 9^(2Q) 6` family), `verify.cpp` computes each of the first **20,000** reverse-and-add transitions in *two different forms*: literal grade-school decimal digit arrays, and GMP arbitrary-precision integers whose reversal is performed on a separately regenerated base-10 string. State equality is checked at every `t=0..20000`, not merely at checkpoints. It also checks every normalized pair parity against the exact carry-cocycle equations in `reports/AgentX27ParityBeacon.md`, and every resulting 32-site pairword prefix against the independently implemented high/low carry-prefix formula (when the number is shorter than 64 digits, the current prefix is shortened to `floor(n/2)`).

On every one of the 20,001 independently checked states, the mirrored pairword has an odd coefficient in positions `0..20`. Therefore no palindrome can occur. The `t=20000` integer has 8,272 decimal digits and residue 997364508 modulo 1,000,000,007. There were 8,264 decimal carry-overflows during the 20,000 steps, 10,038 odd-length states and 9,963 even-length states, and the parity-trace checksum was `16db298943fc4b8e`.

The separate `longrun.cpp` literal-digit computation reaches `t=100000` with 41,354 digits and 41,346 overflow steps. All 100,001 states have an odd mirrored pair coefficient in the first 32 positions, with maximum earliest-odd index **20**. Exactly 17 states have earliest odd index >=16, first at time 1,731. None has all-even mirrored coefficients; hence no palindrome. The 100,000-step extension has **one implementation**, not the two-engine cross-check used through 20,000.

`coalescence.py` independently verifies the seven seeds' full pairwords and common integer successor, and checks the first loss of the odd-pair witness at `t=1,2,10` for `Q=0,1,2`. Thus `Q=3` is the first member of this tightly defined one-parameter family that meets the `F_32` property through 20,000 steps. This observation does not exclude other smaller seeds or other certificate formats.

**Key limitation:** A periodic finite centre has not been found; the parity witness's bounded distance over 100,000 iterations is a computed observation, not an inductive invariant. An honest infinite decimal certificate would require an exact closed two-sided carry state family or a stronger structural renewal argument. The unrestricted-centre prefix graph in the first result did not close under its two adversarial carry choices; incorporating a *feasible* centre-to-boundary carry process remains the target.

Reproduce: `bash calculation/new_structures/agent-X27/short_reproduce.sh`, requiring GMP C++17 and Python 3. The source explicitly fails if either engine disagrees, if any prefix parity witness is missing, if any carry identity fails, or if the logged final summaries deviate.
