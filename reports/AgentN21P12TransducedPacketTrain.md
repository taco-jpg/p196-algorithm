## Candidate finite-state certificate format: synchronized packet firewalls

The following **PAPER PROOF** supplies a sound sufficient local composition rule, not a discovered decimal cycle. Work in any base b>=2. A symmetric coefficient word P has entries in [0,2b-2], and S_b(P)=pairs(norm_b(P,0)); norm outputs ordinary radix digits with a final overflow digit if present. Given nonoverlapping symmetric replacement intervals I_j together with mirror(I_j), define P^j to equal P except inside packet j, and P^J=P+SUM_{j in J}(P^j-P). Suppose for every j the radix carry normalization of P^j has EXACTLY the reference carry at the entrance and exit of each of the two replacement intervals, and no replacement interval overlaps another (even under reflection). These conditions are finite local carry-table checks. Then normalization of P^J has exactly the reference digits outside all intervals and exactly the P^j digits inside interval j. Proof: scan low to high. Up to the first interval all inputs and carries equal the reference; the packet enters with reference carry; it leaves with reference carry by hypothesis; induction over the ordered intervals completes the scan. All words have the same overflow, the canonical digit strings superpose componentwise, and pairing the output digits is linear:

    S_b(P^J) = S_b(P) + SUM_{j in J} (S_b(P^j) - S_b(P)).

For an **infinite pumpable packet-train certificate**, specify a finite cycle of reference/bulk states, finite-state packet words (or finite transducer rules), symbolic periodic gap lengths, and a proof for every edge and ALL admissible gap counts that (i) the carry-firewall conditions hold, (ii) the next packet supports are prescribed disjoint intervals with valid coefficients, (iii) overflow and symbolic gap updates are exact, (iv) canonical digits are nonpalindromic, and (v) the gap vector is bounded below throughout each cycle and nondecreasing after a full cycle. Each edge follows from the firewall lemma and finite state-to-state matches. Induction over cycles proves palindrome avoidance at every future orbit step. Pumping neutral/absorbing periodic gaps can be checked with their two-state carry maps and exact word concatenation, rather than extrapolated from numerical replays. This is a sufficient FORMAT, not a complete implemented arbitrary-gap checker and not a Lean theorem. A Lean development would first prove norm_append, the carry-firewall lemma by list induction, linearity of reflected digit pairing, and the cycle induction; none is claimed kernel-checked here.

The concrete 820-step witness reveals the obstacle: packet widths grow as their fronts propagate; the spacing stays 504. The supports overlap at 816, and a nonlinear collision destroys superposition at 820. A prospective infinite construction must reset/absorb packet wakes or increase interpacket gap, rather than merely widen a fixed centre.
