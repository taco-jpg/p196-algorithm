## 2. Findings

### 2.1 N08 decimal pilot (WRONG)
`decimal_replay.cpp` performs the reverse-and-add in place:
`for(i<n){ z=d[i]+d[n-1-i]+carry; d[i]=z%10; carry=z/10; }`

I confirmed the true statistics with three independent engines (N07/N09 outputs, my `rra`).
