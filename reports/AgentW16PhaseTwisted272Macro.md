## Why a phase cocycle is needed

On this orbit the overflow clock is 1,0,1,0,..., so over T=272h steps the pairword length increases exactly 136h. A symmetric insertion of h W copies and h V copies adds 2h(20+48)=136h coefficients. However insertion of h W blocks moves the start of the V domain by 20h physical columns. To represent the same unchanged V-phase values at those columns, the V template must be rotated by `phi_h=20h mod 48`. This follows directly from the identity `V[(j+20h) mod 48]` for the old V domain evaluated at a position moved right by 20h. Because V has primitive period 48, h=12 is the first positive h with zero net phase, and phi runs through twelve distinct values. This is an index-arithmetic **PAPER PROOF of the target-phase rule**, not a proof that actual orbit equals the target.

Define `Target(h)` as the symmetric pair-coefficient word with lower half `B W^(440+h) [9] rotate(V,20h mod 48)^(530+h)` and centre [9]. At t=272h, direct digit-list reverse-and-add computes P_t, then compares the entire pairword, coefficient by coefficient, to Target(h). The code also computes the naive target, which rotates V by zero. At h=1, the naive mismatch is 50,053 out of 68,697 coefficients; the phase-aware mismatch is only 627. An exhaustive check of all 20*48 bulk-rotation choices at h=1 independently finds (0,20) optimal.

For h=1..12, the phase-aware mismatch counts are (627,1247,1919,2571,3195,3823,4455,5131,5751,6367,6977,7629). The corresponding naive counts, the first mismatch indices, left joint-region counts, and cluster counts are in `phase_observations.txt` and are deterministically regenerated by `twisted_macro.cpp`. All twelve have exactly three mismatch clusters when consecutive differing indices with gaps at most five are connected: a left joint cone, its reflected right joint cone, and a single central cell. No other coefficient positions differ in this comparison. At h=1 the first discrepancy occurs at index 8778 and at h=12 it occurs at 8098, a net retreat of 680 across 2,992 operations, consistent with the earlier p8 glider speed 5/22. This is a finite observation, not an all-time velocity law.

**Independent replay:** `twisted_macro_gmp.cpp` starts from the canonical decimal integer and, in each of 3,264 steps, reverses its ordinary base-10 string and performs `mpz_class` addition. At every t=272h it independently constructs the pairword of the pre-addition integer (the one-step predecessor) and checks both exact mismatch counts. It also checks that the post-addition integer is nonpalindromic. All 12 snapshots pass. Previous W16 independent GMP replay covers the same orbit through 20,000 steps.

The residual grows by roughly 620 discrepancies per macrocycle and is 7,629 even at h=12 when the phase fully closes. Thus simply carrying a finite mod-12 phase state is insufficient. The constructive redirection is to find a local seam rule that absorbs or re-emits these two defect cones after 272 or 1,152 steps, rather than trying an unrotated fixed-centre equation. The exact data and checker provide a target for such a rule.
