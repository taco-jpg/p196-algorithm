## Construction
For q a positive integer, set N_0(q)=88*10^(2q)+3*10^q+75, whose decimal digits are `88 0^(q-1) 3 0^(q-2) 75`. Represent N_t(q)=C_t*10^s0 + M_t*10^(q+s1) + A_t*10^(2q+s2), with positive integer packets and integer offsets; initially (C,M,A)=(75,3,88), (s0,s1,s2)=(0,0,0). The two gaps can vary independently. This is a genuine three-zone, two-gap family, not N29's two-end-packet single-gap trajectory.

## Exact update and soundness
Suppose q>=max_j(s_j+digits(p_j)-s_(j+1)), j=0,1, so the three packets are disjoint; then the full integer word is `A 0^g1 M 0^g0 C`, with g1=q+s2-s1-digits(M), g0=q+s1-s0-digits(C). Let E=s2+digits(A). Reversal maps packet (p_j,s_j) to (rev(p_j), E-s_j-digits(p_j)) in exponent class (2-j)q. For each class k=0,1,2, take m_k=min(s_k,E-s_(2-k)-digits(p_(2-k))) and set p'_k=p_k*10^(s_k-m_k)+rev(p_(2-k))*10^(E-s_(2-k)-digits(p_(2-k))-m_k), s'_k=m_k. This exactly computes N+reverse(N) for every q with separated packets; no finite sample inference is used in the formula. Recompute separation for the next state before inducting.

The raw symmetric pairword at each state is the carry-free coefficient sum of the original three packet zones and their reversed opposites, each coefficient in [0,18]. Its three finite zones remain disjoint if q exceeds the computed `pairsep` threshold. The independent full-digit replay constructs the entire number, checks every predicted word, and tests each palindrome, not merely its endpoints.

## Uniform nonpalindrome lemma
Write X=10^q. The exact difference N_t(q)-reverse(N_t(q)) is 10^m D_t(X), with D_t an integer polynomial of degree at most 2 and m an integer shift. Detect the highest nonzero coefficient h exactly by comparing the two shifted packet strings in each exponent class. If h=0 there is no positive root. Otherwise choose M=max over the <=4 lower packet terms of (digits(packet)+shift-m). Every term has absolute value <10^M; thus the lower coefficient sum is <4*10^M and D_t(X) !=0 whenever q>=M+1. This is a conservative, integer-only root bound, checked at every t. The maximum over t=0..20000 of separation, pair-zone, and nonpalindrome sufficient bounds is respectively 12009, 12009, and **12031**. Therefore **for every integer q>=12031**, states 0..20000 are nonpalindromic and have the stated separated three-packet and pair-zone form. The claim stops at t=20000.

## Limitation and next search
The method maintains two separate zero reservoirs for the checked horizon, but **both shrink** (the final smaller one is 491 for q=12500). No renewal macrocycle or parameter gain has been shown. To close a true infinite certificate, search for a periodic transformation of the triplet `(C,M,A,s0,s1,s2)` into a translated or rotated copy with positive net drift of both gaps, possibly by coupling the centre packet to an absorbing lattice or a geometric repetition. The present finite recurrence gives an exact algebraic edge solver for that search.
