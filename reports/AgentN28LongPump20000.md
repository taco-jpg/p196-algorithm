Label: COMPUTED. This is a constructive 20,000-step candidate-family observation, **not a proof of an infinite decimal Lychrel orbit**. The full finite arithmetic and pairword checks were completed; no Lean theorem or periodic cycle was found.

Let P(k1,k2,k3) be the symmetric least-significant-first coefficient word L ++ 2^k1 ++ (6,12)^k2 ++ 18^k3 ++ (8) ++ reverse(L ++ 2^k1 ++ (6,12)^k2 ++ 18^k3), where L=(18,7,18,12). Split each paired coefficient x into lower digit floor(x/2) and upper digit ceil(x/2), and centre 8 into digit 4. The resulting decimal integer N(k1,k2,k3) is legitimate and initially nonpalindromic (the outer x=7 contributes unequal digits 3 and 4). No 196-related seed is implied.

At k1=k2=k3=25,000, the initial integer has 200,009 digits. Pumping cuts are 12504, 50004, 87504 in the left coefficient half, and each corresponding mirrored cut in the high half. For 20,000 consecutive steps, the checker establishes all of the following for every step:
1. Ordinary digit-vector addition of N and reversed N agrees **exactly** with independently normalized symmetric pair coefficients; the entire resulting digit vector is compared, not sampled.
2. Every pumped slot still contains its current periodic word, and the reflected high slot contains its reverse. For an overflowing (gamma=1) step, the one-cell shifted high insertion has the same input word as insertion at the mirrored slot, using the exact local period identity.
3. Both entering carries are fixed by the corresponding inserted period. The inserted output pairword period is calculated from the low and high emitted digit blocks. Hence, by the paired-pump soundness lemma in reports/AgentN28PairedInsertion.md, the entire input family with independently larger k1,k2,k3 has the same specified pairword structure under that step.
4. A digit mismatch at some index strictly before the first slot witnesses nonpalindromicity of the next number, for all independent repeat counts. The initial number is also nonpalindromic.

**Finite uniform conclusion:** for each (k1,k2,k3) with every kj>=25,000, the first 20,000 reverse-and-add transitions are nonpalindromic and have a three-slot pumping template at each step. This conclusion is conditional only on the finite arithmetic trace and the elementary paper lemma, NOT on a conjectured infinite cycle. The baseline trace uses 20,000 checks; the words of the three pumped bands change through time, and the finite baseline literals are recalculated for each step. A size-independent repeating centre/renewal law remains missing.
