# Agent N15: period-doubling folded-carry substitution edges in base 10

## Exact positive result (PAPER PROOF, no decimal Lychrel proof)

All pairwords are least-significant coefficient first; an input pairword has coefficients in 0..18 and is symmetric. Let `sigma(A)=AB`, `sigma(B)=BA` be a **uniform 2-substitution**, with numerical coding `A=14, B=16`. Put `w_h=sigma^h(A)` and `H_h=[4]++w_h`, `P_h=H_h++reverse(H_h)`, for every integer `h>=2`. The fundamental substitution scale doubles at each h; this is not a statement that a single RAA step doubles the integer's length. For any positive decimal N whose symmetric digit-pair sums equal P_h, define T(N)=N+rev(N). This construction has EXACT identities:

1. Let `m=2^h+1` and `q=[9,9]++[2*(x-9) for x in w_h[1:]]` (length m). The full symmetric pairword of T(N) is exactly `P1=q++reverse(q)`; there is no digit-length overflow. The digits of T(N) begin with 4 and end with 5, so T(N) is not a palindrome.
2. Let `r=[10,9,q[2]-10] ++ [q[i]+q[i-1]-18 for i=3..m-1] ++ [2*(q[m-1]-9)]`. The full symmetric pairword of T^2(N) is exactly `P2=r++reverse(r[:-1])`, of length `2*m+1`. This edge has overflow 1, and the digits of T^2(N) begin with 9 and end with 1: T^2(N) is not a palindrome.

Proof of 1: every internal coefficient of P_h is 14 or 16, and therefore emits carry 1 regardless of incoming carry in {0,1}. At coefficient 4 the final output carry is 0, with top digit 5 and bottom digit 4. The low and mirrored-high normalized digits at the first cell yield 9, at the first 14 cell yield `14-10+14-9=9`, and at every later cell with input x yield `2*(x-9)`. This proves the full identity at any h without extrapolation.

Proof of 2: all entries of q except q0=q1=9 are 10 or 14, so the highward carry is 1 through the entire mirrored upper half. The two first lowward carries are 0; all later lowward carries are 1. The final 9 receives carry 1 and overflows. Pairing normalized digits against the one new leading digit gives r0=10, r1=9, r2=q2-10, r_i=q_i+q_{i-1}-18 for 3<=i<m, and a doubled central digit r_m. This is again independent of h. The leading and trailing digits of T^2(N) are 1 and 9 respectively. The canonical N0 used in numeric experiments has end digits 3 and 1, so N0 itself is not a palindrome.

## New finite, sound edge-checking FORMAT

For an **even-length symmetric source pairword** `P=H++reverse(H)` of lower half `H=(p_0,...,p_{m-1})`, normalize in base b to digits D. Define the low carry entering cell i as `c_i`; define high carry `f_i` as the carry **leaving** mirrored high cell i toward the outer end. Both are in {0,1}. The outer state is `(c_0,f_0)=(0,gamma)` with gamma in {0,1}. At cell i, guess `f_{i+1}` and set `u_i=(p_i+c_i)%b`, `v_i=(p_i+f_{i+1})%b`, while imposing `c_{i+1}=(p_i+c_i)//b`, `f_i=(p_i+f_{i+1})//b`. At the centre require `c_m=f_m`. Exactly these finite carry paths correspond to genuine normalization (the constraint determines every digit and checks its carry boundary).

* **gamma=0 (even -> even):** lower target pair `q_i=u_i+v_i`. This yields a 4-state Boolean transition relation `M_(p,q)` on boundary states `(c,f)`. Products of the 4x4 Boolean matrices accept exactly valid whole-word edges from `(0,0)` to a diagonal centre state.
* **gamma=1 (even -> odd):** lower target has length m+1: `q0=u0+1`, `q_i=u_i+v_(i-1)` for 1<=i<m, `q_m=2*v_(m-1)`. Store the one previously produced high digit (0..b-1) in the state. This yields a finite delayed 4b-state relation with an initial and terminal guard. `folded_relations.py` independently checks both cases; 2,027 accepting controls and 27 mutated rejections were tested. Odd-source variants use the central source coefficient as a one-cell terminal transition; no odd-source implementation is claimed here.

For source/target morphic edge schemas whose aligned `(p,q)` letter stream is generated by a finite 2-uniform grammar, assign each grammar symbol its boundary relation matrix. Concatenation is Boolean matrix multiplication. For a grammar `A->AB, B->BA`, let `X_h,Y_h` be the images of the two level-h words, and `T_h` the image of `sigma^h(A)` **with its initial A omitted**. Then `X_(h+1)=X_h Y_h`, `Y_(h+1)=Y_h X_h`, `T_(h+1)=T_h Y_h`. This triple lives in a finite Boolean-matrix monoid, so exhaustively following its deterministic update until a repeated triple is a **finite, sound test of the edge for every level**, not just tested lengths. Here the `(p,q)` edge is first two paired letters `(4,9),(14,9)` followed by `(14,10)/(16,14)`; the matrix triple repeats after h=1, and the initial-to-centre acceptance succeeds at all levels. No oracle, unbounded depth, or guessed carry is trusted without its terminal check.

## Independent computed evidence, bounds and exact failure mode

`dyadic.py` checks both exact identities for every h=1..14 (second for h>=2), and records finite complexity counts. `monoid.py` checks the all-h first-edge monoid: 2 reachable triples, preperiod 1, period 1, every reachable matrix accepts. `folded_relations.py` checks independent, explicit finite carry paths on the same families and 2,000 random symmetric inputs; 27 intentional corrupted outputs are rejected. Ordinary C++ digit-list reverse-add is checked **at every time step** against independently normalized pairwords for t=0..20,000 and for h=5,6,7,8,9,10,11,12; the seeded h9 test has initial length 1026, final length 9362, and 8336 overflows, with no palindrome among 20,001 integer states. Python bigint using `int(reverse(str(n)))` independently checks h9 through 1000 steps, ending at 1455 decimal digits. These finite runs do NOT validate a stable dyadic formula beyond the first two exact edges.

The obstruction to the simple two-letter morphic *time cycle* is visible immediately. At level h=11, restricting to positions 50 through the initial half-length minus 50, the smallest observed radius r such that each coefficient at time t is a function of its original `(2r+1)`-symbol neighbourhood is: t=1:0; 2:1; 3:2; 4:3; 8:4; 16:8; 20:11; 32:20. These are exact finite-window counts from `radius.py`, **not** all-length lower bounds or a wall-speed theorem. The word remains morphically generated for fixed finite t by a larger sliding-block transducer, but the required memory grows with t. A genuine renormalisation must compress that growing block-context alphabet, or arrange exact cancellation at dyadic times; we have not obtained this.
