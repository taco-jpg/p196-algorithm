Start in base 10 from the symmetric pairword `P0_i=17-3*u(min(i,n-1-i))`, `u(x)=popcount(x) mod 2`, `n=2k+8`, with integer seed reconstructed by splitting each symmetric coefficient into valid digits. Extending the *genuine* digit-level dynamics to t=9 produces a surprising time-dependent regeneration: many coefficient-9 sites appear in phases t=6,7,8, but the t=9 configuration collapses back to two boundary ports, with at most one extra symmetric *central* pair in the tested range.

**Exact observed rule for k=2..3000, all integers in this range (not a proof beyond it):** the first nine overflow bits are `101010101`, so the t=9 pairword length is `n9=2k+13`. Let `s(k)=u(k-2)u(k-1)u(k)u(k+1)u(k+2)u(k+3)` (a six-character bit word). Then the exact set of indices with `P9_i=9` equals `{2, n9-3}` except when `s(k)` is either `101100` or `001101`, in which case it is `{2, n9-3, k+5, k+7}`. The two extra cells straddle the centre index k+6. Among k=2..3000, exactly 250 indices trigger the extra pair. For k=0 the only ports are 2 and 10; k=1 is exceptional with ports 2,4,6,8,10,12. The stated rule passed all 3001 widths k=0..3000 and 40 pseudorandom widths in 3001..20000 (PRNG seed 160016), but those checks cannot establish it for arbitrary k. The first nine overflows matched in every test. There were zero computed mismatches.

**Independent arithmetic:** every one of the nine steps at every tested width was checked both by pair-coefficient normalization with carries and by direct digit-list reverse-add. For k in {0,1,2,7,21,25,63,127,255}, nine edges per seed were additionally checked via Python's ordinary big integers and decimal reversal. This tests the engine, not the universal conjecture.

The centre guard `s(k) not in {101100,001101}` is itself binary-automatic: the six adjacent Thue-Morse bits are produced by a small DFAO with index adders. The observed nine-step return therefore suggests an **indexed, regular guard on centre widths** that preserves two boundary neutral ports even though the intervening phases have O(k) neutral coefficients. This is neither a bounded centre nor an absorbing spatially periodic band. In the tested data for k=2^m-1 (m=2..12), there are exactly two t9 ports, despite large neutral storms at t6..8 (e.g. k=4095 gives counts 680,1370,2050 in those three phases before returning to two).
