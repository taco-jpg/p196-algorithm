# N01: a diagonal two-band pumping criterion and a decimal one-step witness

## Scope, labels and novelty
**PAPER PROOF (generic soundness criterion); COMPUTED (explicit decimal instance).**
This is a new *edge format*, not an infinite certificate: two distinct periodic spatial bands with repetitions synchronized by the same unbounded integer parameter. Existing symbolic engine gt.py assumes distinct independently large band variables and can reject length comparisons coupled between bands. The present format uses exact finite carry-transfer relations and has no unspecified large-gap threshold. It does not prove a base-10 Lychrel number or a certificate for 196.

## Exact finite-state transducer (paper proof)
Write a palindromic coefficient word P=H ++ C ++ reverse(H), with C empty or a single coefficient, all coefficients 0..2b-2, H[0]>0, base b>=2. Let N=val_b(P), D=digits_b(N), gamma=|D|-|P| in {0,1}. The next coefficient word is pairs(D), i.e. coefficients D_i+D_(|D|-1-i), and val_b(pairs(D))=N+reverse_b(N). Read H from the least-significant end inward. A state is (a,h,z,e): a in {0,1} carry entering the next low digit; h in {0,1} carry *out* from the corresponding upper digit toward the most-significant end; z in 0..b-1 is the upper digit one column previously visited; e is the accumulated nonpalindrome flag. Initially (0,gamma,gamma,0), where z=1 for gamma=1 is the overflow digit.

On an input coefficient x and a proposed output pair coefficient y, guess r in {0,1}, the carry entering the mirrored upper digit from the centre. Require floor((x+r)/b)=h. Set dl=(x+a) mod b, dh=(x+r) mod b, a'=floor((x+a)/b). Require y=dl+dh if gamma=0, or y=dl+z if gamma=1. The new state is (a',r,dh,e OR (dl!=dh)) for gamma=0, or (a',r,dh,e OR (dl!=z)) for gamma=1. All arithmetic is finite. At the centre: if C is empty require a=h; gamma=0 gives no extra output coefficient, gamma=1 gives one central coefficient 2z. If C=(x), require floor((x+a)/b)=h, let d=(x+a) mod b, and require the extra output coefficient 2d for gamma=0 or d+z for gamma=1; in the latter case extend the mismatch flag by (d!=z). Accept only if the flag is set when nonpalindromicity is requested. This scans the *entire* normalized digit word, because the guessed upper carries are forced by the central condition; it therefore accepts exactly the actual one-step pairword and correct palindrome test. The relation has 8b states, or 80 states in base 10.

## Unbounded two-band pumping (paper proof)
Suppose source and target lower-half words are matched in equal-length chunks:
X ++ A^t ++ J ++ B^t ++ Y versus X' ++ A'^t ++ J' ++ B'^t ++ Y', for every t>=0. Each paired chunk must have equal length; source/target centre conditions may differ by one coefficient under gamma=1. Construct Boolean 8b-by-8b transition matrices M_X, M_A, M_J, M_B, M_Y, using the transducer above, for each paired chunk. The exact relation for parameter t is their Boolean product M_X M_A^t M_J M_B^t M_Y. Since Boolean matrices form a finite semigroup, the sequence of pairs (M_A^t,M_B^t) is eventually periodic. Iterate this pair from t=0 until the first repeated pair, and test central acceptance and nonpalindromicity for EVERY distinct pair. Then the edge is exact for all t>=0 by induction on the repeated-power pair. This does not infer an unbounded result from a small unproved sample: the complete matrix-power period is explicitly checked.

A cycle of such certified edges, with consistent source/target templates, synchronized gap shifts, nondecreasing parameters after each cycle, valid centre/parity choices and nonpalindromicity on every edge, implies an infinite palindrome-free reverse-and-add orbit by induction. This is a sufficient *format theorem*, not an identified decimal cycle. A possible Lean formalization has bit-vector encoding of the finite state relation, matrix product soundness, finite semigroup powering, and induction over the edge cycle.

## Decimal two-period witness (computed, exact all repetition counts)
Use the base-10 period-8 exterior lattice phase-0 blocks W (length 20, rec0) and V (length 48, rec2), with B length 39 reconstructed from the rec0 depth-39 path in lattice_formats/p8_scc.txt. The exact numerical arrays are in reproduce.py. For every k>=2 let H_k=B ++ W^k ++ [9] ++ V^k, P_k=H_k ++ [9] ++ reverse(H_k), and N_k=val_10(P_k). Both the 20-block and 48-block are nonuniform and have different spatial periods.

Let W1 and V1 be the respective stationary-carry bulk images for gamma=1. Infer B1 (39 digits), J1 (49 digits), and C1=[12] by the explicit k=2 arithmetic in reproduce.py. Define H'_k=B1 ++ W1^k ++ J1 ++ V1^(k-1) ++ C1 and P'_k=H'_k ++ reverse(H'_k). Then for ALL integers k>=2,
    N_k + reverse_10(N_k) = val_10(P'_k),
and N_k is not a palindrome. In particular the two independent period-20/period-48 regions survive one exact decimal update despite a one-symbol initial junction; the image junction is a 49-symbol block. For k=2+t, paired chunks for the finite checker are [B W^2] versus [B1 W1^2], W versus W1 repeated t, [9 V^2] versus [J1 V1], and V versus V1 repeated t. The final source coefficient is 9 and the final target half-digit is 12. The TWO matrix powers jointly repeat after t=1 (preperiod 1, period 1). Thus central acceptance for t=0 and t=1 certifies every k>=2, not merely the six direct examples.

Ordinary Python-integer reverse-and-add comparisons separately passed k=2,3,4,8,15,40, with initial coefficient lengths 353,489,625,1169,2121,5521; 200 seeded random canonical-integer tests independently checked two-ended normalization, both length parities, both gamma values, palindrome flags and centre rules. The arrays here describe a symmetric coefficient word P_k; P_k need NOT itself be the pairword of an earlier integer. The number N_k=val_10(P_k) is canonicalized first and is an ordinary legitimate decimal starting number.

## What does not close, and new search direction
This is an exact **single edge only**, not a finite temporal cycle. The period-48 interior develops a growing defect when evolved further; it is not proved that the 49-symbol junction returns or remains bounded. A next constructive search should solve *finite transducer boundary-state cycles* with synchronized period-20/period-48 band powers rather than testing ever wider isolated centres. In particular, allow a migrating phase register at the junction and period-480 temporal returns: over 480 steps the alternating overflow stream supplies 240 new decimal positions, compatible with both spatial periods 20 and 48. No assertion of reachability, existence or palindrome avoidance beyond the certified edge follows from that observation.
