# Agent W11: four-corner bivariate packet polynomial and synchronized centre twins

**COMPUTED:** the explicit decimal initial family `88 0^(q2+delta) 3 0^q2 75` avoids palindromes for all iterates t=0..20000 and has a separated four-corner pairword at each state, for **all integers q2>=11986 and delta>=11986**. **PAPER PROOF:** the exact polynomial recurrence and sufficient nonpalindrome bound below. No infinite Lychrel certificate and no Lean formalization.

## Genuinely bivariate format
Set q1=q2+delta. Represent an integer as `p00*10^s00 + p01*10^(q2+s01) + p10*10^(q1+s10) + p11*10^(q1+q2+s11)`, with nonnegative coefficient packets and signed offsets. These four corners are monomials 1, V, U, UV in (U,V)=(10^q1,10^q2). Initially `(p00,p01,p10,p11)=(75,3,0,88)` and `(s00,s01,s10,s11)=(0,2,0,3)`. A mirrored middle packet appears at t=1. This format has *two independent* gap parameters; N29's single-q geometric-word representation need not order all four corners simultaneously when the two gaps vary.

## Exact edge rule and pairword soundness
If the packet supports do not overlap, the total length is q1+q2+E, with E=s11+digits(p11). Under whole-number reversal a packet in corner j is reflected to corner 3-j with coefficient `rev(p_j)` and offset `E-s_j-digits(p_j)`. For each corner k, merge its old contribution with its reflected opposite by taking the smaller offset and summing the shifted integer packets. This is an exact algebraic identity, not an extrapolation from finite words. Recheck three adjacent separations after every step: the outer gaps are controlled by q2; the middle gap by delta. The raw digit-pair word is a carry-free sum of the original supports and their reflected supports (coefficients 0..18); its four zones are disjoint when the analogous reflected-zone bounds are met.

## Universal nonpalindrome root bound
Let E_k be the exponent coordinates (0,q2,q2+delta,2q2+delta). The difference between an integer and its full reversal is `10^m * sum_{k=0}^3 d_k*10^E_k` with exact integer coefficients d_k after pulling out the minimal shift m. If h is the highest k with d_k nonzero, h=0 forbids a zero; otherwise each lower integer term has magnitude below 10^M, where M is the largest packet digit count plus its shifted offset minus m. There are at most six lower signed terms. Thus q2,delta>=M+1 imply `10^(E_h-E_(h-1)) >= 10^(M+1) > 6*10^M`, and the leading term strictly dominates: the number cannot be palindromic. `nonpal_bound` checks h exactly through shifted-string equality and returns this conservative sufficient threshold.

Across all t=0..20000 the maximum packet-separation bounds are (q2,delta)=(11969,11953), the pairword-zone bounds are (11969,11953), and the common sufficient nonpalindrome bound is 11986. Therefore q2,delta>=11986 give the full finite theorem. For q2=delta=14000 (q1=28000), independent full digitwise arithmetic matches the four-corner polynomial state at every one of 20001 states and no state is palindromic. Independently, a completely separate GMP-only ordinary integer program checks all 20000 additions; the two paths agree on the full-number and pairword hashes at all 21 thousand-step checkpoints and exact complete integers at t=10000 and t=20000. The endpoint has 50259 digits.

## Middle-twin invariant
At t=1, the initial single middle packet p01=3 is copied by reversal to the previously empty corner p10, with identical offset s01=s10=2. Whenever p01=p10 and s01=s10, the two corner merge formulas are the same, so this equality is preserved at every subsequent exact edge. At t=20000, both middle packets have 11953 digits and both offsets equal -1847. This is a synchronized, mirror-duplicated centre on two distinct exponent scales, not a one-band kink of bounded width.

## Open gap
These are exact **finite** edges, not a closing polynomial-cycle certificate. The twin centre packets grow from one digit to 11953 digits, consuming both large gaps. A genuine infinite result would require a cycle or regeneration rule on the four-corner packet states with a nondecreasing cone invariant; no such renewal has been found.
