## Evolution / soundness of the finite template checks
Given B,C, carry-normalize B from carry 0 to get DB and e1, normalize C from e1 to get DC and e2, and normalize reverse(B) from e2 to get DR and overflow gamma. If e1 != e2, the two neutral 9^g bands transmit different carry bits, so the normalized digit word is nonpalindromic wherever g>0. For gamma=0: new B=DB+reverse(DR), new C=DC+reverse(DC). For gamma=1: new B=(DB++[9(1-e1)])+([1]++reverse(DR)); new C=(DC++[9(1-e2)])+([9(1-e2)]++reverse(DC)). Pairwise additions are coefficient-wise. Remove trailing 9s from new B (u of them) and symmetric boundary 9s from new C (v pairs). Then g'=g+u+v-gamma. This is the previously known exact neutral-band rule, not a newly proved infinite cycle. The new result is the explicitly replayed 20,000-step *growing-centre* instance whose core is not bounded, with all full-word equalities checked.
