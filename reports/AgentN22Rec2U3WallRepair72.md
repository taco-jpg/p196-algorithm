# N22: rooted decimal rec2/W48 wall restores exactly after 72 steps using a charged U3 head

**Status: PAPER PROOF (two-ended carry-pumping lemma with a finite exhaustive edge check); independently COMPUTED in ordinary decimal big integers.** This is **not** a decimal Lychrel proof, a full-interface cycle, or a result about 196.

## Quantified result

Use the repository's physical period-eight, spatial-period-48 root `B_t` (11 entries) and `W_t` (48 entries) in `calculation/new_structures/agent-N22/{B.csv,rows.csv}`, least significant coefficient first. Write `U=(10,4,10)`. For arbitrary independent integers `q>=5,k>=34` and `d` in `{-5,4}` define

```
H(q,k,d) = B_0 ++ W_0^q ++ [3,4] ++ U^k ++ [10,4],
H[11+48q+2] += d,   H[11+48q+4] -= d,
P(q,k,d) = H ++ reverse(H[:-1]).
```

All coefficients of `P` are in 0..18 and its middle coefficient is 4. The *canonical positive decimal integer* `n(q,k,d)` is obtained by splitting each mirrored coefficient `p_i` into lower digit `p_i-min(9,p_i)` and upper digit `min(9,p_i)` and taking the middle digit `p_mid/2`; its leading digit is 9. Thus `n+rev(n)=val_10(P)` exactly.

**Theorem (72-step finite wall repair for an infinite two-parameter family).** For every such `(q,k,d)`, let `n_t` be the actual decimal reverse-and-add orbit of `n(q,k,d)`. For **all** `0<=t<=72`: (i) the symmetric pairword `P_t` starts with `B_(t mod 8)`; (ii) `n_t` is not palindromic (an odd coefficient occurs within those eleven positions); (iii) for `0<=t<72` the overflow is 1 at even `t`, 0 at odd `t`; and (iv) **at t=72 the next 48q pairword coefficients after B_0 are precisely W_0^q**. The maximal W48-matching frontier relative to `48q`, at eight-step checkpoints `t=0,8,...,72`, is exactly `[0,-1,0,-1,0,-1,+1,0,-1,0]`. Consequently the outer wall is restored in 72 steps despite transient defects, and the decimal integer gains exactly 36 digits. The two distinct heads `d=-5,+4` coalesce to the *same actual integer* after two reverse-and-add steps, for all q,k in the theorem.

This theorem provides a **recovering outer-wall transition**, not the missing full-interface renewal: the U3 inner reservoir shrinks, the finite junction does not return, and the 72-step transition cannot presently be iterated with both counters nondepleting. For example at `(q,k)=(10,80)`, the original U segment is 240 coefficients while the longest U-rotated run at t72 in a 130-cell seam scan is 212 coefficients.

## Finite-state proof principle and kernel

For a symmetric coefficient word P of length n, write `c_0=0`, `c_(i+1)=floor((P_i+c_i)/10)` and `a_i=P_i+c_i-10c_(i+1)`. If the last carry is 1, append a leading 1. Its successor pairword is `(a_i+a_(n'-1-i))_(i=0..n'-1)`. This is exact schoolbook arithmetic for `n+rev(n)` regardless of how the original pair digits were split.

**Two-ended insertion lemma.** Consider insertion of r identical periodic coefficient blocks `Z` at an interior lower-half cut and the r reversed blocks at the reflected cut. If (a) the source has at least one whole identical periodic block on each side of the insertion, (b) the inward low-carry and outward high-carry states both return to themselves through `Z` and `reverse Z`, and agree with the uninserted word's carry states at the two cuts, and (c) the one-block successor has the same symbolic insertion property at the next-phase cut, with the same terminal overflow, then the exact successor of **any number r>=0** of inserted blocks is obtained by inserting r copies of the successor block. Proof: split decimal normalization into three subwords around the two cuts; fixed-point carry maps imply by induction that inserting another copy leaves all outside digits and terminal carry unchanged. Inside the inserted copies, normalization is periodic, including at the reflected high-side insertion. Re-pairing output digits shifts only by the common overflow 0 or 1, so the one-block shift checked in (c) repeats r times. The adjacent-copy guard ensures the boundary pairings match. This is a finite arithmetic identity, not a finite-horizon extrapolation.

Apply the lemma to W-block insertion (width 48, fixed cut `11+2*48`) and U-block insertion (width 3, fixed cut `11+48q+2+3*floor(k/2)`). The four corners `(q,k),(q+1,k),(q,k+1),(q+1,k+1)` let the two insertions commute; note that the U cut moves by +48 under W insertion. The certificate checker explicitly verifies **all four source-to-corner insertion comparisons**, both carry fixed points per cut and their agreement with unpumped words, two adjacent identical blocks, the canonical full-word output, the same overflow, the root B and odd witness, at **every one of 73 states t=0..72** for the base `(q,k)=(5,34)` and both d. Each next state is generated by an independent full coefficient normalizer, not by a guessed phase table. Thus the two insertion lemmas induct simultaneously over all additional independent q and k and over 72 time steps. The terminal matching and front table at the base propagate through the same literal insertions, proving the claimed exact frontier for all q,k. No Lean kernel is claimed.
