**PAPER PROOF of the sound step rule:** For `1<=a<=9`, `|k|<=9999` and sufficiently large `n`, write the LSB-first digit string of `a(10^n-1)/9+k` as `A ++ u^L ++ H`, where `|A|=|H|=m` and `u=a`. Given any such string with `L>=2`, normalize `N+rev(N)` from its low end: the first `m` coefficients are `A[i]+H[m-1-i]`; the first bulk coefficient is `2u`; and the final `m` are `H[i]+A[m-1-i]`. Since `2u` is even, its carry-out from either input carry `0` or `1` is always `c*=floor(2u/10)`. After the first bulk coefficient the entire remaining bulk is the **single** digit `v=(2u+c*) mod 10`. The code absorbs the exceptional first bulk digit into the low frontier and, depending on the final carry, either absorbs the last bulk digit into the high frontier or appends a top `1`. It then strips matching `v` digits from both frontiers. This yields the exact new `A',v^{L'},H'` and `n'=n+gamma`; no inner digits have been enumerated. In particular the pairword has shape `B ++ (2u)^L ++ reverse(B)` with `B[i]=A[i]+H[m-1-i]`.
