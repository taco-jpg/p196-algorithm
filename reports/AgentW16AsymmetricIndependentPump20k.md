## Sound finite-horizon contract

For a symmetric coefficient word P, let D=normalize(P), gamma=|D|-|P|, Q=pairs(D). A paired insertion at a lower cut i and upper cut |P|-i inserts an m-word w and its reverse, respectively. Let r=|P|-i and h=|D|-i=r+gamma. It suffices to check: (1) the lower block P[i:i+m] equals w, and P[r:r+m]=reverse(w); (2) if gamma=1, P[r]=P[r+m], ensuring that insertion of the upper block at r has the same coefficient-word effect as inserting the shifted block P[h:h+m] at h; (3) decimal carries entering the lower block at i and shifted upper block at h return to the same carries after m places; (4) the emitted low and high m-digit blocks form v[j]=low[j]+high[m-1-j]; (5) the canonical digit D has a mirror mismatch at some e<i. Standard radix carry induction shows `pairs(normalize(insert_pair(P,w,i)))=insert_pair(Q,v,i)` and preserves terminal carry, and hence the same equality for any number of inserted copies. For two disjoint nested paired cuts, insertions commute after index adjustment. This is the known finite paired-insertion lemma, applied here to a new 20,000-edge two-clock skew witness.

We take iW=839 and iV=24200, both fixed **lower-half** indices of the reference P_t throughout the finite verification. At each t=0..19999, all criteria above pass for both bands; the computed next block v agrees independently with the period-20/period-48 cyclic bulk update. At each t=0..20000 a mirror mismatch is found strictly before iW, giving a nonpalindrome witness common to all independent pump increments. Therefore the 20,000 checked base edges lift, by induction in time and independent inserted-copy counts, to **every** initial count kw>=440 and kv>=530. The proof is finite-horizon only: the checked edges depend on t and are not periodic-closed.
