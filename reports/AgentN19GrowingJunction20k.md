# Agent N19: a 20,000-step decimal orbit with two persistent clocked bulk blocks and a growing counter-machine centre

## Result and scope
**COMPUTED:** The explicit decimal seed

    N(k) = decimal digits 9 8^k 59 9^k 80 7^k 20 0^k 1

with `k = 15000` has 60,008 digits and survives **exactly steps 0..20,000 (20,001 states)** without a palindrome, while every state has the *strict two-block folded pairword shape*

    H_t = L_t ++ x_(t mod 6) ^ a_t ++ J_t ++ y_(t mod 6) ^ b_t ++ R_t,

where `L_t,J_t,R_t` are unconstrained finite words and the run lengths `a_t,b_t >= 100`, in that order and disjoint. Here `H_t` is the least-significant-first half of the canonical palindromic pairword `P_i=d_i+d_(n-1-i)`. The observed bulk clock is

    t mod 6:     0    1    2    3    4    5
    x:           8   16   14   10    2    4
    y:          16   14   10    2    4    8

The `x` and `y` blocks are defined as **the longest maximal runs of those respective values** in the folded half, and their nonoverlapping order is checked separately on every step. This is a finite-horizon verified shape; the junction `J_t` is *not* a bounded string or a finite-state periodic centre. This gives a concrete reason a counter/stack is useful. There is NO decimal Lychrel certificate, no infinite avoidance claim, no claim for all k, and no Lean proof.

At t=0 the blocks have lengths 15,000 and 15,000 with junction length 2. At t=20,000 they have lengths 1,820 and 1,786 and junction length **13,183**. The decimal integer has **68,241** digits. Both blocks survived all 20,001 states and the independent replays agree on each state's full-pairword 64-bit FNV-1a checksum, total digit count, and all five block-location statistics. This is considerably stronger than comparing the endpoints alone, but per-step hashes alone are not a mathematical proof of bytewise equality or the infinite orbit.

## Reproduction, all source in this commit

    bash calculation/new_structures/agent-N19/run_bulk20k.sh

Requires C++17 g++, Python3, GMP and SHA256 utilities. The script compiles independently implemented `bulk20k_digits.cpp` (ordinary digit vector with explicit carry) and `bulk20k_gmp.cpp` (independent `mpz_class` integer arithmetic with independently reconstructed folded pairword). The GMP replay uses a checkpoint after step 10,000 to keep each invocation within practical runtime; the checkpoint is generated by GMP itself, never imported from the digit-vector engine. It checks all 20,001 states individually for palindromes and strict block shape, compares the two entire **20,001-line** trace files, verifies the SHA256 of that exact CSV (below), checks all the supplied independent checkpoints, and asserts final statistics.

The two engines implement different transitions: digit vector computes new digits by full coefficient normalization; GMP computes `mpz_class(s) + mpz_class(reverse(s))` using decimal conversion, so the equality of the *whole trajectory of row hashes and shape statistics* is an independent consistency check. The log's SHA256 digest of the complete trace CSV is

`0ac6f1d97ed2da30430bd1d8f393918691de847abcf4b678738b27187b297851`.

Compact checkpoints: `calculation/new_structures/agent-N19/bulk20k_checkpoints.csv`. No unneeded 1.1-MB traces are committed. An intentionally impossible minimum-block-length control is `./bulk20k_digits 15000 0 15001`, which exits 3 (as any initial block is exactly 15,000). A palindrome negative control can be inspected by the existing random/transducer checks in the first N19 result.

## Mechanism and obstruction

The six-step **universal for all k >= 5** two-block identity in the previous N19 result has `(k,k)->(k-5,k-2)` after six steps, with genuine positive propagation of the two ordered domains. In longer exact replays the central junction becomes *growing, structurally irregular memory*: the first exterior-front boundary moves inward in the folded half, while the second also moves inward more slowly. Consequently the junction expands. A construction seeking an **infinite** decimal orbit of this shape must add a replenishment or moving-emitter rule capable of preventing eventual depletion of the two runs; simply enlarging the initial seed only delays depletion. This finite-horizon result is **not** such a rule.

`k=2000` provides an independent shorter probe in `growth_trace.py` and `growth_gmp.cpp`: matching all 3,401 states through t=3400; the thresholded length-30 two-block shape holds through t=2940 and first falls below threshold at t=2946. See `run_bulk20k.sh` for the stronger 20,000-step instance.
