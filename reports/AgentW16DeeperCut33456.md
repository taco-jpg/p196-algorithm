## Seed and recurrence

Base 10; all coefficient words and digits are least-significant first. Explicit `B`, `W`, `V` are shared with the preceding W16 results in `calculation/new_structures/agent-W16/skew_contract.cpp`. Their lengths are respectively 39, 20, 48. For independently varying integers `kw>=440`, `kv>=530`, define `H=B ++ W^kw ++ [9] ++ V^kv`, `P_0=H ++ [9] ++ reverse(H)`, and the actual decimal starting integer by carry-normalizing `P_0` into `D_0`. Thereafter `P_(t+1)=pairs(D_t)`, `D_(t+1)=normalize(P_(t+1))`, which is exactly `D_t+reverse(D_t)` because the pairs are digitwise sums. In particular `P_t` is the coefficient input normalized to `D_t`; its successor is the actual pairword of `D_t`.

At each reference state `P_t`, put a 20-block cut at `iW=39+20*40=839` and a 48-block cut at `iV=39+20*kw+1+48*427`. The right cuts are at `n-iW`, `n-iV` in the palindromic coefficient input; if `gamma=1` normalization increases the length, so the right digit-output cut is at `n+1-i` instead. At every time `0<=t<33456` the checker verifies (i) the word at each low cut is exactly the bulk 20/48 word updated by the cyclic-carry transducer; (ii) its mirrored high block matches; (iii) both entering carry bits are loops through the corresponding low and shifted high blocks; (iv) the one-column `gamma` shift is compatible with the reflected insertion, explicitly `P_t[n-i]=P_t[n-i+m]` when gamma is 1; (v) the entire emitted pair-insertion word equals the independently computed bulk update. The checker also evaluates complete pumped inputs at checkpoint times and checks their exact normalized pairword, and checks the original reference integer against independent ordinary digit-array reverse-and-add at every step.

## Why this certifies all independent band counts, not only two examples

At a low/high cut, inserting a period which returns the entering carry to itself merely inserts a fixed emitted digit block, without altering subsequent normalization. Condition (iv) makes the palindromic coefficient insertion and the high digit insertion at the gamma-shifted cut identical, including the one-column overlap. Consequently forming reflected digit pairs inserts a period block in the next pairword. Apply this argument separately at the disjoint W/V cut pairs, and commute the two insertions. By induction on the two nonnegative insertion counts, the exact same checked edge works for every extra W period and every extra V period, independently. Induction on `t=0..33455` propagates the two-parameter family. Every normalized integer `D_t` has a mismatch index before the earliest cut `iW`, unaffected by either insertion, proving nonpalindromicity for `t=0..33456`.

Unlike an infinite template cycle, this is an exact **finite** chain with a growing unstructured central seam; no later-time safety is claimed. `gamma(t)=1` for even `t` and `0` for odd `t` throughout the checked interval. The reference `kw=440,kv=530` grows from 68,562 digits to 85,290 digits at 33,456 additions. The shifted control `kw=443,kv=537` repeats the full digit-array check and has the same clock. Independent GMP arbitrary-precision integer arithmetic (no pairword template) checks the baseline through the same number of additions and compares the final least-significant-first FNV-1a hash; the numeric control results are in `cut_observations.json`.
